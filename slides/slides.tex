\section{Problem}

% -------------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}{The gap}
\begin{columns}[T]
\column{0.55\textwidth}
\textbf{Energy transition models need plant-level data.}

\medskip
Capacity expansion models, grid simulations, emissions inventories
--- all require structured tables of power plants with fuel, status,
capacity, and location.

\medskip
In countries with weak statistical offices, these datasets are
compiled manually by expert teams. This is slow, expensive, and
doesn't scale.

\medskip
\textbf{Can AI produce research-quality datasets?}

\medskip
We pilot this question on one task --- Vietnamese thermal plants ---
to find out which \emph{methods} work, before scaling to more
countries and sectors.

\column{0.42\textwidth}
\textbf{Why it's a hard extraction task:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Multilingual} --- Vietnamese diacritics,
        multiple transliterations per plant
  \item \textbf{Temporal} --- plans change yearly,
        plants retire or get cancelled
  \item \textbf{Technical} --- MW\textsubscript{e} vs MW\textsubscript{th},
        plant vs unit vs turbine
  \item \textbf{Scattered} --- data lives across
        PDPs, EVN reports, government decrees
\end{itemize}

\medskip
{\small We use Vietnam thermal power (163~plants) as the pilot task.}
\end{columns}
\end{frame}

% -------------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}{The obvious approach: ask the AI}
\begin{columns}[T]
\column{0.52\textwidth}
\textbf{One prompt:} 77 plants, \$0.02, 30 seconds.\\
With RAG + decomposition: 163/163, F1=89.8\%, \$0.06.

\medskip
\textbf{Go further:} reasoning + deep research + subagent flock\\
$\to$ 77k chars, 162 rows, 46 source refs, \$0.79, 8 min.

\medskip
14 models across 10 labs: impressive range of outputs,\\
consistent pattern of uncertainty.

\column{0.45\textwidth}
\textbf{Both approaches share the same ceiling:}

\medskip
\begin{itemize}
  \item Which plants are missing and why?
  \item Are the capacities right?
  \item 46 source refs --- none machine-checked
  \item No way to update incrementally
  \item Would it work for Indonesia?
  \item Would you stake a policy recommendation on it?
\end{itemize}

\medskip
A plausible table in seconds.\\
But can we \textbf{trust} it ---\\
without cognitive surrender?
\end{columns}
\end{frame}

% -------------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}{The central question}
\begin{center}
\large
A plausible table in seconds.\\[1em]

\textbf{But can we trust it --- without cognitive surrender?}

\bigskip\bigskip
\normalsize
Research-quality data isn't correct data.\\
It's data whose errors are \textbf{locatable}.

\bigskip
\textit{This talk: what breaks, what enables locatability,\\
and where stateful-agentic architectures lead.}
\end{center}
\end{frame}

% -------------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}{Why not just verify the output?}
\begin{columns}[T]
\column{0.48\textwidth}
\textbf{Three options:}

\medskip
\begin{description}
  \item[Trust] Accept the table.\\
        {\small (Cognitive surrender.)}
  \item[Verify] Check each row against cited sources.
        Fix errors. Keep the table.\\
        {\small (Fast: ${\sim}$3h for 162 rows.)}
  \item[Re-extract] Build validated corpus,
        extract independently, use LLM list
        for coverage only.\\
        {\small (Slower, but reusable.)}
\end{description}

\column{0.48\textwidth}
\textbf{Verification is sufficient for one table.}

\medskip
But it has a ceiling:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Can only check what the LLM listed ---
        cannot find the \textbf{86 missing plants}
  \item Cannot update when PDP8 is revised
  \item Does not transfer to the next country
  \item Produces a verified table,
        not a reusable process
\end{itemize}

\medskip
\textbf{Post-hoc LLM verification fails.}\\
Multi-agent panel (3 models, 3 runs):\\
\textbf{0--10\%} inter-verifier agreement.\\
{\scriptsize The 0--4 rubric asks models to recall citations from parametric knowledge. Opinions, not provenance.}

\medskip
For one dataset, verify manually.\\
For 60 campaigns, \textbf{front-load the justification}.
\end{columns}
\end{frame}

% -------------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}{From extraction to information fusion}
\begin{columns}[T]
\column{0.52\textwidth}
\textbf{Reframe the problem.}

\medskip
RAG treats extraction as\\
\textit{document $\to$ table} in one shot.

\medskip
The real problem is \textbf{fusion}: diverse information fragments,
accumulated into a statistical table through a controlled,
inspectable process.

\medskip
\textbf{Three fragment types:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Serial} (tables: PDP annexes, EVN reports) ---
        schema align + record linkage; defines the skeleton
  \item \textbf{Multi-scalar} (prose lists) ---
        entity existence check; confirms or adds rows
  \item \textbf{Scalar} (isolated facts) ---
        attribute update on matched entity; fills cells
\end{itemize}

\medskip
{\small v0 scope: serial (tables) only. $\sim$18 tables for Vietnam alone.}

\column{0.44\textwidth}
\textbf{The fusion primitive:}

\medskip
\begin{center}
$\mathrm{master} + \mathrm{fragment} \longrightarrow \mathrm{master}'$
\end{center}

\medskip
One source at a time. Never\\
all fragments in one shot.

\medskip
\textbf{Consequences:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Every step has an inspectable diff
  \item Cost scales with $N$, not $N\!\times\!S$
  \item Auditable by construction
  \item Failure is localized to one step
\end{itemize}

\medskip
This is the \textbf{stateful} in\\
``stateful-agentic.''

\medskip
Separating extraction from fusion earns reproducibility:\\
fusion can be deterministic\\
even when extraction isn't.
\end{columns}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Epistemic accountability: LLM vs pipeline}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Property} & \textbf{LLM report} & \textbf{Fusion pipeline} \\
\midrule
Provenance     & ``Claude said so'' & cell $\to$ source ID $\to$ document \\
Update         & regenerate all      & append a source \\
Audit          & opaque              & step log + sidecar + registry \\
Falsifiable    & no                  & check source against claim \\
Incremental    & no                  & asset by asset, source by source \\
Stateful       & no                  & registry + memory persist \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\medskip
\begin{columns}[T]
\column{0.48\textwidth}
The LLM is a \textbf{compressed index}.\\
Use it for \emph{scope discovery},\\
not as ground truth.

\column{0.48\textwidth}
\textbf{Sourced extraction (3 Opus runs):}\\
$\approx$100\% of plants cite a corpus source (median 100\%, min 99\%).\\
$\approx$98\% of citations trace to a corpus file (median 98\%).\\
\textbf{$\approx$80\% end-to-end traceable} (citation $\to$ file $\to$ plant name; median 79\%, $n$=3 runs).

\medskip
{\small Decomposed RAG (best F1=89.8\%):
\textbf{0\% traceable} --- no citations emitted.\\
Coverage vs.\ traceability: the open trade-off.}
\end{columns}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Verification at scale: three tiers}
\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}rl@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Tier} & \textbf{Mechanism} \\
\midrule
0 & Diff audit: only changed cells need verification \\[2pt]
1 & String match (deterministic) cell $\leftrightarrow$ source table \\[2pt]
2 & LLM adjudication on tier-1 misses $\to$ candidate rule \\[2pt]
3 & HITL ratification $\to$ rule enters memory (audit trail) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\medskip
\begin{columns}[T]
\column{0.48\textwidth}
\textbf{What this buys:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Determinism first; LLM only as fallback
  \item Human only ratifies \emph{once} per rule
  \item Memory makes run $N+1$ cheaper than run $N$
  \item Escalation rate is itself a signal
\end{itemize}

\column{0.48\textwidth}
\textbf{Metrics:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Escalation rate per fusion step
  \item Rule count growth (per taxonomy)
  \item Ratification acceptance rate
\end{itemize}

\medskip
{\small Dead-end avoided: multi-agent panels (ticket 0059). Here the human gates every rule once, not every cell every time.}
\end{columns}

\medskip
{\small\textit{Design --- v0 implementation in progress; empirical results in Phases 2+3 (post-talk).}}

\begin{columns}[T]
\column{0.48\textwidth}
\textbf{Four information layers:}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Document store} --- cached raw files
  \item \textbf{Validated corpus} --- Zotero-indexed;\\

  \item \textbf{Master + sidecar} --- fusion output\\

  \item \textbf{Memory} --- per-taxonomy rule store\\
        {\scriptsize aliases / units / source-terms / attribute-synonyms + audit.jsonl}
\end{enumerate}

\column{0.48\textwidth}
\textbf{Agents, each owns one concern:}
\begin{description}
  \item[Watcher] source discovery
  \item[Curator] HITL source triage\\
        $\to$ writes registry entries
  \item[Analyst] runs the fusion primitive\\
        (chronological order with authority tie-breaker)\\
        $\to$ mutates master + sidecar\\
        $\to$ appends to fusion\_log
  \item[Auditor] runs 3-tier verification\\
        $\to$ proposes + ratifies rules
\end{description}
\end{columns}

\medskip
\centering
\textbf{State is persistent.} Registry, sidecar, log, memory: all git-tracked.\\
{\small Next run starts where this one left off. That's the path to 60 campaigns.}

\section{Perspective}

% -------------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}{What we learned about methods}
\begin{columns}[T]
\column{0.48\textwidth}
\textbf{Method conditions, ranked by impact:}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Documentation} (RAG): +18 to +43\,pp
  \item \textbf{Decomposition}: 60\% $\to$ 99\%
  \item \textbf{Aggregation} (union vote): +35\,pp at zero cost
  \item \textbf{Conversation} (multi-turn): marginal gain over single-shot
  \item Model choice alone: \MinBaselinePlants--\MaxBaselinePlants{} plants
\end{enumerate}

\medskip
\textbf{Insight:}\\
Which method you use matters more than which model you pick.

\column{0.48\textwidth}
\textbf{Key numbers:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \NumModelsTested{} models, 6 condition axes, \$0.82
  \item Best baseline: 125/163 (model only)
  \item Best RAG: 141/163 (cheap model + docs)
  \item \textbf{Best overall: 163/163, mean F1=89.8\% [85.8--95.6\%], \$0.06}
\end{itemize}

\medskip
\textbf{Bottom line:}\\
An off-the-shelf pipeline with the right \textbf{method conditions} recovers 89.8\% [85.8--95.6\%] of an expert inventory for \$0.06.
\end{columns}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{What this pilot measured --- and what it didn't}
\begin{columns}[T]
\column{0.48\textwidth}
\textbf{What the pilot establishes:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Method conditions (RAG, decomposition, aggregation)
        matter more than model choice
  \item Off-the-shelf pipeline recovers 89.8\% [85.8--95.6\%] of an expert
        inventory for \$0.06
  \item Feasibility of automated evaluation via
        fuzzy reconciliation
\end{itemize}

\column{0.48\textwidth}
\textbf{What it cannot distinguish:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Extraction vs.\ recall} --- models trained on
        PDP7/PDP8 may be reciting, not extracting
  \item \textbf{Task-specific vs.\ general} --- one reference
        (N=163), one country, one sector
  \item \textbf{Signal vs.\ noise} --- 3 runs, point F1,
        no confidence intervals
  \item \textbf{Method interactions} --- axes tested
        separately, never composed
  \item \textbf{Temperature} --- 211/280 runs used provider
        defaults ($t{\approx}1$); $t{=}0$ enforced only for
        later sweeps (PR\,\#244)
\end{itemize}
\end{columns}

\medskip
\centering\small
These are not flaws --- they are \textbf{design requirements} for a production data pipeline.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Why a knowledge graph?}
\textbf{v0 is relational.} Master + sidecar + registry are CSVs. This is sufficient
for one country, one sector, one source chain.

\medskip
A \textbf{knowledge graph} becomes necessary at three boundaries:

\medskip
\begin{description}
  \item[Entity resolution at scale]
        ``Vinh Tan 1'', ``VT1'', ``Vĩnh Tân 1'', ``Vinh Tan Unit 1'' are one plant.
        v0 handles this with an alias rule store.
        A KG handles it natively as identity edges --- across countries and languages.

  \item[Multi-hop provenance]
        A capacity figure sourced from PDP7, revised by PDP8, confirmed by EVN
        is a \emph{path} in a graph. Forcing it into a flat sidecar loses the
        structure needed for audit and defeasible reasoning.

  \item[Cross-domain fusion]
        Plants link to companies, permits, grid operators, emissions records.
        ASEAN $\times$ 6 fuel types $\times$ permit data is a graph problem,
        not a master-table extension.
\end{description}

\textbf{v2+:} KG once entity resolution and cross-domain fusion are the bottleneck.
