## **1. Sector Overview**

### **Electricity Mix (2026)**
- **Coal:** 45% of generation (123 TWh), ~28–30 GW installed capacity
- **Hydro:** 29% (80.6 TWh), ~24.6 GW
- **Natural Gas (Domestic & LNG):** 10% (26.4 TWh), ~7–13.5 GW (rising to 22–33 GW by 2030)
- **Renewables (Solar, Wind, Biomass):** 14–15% (40.9 TWh), ~20–25 GW
- **Oil & Other:** 2% (3.5 TWh)

**Total Installed Capacity (2026):** ~80–85 GW

### **Policy Framework**
- **Power Development Plan VIII (PDP8, Revised 2025):** Guides Vietnam’s power sector to 2030, with a vision to 2050. Key targets:
  - **No new coal plants after 2025** (only ongoing projects allowed)
  - **Coal phase-out by 2050:** All coal plants to convert to biomass/ammonia or retire
  - **LNG expansion:** 22.4 GW by 2030, with a focus on domestic gas first, then LNG
  - **Renewables:** 28–36% of mix by 2030, 74–75% by 2050
  - **Grid investment:** USD 136.3 billion (2026–2030), with USD 118.2 billion for generation

### **Key Institutional Actors**
- **Ministry of Industry and Trade (MOIT):** Policy, planning, and regulation
- **Vietnam Electricity (EVN):** State-owned monopoly on transmission, distribution, and major generation
- **National Power System Dispatch Centre (NPSDC):** Grid operation and dispatch
- **PetroVietnam (PVN):** Domestic gas production and gas-fired power
- **Vinacomin:** Coal production and supply

### **Current Challenges (2026)**
- **Supply shortages:** Rising demand (7–15% annual growth), delayed projects, and fuel supply risks
- **Grid congestion:** Transmission bottlenecks, especially in the North
- **Fuel security:** Declining domestic gas reserves, reliance on LNG imports, and coal supply risks
- **Project delays:** Slow progress on large-scale thermal and hydropower projects
- **Environmental and social issues:** Air pollution, resettlement, and community opposition

## **2. Per-Plant Narratives (Selected Examples)**

### **Vũng Áng-1 (Coal)**
- **Development:** Built by PetroVietnam and Lilama, construction started 2009, units commissioned 2014–2015.
- **Issues:** Environmental concerns, cooling water discharge, and ash storage.
- **Status:** Operating.

### **Vũng Áng-2 (Coal, USC)**
- **Development:** USD 2.2bn BOT project by Mitsubishi (60%) and KEPCO (40%), inaugurated April 2026.
- **Issues:** Part of PDP8’s last coal projects; will transition to biomass/ammonia by 2050.
- **Status:** Operating (as of April 2026).

### **Vũng Áng-3 (Coal → Gas)**
- **Development:** Originally planned as 2.4 GW coal plant, but converted to 4.8 GW gas in 2021.
- **Issues:** Samsung withdrew; status uncertain as of 2026.
- **Status:** Cancelled as coal; proposed as gas (pre-FID).

### **Quảng Trạch I (Coal, Supercritical)**
- **Development:** 1,403 MW (2 units), first unit connected to grid April 2026, commercial operation May 2026.
- **Issues:** Key project to address 2026 dry season shortages.
- **Status:** Unit 1 operating (as of May 2026), Unit 2 under construction.

### **Duyên Hải Complex (Coal)**
- **Development:** Three plants (Duyên Hải-1, -2, -3), total ~3,600 MW, developed by EVN and Teknik Janakuasa.
- **Issues:** Environmental concerns, resettlement, and ash storage.
- **Status:** All units operating.

### **Nhơn Trạch (Gas, CCGT)**
- **Development:** PetroVietnam’s LNG-fired plant, first unit (812 MW) operational 2025, second unit expected 2026.
- **Issues:** Part of Vietnam’s first LNG projects; delays in financing and PPAs.
- **Status:** Unit 1 operating, Unit 2 under construction.

### **Phú Mỹ (Gas, CCGT)**
- **Development:** Complex of four gas-fired plants (Phú Mỹ 1, 2-1, 2-2, 4), total ~3,900 MW, operated by EVN and Mekong Energy (EDF/Sumitomo/TEPCO).
- **Issues:** Aging infrastructure, transition to LNG/hydrogen planned.
- **Status:** All units operating.

### **Long An (LNG, CCGT)**
- **Development:** 3,000 MW LNG plant, originally coal, switched to LNG in 2017.
- **Issues:** Financing and site clearance delays; first unit expected 2025–2026.
- **Status:** Pre-construction (permitting).

### **Sông Hậu-1 & -2 (Coal)**
- **Development:** Sông Hậu-1 (1,200 MW) built by PetroVietnam, Sông Hậu-2 (2,000 MW) by Toyo Ink.
- **Issues:** Sông Hậu-2 BOT contract terminated in 2024; status uncertain.
- **Status:** Sông Hậu-1 operating; Sông Hậu-2 cancelled.

### **Vĩnh Tân Complex (Coal)**
- **Development:** Four plants (Vĩnh Tân-1, -2, -3, -4), total ~6,200 MW, developed by EVN, Vinacomin, and Chinese partners.
- **Issues:** Environmental violations, air pollution, and community opposition.
- **Status:** Vĩnh Tân-1, -2, -4 operating; Vĩnh Tân-3 status uncertain.

### **Nghi Sơn-2 (Coal, USC)**
- **Development:** 1,200 MW BOT project by Marubeni/KEPCO, commissioned 2022.
- **Issues:** Part of BOT tax exemption list.
- **Status:** Operating.

### **Mông Dương-2 (Coal, USC)**
- **Development:** 1,200 MW BOT project, commissioned 2015.
- **Issues:** Environmental concerns, part of BOT tax exemption list.
- **Status:** Operating.

### **Hải Phòng-3 (Coal)**
- **Development:** 2,400 MW (4 units), Units 1–2 delayed to 2025–2026, Units 3–4 cancelled.
- **Issues:** Financing and permitting delays.
- **Status:** Pre-construction (Units 1–2).

### **Na Dương-1 & -2 (Coal)**
- **Development:** Na Dương-1 (100 MW) built 2002–2005, Na Dương-2 (200 MW) under construction.
- **Issues:** Na Dương-2 delayed, target COD 2026.
- **Status:** Na Dương-1 operating; Na Dương-2 under construction.

### **O Mon (Gas, CCGT)**
- **Development:** Complex of four gas-fired plants (O Mon I–IV), total ~2,800 MW, developed by PetroVietnam and partners.
- **Issues:** Fuel supply from Block B gas field, delays in LNG infrastructure.
- **Status:** O Mon I–III operating; O Mon IV under construction.

## **6. Notes on Data Quality and Confidence**

- **High Confidence:** Plants with recent COD, official government or EVN reports, or multiple independent sources.
- **Medium Confidence:** Plants with delays, pre-FID status, or conflicting reports (e.g., Vũng Áng-3, Vĩnh Tân-3).
- **Low Confidence:** Proposed projects with no recent updates or primary sources.
- **Cancelled Projects:** Included if officially cancelled or removed from PDP8 (e.g., Sông Hậu-2, Vũng Áng-3 as coal).
- **Sources:** All sources are cited; URLs are verified where possible. For some older or cancelled projects, URLs may not be available (“URL not verified”).
