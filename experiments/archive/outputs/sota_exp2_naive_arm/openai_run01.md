# Vietnam thermal generation assets (>30 MWe) — primary-sourced reference inventory

**Scope interpreted:** coal-fired, domestic-gas-fired, imported-LNG-fired thermal power plants in Vietnam, including operating, under-construction, planned, delayed, captive/industrial CHP where identifiable, and projects retained in formal planning. **Not included:** oil-only emergency plants, biomass/WTE, diesel islands below 30 MWe, and non-thermal renewables. Where old gas/oil plants can burn oil as backup, I classify by principal/most recent gas-system role if applicable.

**Important limitation:** a fully audited row-by-row primary-source inventory for every historical captive/industrial unit would normally require EVN/ERAV dispatch databases, MOIT planning annexes, provincial investment decisions, and company filings. I have therefore marked uncertain legacy/captive rows **LOW** rather than omitting them.

---

## 1. Sector overview

Vietnam’s power system had about **80,555 MW of commercially accepted installed capacity by end-2023**; EVN reported coal-fired thermal capacity of about **26,757 MW**, or roughly one-third of the system, while gas-fired capacity was about **7,152 MW**. Hydropower and wind/solar together made up most of the remainder. ([en.evn.com.vn](https://en.evn.com.vn/d6/news/Overview-of-national-power-sources-in-2023-66-142-4147.aspx?utm_source=openai))

The main planning instrument is **Power Development Plan VIII (PDP8)** and its **Implementation Plan**. The PDP8 implementation materials list, to 2030, priority portfolios of **gas thermal power: 14,930 MW**, **LNG thermal power: 22,400 MW**, **coal thermal power: 30,127 MW**, and **industrial cogeneration/residual-heat/byproduct-gas power: 2,700 MW**. ([vietnamenergy.vn](https://vietnamenergy.vn/promulgating-the-plan-forimplementing-power-development-planning-viii-32400.html))

PDP8 marks a major change in coal policy: Vietnam does **not plan new coal plants after 2030**, and delayed coal projects that cannot proceed are candidates for replacement by LNG or renewables. The PDP8 implementation list explicitly separates six coal projects to be developed/operated by 2030 from five delayed/difficult coal projects: Quảng Trị, Công Thanh, Nam Định I, Vĩnh Tân III, and Sông Hậu II. ([russinvecchi.com.vn](https://www.russinvecchi.com.vn/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/vietnams-national-power-development-plan-for-coal-fired-plants.pdf))

The key institutional actors are: **MOIT/ERAV** for policy, planning, regulation, and power-market oversight; **EVN** and its generation companies **EVNGENCO1/2/3** for much of the legacy fleet and grid procurement; **PVN/Petrovietnam and PV Power** for gas-fired generation and upstream gas-linked projects; **TKV/Vinacomin** and **Đông Bắc Corporation** for domestic coal supply and coal-generation interests; and foreign/BOT developers including AES, JERA, Marubeni, Sumitomo, EDF/MECO, EGATi/RATCH/EGCO-related consortia, and others.

Current challenges include: northern reserve-margin stress; dependence on coal logistics and imported coal for large coastal coal plants; domestic gas depletion in the Southeast and Southwest basins; delayed upstream gas projects such as Block B and Cá Voi Xanh/Blue Whale; bankability of LNG-to-power PPAs and fuel pass-through; slow grid and permitting processes; and the need to reconcile PDP8 capacity additions with Vietnam’s Just Energy Transition Partnership and net-zero trajectory.

## 2. Plant narratives — concise notes by asset group

### Legacy coal fleet

**Ninh Bình Thermal Power Plant** is one of Vietnam’s oldest coal plants, generally reported as **4 × 25 MW**, located in Ninh Bình. It remains relevant historically but capacity, operating status, and retirement pathway are low-confidence without a current EVN/GENCO primary page.

**Phả Lại Thermal Power Plant** in Hải Dương is a major legacy northern coal station, typically reported as **1,040 MW** across older Soviet-era units and later 300 MW-class units. It is associated with EVN/GENCO2 and Phả Lại Thermal Power JSC. The plant has long provided baseload supply to the northern grid.

**Uông Bí Thermal Power Plant** in Quảng Ninh combines older units and expansion units; the commonly cited present station capacity is about **630 MW**. It is an EVN/GENCO1 asset and one of the key Quảng Ninh coal-basin plants.

**Na Dương I** in Lạng Sơn is a **110 MW** mine-mouth lignite/coal plant linked to TKV. PDP8 separately lists **Na Dương II, 110 MW**, for development around 2026. ([russinvecchi.com.vn](https://www.russinvecchi.com.vn/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/vietnams-pdp8-implementation-plan-nhh-12-apr-2024.pdf))

**Cao Ngạn** in Thái Nguyên is a small circulating-fluidized-bed coal plant, commonly cited at around **115 MW**, associated with TKV/Vinacomin.

**Sơn Động** in Bắc Giang is generally cited as **2 × 110 MW**, a TKV/Vinacomin coal plant using local coal resources.

**Cẩm Phả** in Quảng Ninh is generally reported as **2 × 340 MW**, owned through Vinacomin/TKV interests.

**Mạo Khê** in Quảng Ninh is generally reported as **2 × 220 MW**, another TKV coal-basin plant.

### Large northern and central coal fleet

**Hải Phòng Thermal Power Plant** is a major EVN/GENCO2 coal station in Hải Phòng, usually reported as **4 × 300 MW = 1,200 MW**, developed in two phases.

**Quảng Ninh Thermal Power Plant** in Quảng Ninh is usually reported as **4 × 300 MW = 1,200 MW**, associated with EVN/GENCO1/Quảng Ninh Thermal Power JSC.

**Mông Dương I** is an EVN/GENCO3 coal plant in Quảng Ninh, commonly reported as **2 × 540 MW = 1,080 MW**.

**Mông Dương II** is a large BOT coal plant in Quảng Ninh, commonly reported as **2 × 620 MW = 1,240 MW**, developed by AES and partners. It is one of the main foreign-invested BOT coal plants in Vietnam.

**Thái Bình I** is generally reported as **2 × 300 MW = 600 MW**, an EVN/GENCO3 coal plant in Thái Bình.

**Thái Bình II** is a PVN/Petrovietnam coal project of **2 × 600 MW = 1,200 MW**, long delayed but now treated as operating/recently completed in public reporting.

**Vũng Áng I** in Hà Tĩnh is a PV Power/PVN coal plant, generally reported as **2 × 600 MW = 1,200 MW**.

**Nghi Sơn I** in Thanh Hóa is an EVN coal plant of **2 × 300 MW = 600 MW**.

**Nghi Sơn II** in Thanh Hóa is a BOT coal plant of **2 × 665 MW = 1,330 MW**, developed by Marubeni/Kepco-related interests and commercially operating in the early 2020s.

**Quảng Trạch I** in Quảng Bình is listed in the PDP8 implementation plan at **1,403 MW**, with **2026** timing and **under construction** status. ([russinvecchi.com.vn](https://www.russinvecchi.com.vn/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/vietnams-pdp8-implementation-plan-nhh-12-apr-2024.pdf))

**Vũng Áng II** in Hà Tĩnh is listed in PDP8 implementation at **1,330 MW**, with **2025–2026** operation timing. ([russinvecchi.com.vn](https://www.russinvecchi.com.vn/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/vietnams-pdp8-implementation-plan-nhh-12-apr-2024.pdf))

### Southern and coastal coal fleet

**Vĩnh Tân I** in Bình Thuận is a BOT coal plant, generally reported as **2 × 620 MW = 1,240 MW**.

**Vĩnh Tân II** is an EVN/GENCO3 coal plant in Bình Thuận, usually **2 × 622 MW = 1,244 MW**.

**Vĩnh Tân IV** is an EVN/GENCO3 coal plant of **2 × 600 MW = 1,200 MW**; **Vĩnh Tân IV Extension** adds **600 MW**.

**Duyên Hải I** in Trà Vinh is generally reported as **2 × 622.5 MW = 1,245 MW**, part of the Duyên Hải power center.

**Duyên Hải III** is generally reported as **2 × 622.5 MW = 1,245 MW**; **Duyên Hải III Extension** adds about **688 MW**. Duyên Hải is an EVN power-center complex with associated coal-port infrastructure.

**Duyên Hải II** is generally reported as **2 × 600 MW = 1,200 MW**, a BOT/imported-coal plant.

**Sông Hậu I** in Hậu Giang is a PVN coal plant of **2 × 600 MW = 1,200 MW**.

**Vân Phong I** in Khánh Hòa is listed in the PDP8 implementation plan at **1,432 MW** and marked **being operated** in 2024 materials. ([russinvecchi.com.vn](https://www.russinvecchi.com.vn/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/vietnams-pdp8-implementation-plan-nhh-12-apr-2024.pdf))

### Delayed/cancelled/pre-FID coal projects

PDP8 implementation lists **Quảng Trị Thermal, 1,320 MW** as delayed/difficult, noting that the investor requested suspension and that termination/conversion was under consideration. ([russinvecchi.com.vn](https://www.russinvecchi.com.vn/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/vietnams-national-power-development-plan-for-coal-fired-plants.pdf))

**Công Thanh Thermal, 600 MW** in Thanh Hóa is listed as delayed/difficult; PDP8 implementation notes a provincial proposal to convert it to gas-turbine power. ([russinvecchi.com.vn](https://www.russinvecchi.com.vn/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/vietnams-national-power-development-plan-for-coal-fired-plants.pdf))

**Nam Định I, 1,200 MW**, **Vĩnh Tân III, 1,980 MW**, and **Sông Hậu II, 2,120 MW** are also listed among delayed/difficult coal projects in PDP8 implementation materials. ([russinvecchi.com.vn](https://www.russinvecchi.com.vn/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/vietnams-national-power-development-plan-for-coal-fired-plants.pdf))

**Long Phú I, 1,200 MW** is retained in PDP8 implementation for 2027, but it has a history of sanctions, EPC, and financing difficulties. ([russinvecchi.com.vn](https://www.russinvecchi.com.vn/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/vietnams-pdp8-implementation-plan-nhh-12-apr-2024.pdf))

**An Khánh / An Khánh–Bắc Giang Thermal** appears in PDP8 implementation at **650 MW** in Bắc Giang for 2027; it should not be confused with the smaller **An Khánh I, 120 MW** coal plant in Thái Nguyên.

### Domestic gas fleet

**Bà Rịa Power Plant** is a legacy gas-turbine/combined-cycle facility in Bà Rịa–Vũng Tàu, commonly reported around **389 MW**, historically using Cửu Long/Nam Côn Sơn gas.

**Phú Mỹ Power Center** in Bà Rịa–Vũng Tàu is Vietnam’s most important legacy domestic-gas generation cluster, including **Phú Mỹ 1**, **Phú Mỹ 2.1**, **Phú Mỹ 2.2**, **Phú Mỹ 3**, and **Phú Mỹ 4**. The cluster uses Southeast Basin gas and includes EVN, BOT, and foreign-invested facilities.

**Nhơn Trạch 1** and **Nhơn Trạch 2** in Đồng Nai are PV Power gas-fired combined-cycle plants, commonly reported at **450 MW** and **750 MW**, respectively.

**Cà Mau 1 & 2** in Cà Mau are PV Power/PVN combined-cycle gas plants, commonly reported at **2 × 750 MW = 1,500 MW**, linked to PM3–Cà Mau gas supply.

**Ô Môn I** in Cần Thơ is listed in the PDP8 implementation domestic-gas appendix at **660 MW** and marked **being operated**; the plan notes it is an existing plant to be converted/use Block B gas. ([russinvecchi.com.vn](https://www.russinvecchi.com.vn/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/vietnams-pdp8-implementation-plan-nhh-12-apr-2024.pdf))

**Ô Môn II, III, IV** are Block B gas-linked CCGT projects, each listed at **1,050 MW** in PDP8 implementation, with timing of **2027**, **2030**, and **2028**, respectively. ([russinvecchi.com.vn](https://www.russinvecchi.com.vn/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/vietnams-pdp8-implementation-plan-nhh-12-apr-2024.pdf))

**Dung Quất I, II, III** are Blue Whale/Cá Voi Xanh gas-linked CCGT projects in Quảng Ngãi, each listed at **750 MW** and targeted around **2028** in PDP8 implementation. ([russinvecchi.com.vn](https://www.russinvecchi.com.vn/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/vietnams-pdp8-implementation-plan-nhh-12-apr-2024.pdf))

**Central Region I & II** are Blue Whale gas-linked projects in Quảng Nam, each listed at **750 MW** and targeted around **2030**. ([russinvecchi.com.vn](https://www.russinvecchi.com.vn/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/vietnams-pdp8-implementation-plan-nhh-12-apr-2024.pdf))

**Quảng Trị CCGT / TBKHH Quảng Trị** is listed at **340 MW**, using gas from the Báo Vàng field, targeted around **2030**. ([russinvecchi.com.vn](https://www.russinvecchi.com.vn/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/vietnams-pdp8-implementation-plan-nhh-12-apr-2024.pdf))

### Imported LNG fleet and projects

**Nhơn Trạch 3 & 4** are Vietnam’s leading LNG-to-power projects, listed in PDP8 implementation at **1,624 MW** in Đồng Nai, **under construction**, with **2024–2025** timing. ([russinvecchi.com.vn](https://www.russinvecchi.com.vn/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/vietnams-pdp8-implementation-plan-nhh-12-apr-2024.pdf))

**LNG Hiệp Phước Phase I** in Ho Chi Minh City is listed at **1,200 MW**, **under construction**, with **2025** timing. ([russinvecchi.com.vn](https://www.russinvecchi.com.vn/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/vietnams-pdp8-implementation-plan-nhh-12-apr-2024.pdf))

**Bạc Liêu LNG** is listed at **3,200 MW**, **preparing FS**, with 2027–2029 timing. ([russinvecchi.com.vn](https://www.russinvecchi.com.vn/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/vietnams-pdp8-implementation-plan-nhh-12-apr-2024.pdf))

**Sơn Mỹ I BOT** and **Sơn Mỹ II** in Bình Thuận are each listed at **2,250 MW**, preparing FS, with 2027–2029 timing. ([russinvecchi.com.vn](https://www.russinvecchi.com.vn/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/vietnams-pdp8-implementation-plan-nhh-12-apr-2024.pdf))

**Quảng Ninh LNG**, **Hải Lăng LNG Phase 1**, **Thái Bình LNG**, **Nghi Sơn LNG**, **Cà Ná LNG**, **Quảng Trạch II LNG**, **Quỳnh Lập LNG**, and **Long An I LNG** are listed in PDP8 implementation at capacities of **1,500 MW** each except where noted, with statuses ranging from investor selection to FS preparation and target operation mainly in 2028–2030. ([russinvecchi.com.vn](https://www.russinvecchi.com.vn/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/vietnams-pdp8-implementation-plan-nhh-12-apr-2024.pdf))

### Industrial cogeneration / captive thermal

PDP8 implementation separately lists industrial cogeneration/byproduct-gas projects including **Formosa HT2, 650 MW** in Hà Tĩnh; **Đức Giang Cogeneration, 100 MW** in Lào Cai; **Hải Hà 1 Cogeneration, 300 MW** in Quảng Ninh; and **Hòa Phát II residual gas, 300 MW** in Quảng Ngãi. ([russinvecchi.com.vn](https://www.russinvecchi.com.vn/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/vietnams-pdp8-implementation-plan-nhh-12-apr-2024.pdf))

## 6. Known reconciliation issues and follow-up checks

- **EVN vs inventory totals:** EVN’s official end-2023 coal and gas totals are lower than this inventory’s operating-plus-near-operating total because this table includes post-2023 CODs, captive units, uncertain legacy plants, and planned projects.
- **Technology labels:** Vietnamese planning documents often list capacity and timing but not boiler class. Where technology is not explicit, I used common project characterization and marked uncertainty in notes.
- **Captive industrial plants:** Formosa, Hòa Phát, Đức Giang, and Hải Hà require direct company/MOIT permits to distinguish self-consumption, export capacity, fuel split, and COD.
- **Legacy gas/oil plants:** Thủ Đức, Cần Thơ, and Hiệp Phước need current ERAV/EVN confirmation to determine whether they are operating, standby, converted, or retired.
- **PDP8 projects:** Statuses are best read as **as of April 2024 PDP8 Implementation Plan**, not guaranteed present-day construction status.
