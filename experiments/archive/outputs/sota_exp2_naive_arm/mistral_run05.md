## **1. Sector Overview**

### **Electricity Mix (2026)**
- **Coal:** ~32% of total generation (28,100 MW installed capacity)
- **Hydropower:** ~28% (24,640 MW)
- **Gas (domestic & LNG):** ~10% (7,000–13,500 MW, rising to 22,400 MW by 2030)
- **Renewables (solar, wind, biomass):** ~28% (24,453 MW)
- **Oil:** <1%

### **Policy Framework**
- **Power Development Plan VIII (PDP8, 2021–2030, vision to 2050):** Approved April 15, 2025, sets capacity targets of 89.655–99.934 GW by 2030 and 205.7–228.6 GW by 2050.
- **Key targets:**
  - **Coal:** Capped at 30,127 MW by 2030; no new coal plants after 2030; phase-out by 2050.
  - **Gas:** 14,930 MW (domestic) + 22,400 MW (LNG) by 2030.
  - **Renewables:** 28–36% of mix by 2030, 74–75% by 2050.
- **Just Energy Transition Partnership (JETP):** $15.5 billion to cap and phase out unabated coal.

### **Key Institutional Actors**
- **Ministry of Industry and Trade (MOIT):** Policy, planning, and regulation.
- **Vietnam Electricity (EVN):** State-owned monopoly on transmission and distribution; major power purchaser.
- **PetroVietnam (PVN):** Gas production, LNG, and gas-fired power.
- **Vinacomin:** Coal production and coal-fired power.
- **National Power System Dispatch Centre (NPSDC):** Grid and dispatch management.

### **Current Challenges**
- **Supply shortages:** Northern Vietnam faces power shortages due to hydropower variability and delayed thermal projects.
- **Grid constraints:** Congestion and curtailment of renewables.
- **Financing:** Many thermal projects delayed due to financing and regulatory hurdles.
- **Environmental and social issues:** Air pollution, water use, and community opposition.

## **2. Per-Plant Narratives (Selected Examples)**

### **Vũng Áng-1 (Coal, Hà Tĩnh)**
- **Development history:** Construction began August 2009; Unit 1 COD December 2014, Unit 2 May 2015.
- **Notable issues:** Environmental concerns, ash disposal, and community opposition.
- **Attributes:** 2 × 600 MW, subcritical, owned by PetroVietnam.

### **Vũng Áng-2 (Coal, Hà Tĩnh)**
- **Development history:** Construction started 2021; BOT model; expected COD 2026.
- **Notable issues:** Foreign investor exemptions from global minimum tax.
- **Attributes:** 2 × 660 MW, ultrasupercritical, owned by VAPCO (CLP/Mitsubishi).

### **Quảng Trạch-1 (Coal, Quảng Bình)**
- **Development history:** Construction started 2011; Unit 1 COD May 2026, Unit 2 October 2026.
- **Notable issues:** Key project for national energy security; EPC by Mitsubishi/Hyundai/CC1.
- **Attributes:** 2 × 600 MW, supercritical, owned by PetroVietnam.

### **Nhơn Trạch 3 & 4 (LNG, Đồng Nai)**
- **Development history:** First LNG plants in Vietnam; Nhơn Trạch 3 COD 2025, Nhơn Trạch 4 2026.
- **Notable issues:** PetroVietnam’s first LNG projects; supply chain and financing challenges.
- **Attributes:** 2 × 812 MW, CCGT, owned by PetroVietnam.

### **Vĩnh Tân Complex (Coal, Bình Thuận)**
- **Development history:** Vĩnh Tân-1 (2 × 622 MW) COD 2018; Vĩnh Tân-2 (2 × 622 MW) COD 2019; Vĩnh Tân-4 (2 × 600 MW) COD 2020.
- **Notable issues:** Environmental violations, ash disposal, and community protests.
- **Attributes:** 4 plants, 5,400 MW total, supercritical/USC, owned by EVN/Vinacomin.

### **Duyên Hải Complex (Coal, Trà Vinh)**
- **Development history:** Duyên Hải-1 (2 × 622 MW) COD 2015–2016; Duyên Hải-2 (2 × 600 MW) COD 2018–2019.
- **Notable issues:** Environmental and social impacts on local communities.
- **Attributes:** 3 plants, 3,600 MW total, supercritical, owned by EVN/Teknik Janakuasa.

### **Cancelled/Converted Projects**
- **Vũng Áng-3:** Cancelled (coal), proposed conversion to 4.8 GW gas.
- **Hải Phòng-3:** Cancelled (coal), proposed conversion to LNG.
- **Long Phú-2:** Cancelled (coal), proposed conversion to LNG.
- **Quảng Ninh-3:** Cancelled (coal).
