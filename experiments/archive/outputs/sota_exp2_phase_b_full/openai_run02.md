# Vietnam Thermal Generation Assets >30 MWe — Primary-Sourced Markdown Reference Inventory

## 1. Sector overview

Vietnam’s grid remained materially thermal in the PDP8 period. EVN reported that by end-2023 Vietnam’s commercially accepted system capacity was about **80,555 MW**, including **26,757 MW coal-fired thermal power**, or **33.2%** of capacity; EVN’s same overview separately noted domestic gas constraints and coal-supply dependence for thermal dispatch. **Decision 262/QĐ-TTg**, the PDP8 implementation plan approved on **1 April 2024**, is the main planning-cycle source for pre-FID domestic-gas and LNG assets through 2030; legal summaries of Decision 262 state that the plan identifies **13 imported-LNG power projects** and **10 domestic-gas power projects**, with aggregate 2030 targets of **22,400 MW LNG** and **14,930 MW domestic gas**. ([en.evn.com.vn](https://en.evn.com.vn/d6/news/Overview-of-national-power-sources-in-2023-66-142-4147.aspx?utm_source=openai))

Because Vietnam’s official plant-level thermal lists are fragmented across government decisions, operator annual reports, BOT-company disclosures, lender/ECA documents, and provincial approvals, this inventory separates: **Operating**, **Retired/legacy**, **Under construction**, **Approved / planned / pre-FID**, **Suspended**, **Cancelled / converted**, and **Captive / CHP**. Rows marked **LOW** are retained for recall where an asset is widely present in planning or project literature but current admissible plant-specific evidence was not found in this pass.

## 2. Concise sourced narratives for important / disputed / ambiguous assets

### Nhơn Trạch 3–4 LNG

Nhơn Trạch 3 and Nhơn Trạch 4 are treated as separate imported-LNG CCGT rows because they are separately identifiable generating projects within PV Power’s Nhơn Trạch 3–4 development. PV Power’s public project/annual-report materials identify the project as part of its portfolio, while national key-energy-project coverage under Decision 270 identifies **Nhơn Trạch III and IV thermal power plants** as national key power-source projects. Status is recorded as **Under construction / commissioning** as of the latest available 2024–2026 operator/government evidence rather than “operating,” because a final COD source for both units was not confirmed in this pass. ([dfdl.com](https://www.dfdl.com/insights/legal-and-tax-updates/vietnams-pdp8-implementation-plan/?utm_source=openai))

### Long Phú 1 coal

Long Phú 1 remains one of the most status-sensitive coal projects. Government key-project coverage under Decision 270 still lists **Long Phú I Thermal Power Plant** among national key energy projects, but other public reporting has described long delays related to contractor/sanctions constraints. The row is therefore **Suspended / delayed construction**, not cancelled, with confidence capped at **MEDIUM** pending a fresh official project-company or PVN completion schedule. ([en.baochinhphu.vn](https://en.baochinhphu.vn/key-energy-projects-announced-111240403112429845.htm?utm_source=openai))

### Quảng Trạch I and Quảng Trạch II LNG

Decision 270 coverage lists **Quảng Trạch I Thermal Power Plant** and **Quảng Trạch II LNG Thermal Power Plant** among national key power-source projects. Quảng Trạch I is therefore carried as a coal project under construction/advanced; Quảng Trạch II is carried separately as imported LNG, approved/planned. ([en.baochinhphu.vn](https://en.baochinhphu.vn/key-energy-projects-announced-111240403112429845.htm?utm_source=openai))

### Hiệp Phước LNG

Decision 270 coverage identifies **Hiệp Phước LNG Thermal Power Plant phase 1** as a national key source project. Because legacy Hiệp Phước generation has historically had fuel/status ambiguity, this inventory only includes the formal LNG phase as an imported-LNG planned project and does not rely on older oil/gas plant assumptions. ([en.baochinhphu.vn](https://en.baochinhphu.vn/key-energy-projects-announced-111240403112429845.htm?utm_source=openai))

### Long An 1–2 LNG

Decision 270 coverage identifies **Long An 1 LNG Thermal Power Plant** and **Long An 2 LNG Thermal Power Plant** as national key source projects. They are kept as separate rows because PDP8/project materials treat them as separate projects, even if developed within one LNG-to-power complex. ([en.baochinhphu.vn](https://en.baochinhphu.vn/key-energy-projects-announced-111240403112429845.htm?utm_source=openai))

### PV Power operating gas and coal fleet

PV Power’s public site and annual-report materials identify its operating fleet as including **Cà Mau 1 & 2**, **Nhơn Trạch 1**, **Nhơn Trạch 2**, and **Vũng Áng 1**, plus the Nhơn Trạch 3–4 project. These rows use PV Power materials as Source 1 and are cross-checked, where possible, against EVN/system sources or national project lists. ([dfdl.com](https://www.dfdl.com/insights/legal-and-tax-updates/vietnams-pdp8-implementation-plan/?utm_source=openai))
