# VIETNAM THERMAL POWER GENERATION INVENTORY (>30 MWe)

**As of:** May 2026 | **Classification:** Primary-sourced inventory

---

## 1. Sector Overview

Vietnam's installed generation capacity exceeded 80 GW by end-2024, with thermal power (coal, gas, LNG) supplying roughly 50–55 % of electricity output. The National Power Development Plan VIII (PDP8), approved under Decision 500/QĐ-TTg on 15 May 2023, targets by 2030: coal thermal 30,127 MW, domestic gas 14,930 MW, and LNG 22,400 MW. The Amended PDP8 (Decision 768/QĐ-TTg, 15 April 2025) raised the LNG target to 22,524 MW. An amended Implementation Plan (Decision 1509/QĐ-BCT, 30 May 2025) further refined project lists and timelines.

**Key actors.** MOIT oversees planning. EVN (via EVNGENCO1/2/3) owns the majority of utility-scale thermal assets. PetroVietnam (PVN), through PV Power, operates major gas and LNG plants. TKV (Vinacomin) runs mine-mouth coal plants. BOT investors include Marubeni, KEPCO, Sumitomo, AES, Jaks/ChinaPower, and Janakuasa.

**Challenges.** Of 13 planned LNG projects (~22,400 MW), only three totalling 2,824 MW were under construction on schedule — Nhơn Trạch 3&4 and Hiệp Phước. Five coal projects totalling ~8 GW were cancelled under PDP8 (Công Thanh, Nam Định I, Quảng Trị, Vĩnh Tân 3, Sông Hậu 2). Long Phú 1 remains delayed by sanctions on Russian EPC contractor Power Machines.

## 2. Per-Plant Narratives (Major/Disputed Assets)

**Vĩnh Tân Complex (Bình Thuận).** Four co-located coal plants totalling ~4,244 MW. Vĩnh Tân 2 (1,244 MW, EVNGENCO3) uses two 622-MW subcritical units. Vĩnh Tân 4 + 4MR (1,800 MW, EVNGENCO3, COD 2017–19) and Vĩnh Tân 1 (1,200 MW BOT). Vĩnh Tân 3 (~1,980 MW, One Energy/CLP) was cancelled.

**Long Phú 1 (Sóc Trăng).** A 1,200 MW supercritical plant owned by PVN, stalled since 2019 after sanctions on Russian contractor Power Machines. In April 2025 boiler installation began; Unit 1 scheduled Q2 2027, Unit 2 Q3 2027.

**Nhơn Trạch 3 & 4 (Đồng Nai).** Vietnam's first LNG-fired power plants, with combined capacity of ~1,624 MW, achieved commercial operation in 2025. Operated by PV Power using regasified LNG from the Thị Vải terminal.

**Ô Môn Complex (Cần Thơ).** Ô Môn I (660 MW CCGT, domestic gas, EVNGENCO2) is operating. Three planned LNG expansions (Ô Môn II/III/IV, ~1,050 MW each) await gas supply agreements and terminal infrastructure.

**Vũng Áng 2 (Hà Tĩnh).** A 1,200 MW USC coal BOT developed by Mitsubishi/KEPCO/Chugoku Electric with ~$2.2 B investment. Unit 1 reportedly achieved COD mid-2025; Unit 2 status unconfirmed as of May 2026.
