**Report Date:** 23 May 2026 | **Version:** 1.1 (Verified)

## 1. Sector Overview

Vietnam's power system exceeded 80 GW of total installed capacity by end-2025, with thermal generation (coal, gas, oil) accounting for roughly 45–50%. As of January 2023, Vietnam had an estimated 24.7 GW in coal capacity, with a further 6.1 GW under construction. Domestic gas adds ~7 GW, concentrated at Phú Mỹ, Cà Mau, Nhơn Trạch, and Ô Môn.

**PDP8 Policy:** The PDP8 Implementation Plan identifies priority capacity by 2030 including domestic gas 14,930 MW, LNG 22,400 MW, and coal 30,127 MW. The Amended PDP8 (Decision 768/QĐ-TTg, April 15, 2025) raised the LNG target to 22,524 MW and deferred Long Sơn and Long An II to 2031–2035. Of 13 LNG projects totaling 22,400 MW in PDP8, only 3 projects with 2,824 MW were under construction on schedule.

**Key Actors:** EVN (EVNGENCO1/2/3), PVN (PV Power), TKV (Vinacomin), BOT investors (AES, Marubeni, KEPCO, Sumitomo, JERA, COPI, Jaks/CECI). MOIT provides regulatory oversight.

**Challenges:** Coal phase-down under JETP vs. demand growth; LNG infrastructure delays; Long Phú 1 stalled by Russian sanctions (now restarting); renewable curtailment without adequate baseload.

## 3. Plant-Level Narratives (Major / Disputed Assets)

### Vĩnh Tân Complex (Bình Thuận)
Four plant groups totaling 5,244 MW operating. Vĩnh Tân 3 (~1,980 MW) is effectively cancelled: in February 2021 Mitsubishi announced it was withdrawing from Vinh Tan-3, citing its climate targets as the main reason.

### Long Phú 1 (Sóc Trăng)
Since Russian constructor Power Machines Company withdrew from the project due to US sanctions on January 26, 2018, the project's completed workload was kept at over 70%. Petrovietnam is implementing measures to ensure the plant will be completed and put into operation in 2027, with Unit 1 scheduled for Q2 and Unit 2 in Q3.

### Nhơn Trạch 3&4 LNG (Đồng Nai)
The project comprises two plants with a total design capacity of 1,624 MW. In January 2026, the two plants began participating in the electricity market.

### Quảng Trạch 1 (Quảng Bình)
Unit 1 of Quang Trach 1 Thermal Power Plant was successfully connected to the national grid for the first time on April 12 [2026].

### Thái Bình 2 (Thái Bình)
Long-delayed 1,200 MW PVN project; completed 2022–23 after major cost overruns. Petrovietnam cited the successful completion of Song Hau 1 and Thai Binh 2 thermal power plants as key achievements.

## BUDGET NOTE — Known Gaps

1. **Industrial CHP >30 MW not fully enumerated:** Only Formosa Hà Tĩnh included. Lee & Man (Hậu Giang), Hòa Phát (Dung Quất), various cement-plant waste-heat recovery units may exceed 30 MW threshold. Requires dedicated search.
2. **Oil/diesel peakers:** Thủ Đức and other standby turbines omitted — likely <30 MW individually or status unclear.
3. **Retired units:** Ninh Bình (100 MW, 1974) and pre-2000 Uông Bí units marked LOW confidence; retirement status unconfirmed.
4. **Sơn Động MW:** Listed as 2×110=220 MW but some sources suggest 2×55=110 MW; could not resolve.
5. **Phú Mỹ complex exact MW:** Unit configurations and MW vary across sources (±50 MW per block); LOW confidence rows flagged.
6. **Several cancelled PDP7 coal projects** (Nam Định I, Quảng Trị/EGATi, Sông Hậu 2, Vân Phong 2) excluded from table as they never broke ground and are definitively removed from PDP8.
7. **Hòa Phát Dung Quất steel mill** captive power (~240 MW reported) not included — requires verification of capacity and grid export status.
