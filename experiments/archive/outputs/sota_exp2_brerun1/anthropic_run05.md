# Vietnam Thermal Power Generation Inventory (>30 MWe)
## Primary-Sourced Asset Register — May 2026 (Verified)

---

## 1. Sector Overview

Vietnam's electricity system exceeded 80 GW of installed capacity by end-2024, with thermal generation (coal, gas, oil) providing roughly 55–60% of total output. The system is managed by Vietnam Electricity (EVN) through generation subsidiaries EVNGENCO1, EVNGENCO2, and EVNGENCO3. PetroVietnam (PVN), via PV Power, operates the major domestic gas fleet and is developing coal and LNG projects. Vinacomin (TKV) owns mine-mouth coal plants in the northeast.

PDP8 (Decision 500/QĐ-TTg, May 15 2023) sets thermal capacity to 2030 at: domestic gas 14,930 MW; LNG 22,400 MW; coal 30,127 MW; cogeneration/waste-heat/BFG 2,700 MW. PDP8 projects that Vietnam's coal capacity will peak at 30.1 GW, compared to the 27.2 GW in operation as of June 2024. On April 15 2025, the Prime Minister issued Decision 768/QĐ-TTg approving the Amended PDP8, superseding the original. Decision 262 (April 1 2024) sets out a status update on 13 imported LNG power projects and 10 domestic gas-fired power projects earmarked for completion by 2030.

In Southeast Asia, several countries are moving toward a managed exit from coal; new proposals have dwindled, driven by phaseout pledges and just transition planning in Vietnam. PDP8 stipulates that coal plants unable to convert to alternative fuels after 40+ years must cease operations; by 2050 all coal plants must convert or shut down.

Key challenges: (a) severe LNG project delays from PPA/pricing gaps; (b) coal phase-down commitments under the JETP vs. baseload reliability; (c) financing constraints as banks exit coal; (d) integration of ~25 GW variable renewables requiring flexible gas capacity.

## 2. Per-Plant Narratives (Major/Disputed/Ambiguous Assets)

### Vĩnh Tân Complex (Bình Thuận)
Vietnam's largest thermal cluster (~4,244 MW operating). On October 31, 2024, EVNGENCO3 issued the final acceptance certificate (FAC) for the Vinh Tan 4 project and the Vinh Tan 4 Expansion project. Vĩnh Tân II (2×622 MW, COD 2014–15) and Vĩnh Tân IV/IV MR (3×600 MW, COD 2017–19) are EVNGENCO3-operated. Vĩnh Tân I (2×600 MW BOT, COD 2018–19) is operated by COPI (China). Vĩnh Tân III (~1,980 MW) was cancelled after CLP withdrew from coal.

### Thái Bình 2
1,200 MW (2×600 MW) supercritical coal, developed by PVN/PV Power. Massive delays and anti-corruption investigations. Unit 1 COD mid-2022, Unit 2 early 2023.

### Long Phú 1 (Sóc Trăng)
1,200 MW supercritical coal. PetroVietnam is developer. Originally contracted to Power Machines (Russia)/PTSC in 2014; suspended 2019 due to US sanctions. In January 2025 a launching ceremony was held. Target: Unit 1 COD Q2/2027, Unit 2 Q3/2027. Financing remains unresolved.

### Ô Môn LNG Complex (Cần Thơ)
Ô Môn I (660 MW CCGT, EVNGENCO2) operates on depleting Cửu Long gas. Ô Môn II–IV (~3,150 MW total) depend on Block B (Lô B) gas; none under construction.

### Nhơn Trạch 3 & 4 (Đồng Nai)
Vietnam's first LNG-to-power project. PV Power, 2×812 MW CCGT (Mitsubishi M701JAC). LNG via Thị Vải terminal. COD 2025.

### Hiệp Phước LNG (HCM City)
Phase 1: 1,200 MW, Siemens SGT5-4000F, ~US$892M, BOO model. Developer: Hiệp Phước Power Co. (Hải Linh Group). EVNGENCO3 signed a 12-year O&M contract for Hiep Phuoc Power Plant. PPA signed and construction commenced March 2026. Phase 2: 1,500 MW planned. Existing plant: 375 MW (1998, HFO/gas dual-fuel) to be retired.

### Phú Mỹ 2.2 BOT (BR-VT)
~440 MW CCGT gas plant. EVN officially received Phu My 2.2 BOT Power Plant in February 2025, following BOT contract expiry. Now under EVNGENCO3 management.

### Vân Phong 1 BOT (Khánh Hòa)
1,320 MW net / 1,432 MW gross ultra-supercritical coal. Sumitomo subsidiary Van Phong Power Co. COD January 24, 2024. Investment ~US$2.58B. Vietnam's newest coal plant.

### Sơn Mỹ LNG (Bình Thuận)
Two plants (~2,250 MW each) tied to Sơn Mỹ LNG terminal (AES/PVGas consortium). PDP8 lists for 2027–2030 but minimal progress.
