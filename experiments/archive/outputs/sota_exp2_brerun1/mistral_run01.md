## **1. Sector Overview**

### **Electricity Mix (2024–2025)**
- **Total installed capacity (2024):** 82,387 MW (13,65).
- **Thermal power share (2024):** 45–50% of total capacity, ~63% of electricity output (13,19,36,52,63,79).
- **Breakdown (2024):**
  - **Coal:** 26,757 MW (32.5% of capacity, ~50% of generation).
  - **Gas (domestic + LNG):** 8,653 MW (10.5% of capacity, ~6–10% of generation).
  - **Hydropower:** 23,664 MW (28.7%).
  - **Renewables (solar, wind):** 21,447 MW (26%).
  - **Imports/other:** 1,866 MW (2.3%) (13,19,36,54,65).

### **Policy Framework**
- **Power Development Plan VIII (PDP8, revised 2025):** Sets the roadmap for 2021–2030, with a vision to 2050.
  - **No new coal plants** after 2025; only ongoing projects to be completed (21,58,72).
  - **Gas and LNG expansion:** 13.5 GW by 2025, 28–33 GW by 2030 (64,72).
  - **LNG imports:** 8 MMTPA by 2030, 15 MMTPA post-2030 (21,64).
  - **Thermal power by 2030:** 30,127 MW coal, 22,400 MW LNG, 14,930 MW domestic gas (74,96).
  - **Retirement/conversion:** Coal plants to be retired or converted to biomass/ammonia by 2050 (83,85).

### **Key Institutional Actors**
- **Ministry of Industry and Trade (MOIT):** Policy, planning, and regulation.
- **Vietnam Electricity (EVN):** State-owned utility, dominant in generation, transmission, and distribution.
- **Petrovietnam (PVN):** Gas production and LNG imports.
- **Vinacomin:** Coal mining and supply.
- **Independent Power Producers (IPPs):** Increasing role in gas and LNG projects (2,52,74,96).

### **Current Challenges**
- **Coal dependence:** Despite ESG pressures, coal remains critical for grid stability, especially in the north (19,22,52,63,83).
- **Gas supply constraints:** Domestic gas fields depleting; LNG infrastructure delays (60,61,95).
- **Grid bottlenecks:** Transmission upgrades ongoing to integrate renewables and reduce curtailment (20,37,68).
- **Regulatory uncertainty:** Delays in PPAs for LNG projects; retroactive tariff cuts for renewables (14,61,71).

## **2. Per-Plant Narratives**

### **Major Coal Plants**
- **Vũng Áng-1 & -2 (Hà Tĩnh):** 2,400 MW total. Vũng Áng-1 operational since 2014–2015; Vũng Áng-2 under construction, with Unit 1 commissioned Q1 2024 and Unit 2 expected Q2 2025. Tax exemption proposed for foreign owners (50,78,81).
- **Vân Phong-1 (Khánh Hòa):** 1,320 MW. Operational since 2023–2024 but not at full capacity as of July 2025 (53,87).
- **Duyên Hải 1 & 3 (Trà Vinh):** 3,600 MW total. Both operational since 2015–2021 (85,88).
- **Mông Dương 1 & 2 (Quảng Ninh):** 2,160 MW total. Operational since 2015 (50,85).
- **Phả Lại 1 (Hải Dương):** 440 MW. Retired in 2025 per Decision 500/QD-TTg (82,85).

### **Major Gas/LNG Plants**
- **Nhơn Trạch 3 & 4 (Đồng Nai):** 1,500 MW (LNG). Vietnam’s first LNG-to-power plants, expected COD Q2/Q3 2025. Prime Minister urged completion by June 2025 (60,70,95,99).
- **Long Sơn LNG (Bà Rịa-Vũng Tàu):** 3,200 MW (LNG). EVN + Mitsubishi/GE. First phase (1,200–1,500 MW) expected 2025–2026 (93).
- **O Mon II, III, IV (Cần Thơ):** 2,250 MW (domestic gas). Part of Block B–O Mon Gas-to-Power Chain. O Mon II expected COD 2025; O Mon IV EPC contract signed 2025 (77,96).
- **Dung Quất II (Quảng Ngãi):** 750 MW (LNG). Under construction; expected COD 2024 (92).

### **Cancelled/Retired Plants**
- **Mông Dương 3, Quảng Ninh 2, Quảng Trạch 2, Sông Hồng:** Cancelled per PDP8 (83,85).
- **Phả Lại 1:** Retired in 2025 (82,85).
