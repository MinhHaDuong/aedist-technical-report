# VIETNAM THERMAL POWER GENERATION INVENTORY (>30 MWe)

**Report date**: 23 May 2026 | **Data vintage**: 2023–2025 | **Governing plans**: Decision 500/QĐ-TTg (May 2023) superseded by Decision 768/QĐ-TTg (Apr 2025)

---

## 1. Sector Overview

PDP8 projects that Vietnam's coal capacity will peak at 30.1 GW, compared to the 27.2 GW in operation as of June 2024. On 15 April 2025, Decision 768/QĐ-TTg approved the Revised PDP8, superseding Decision 500/QĐ-TTg dated 15 May 2023. Overall power generation capacity has been increased by approximately 22%–57% by 2030 and 46%–58% by 2050 compared to the original PDP8.

Coal plants under construction per the Revised PDP8 are limited to five projects: Na Duong II, An Khanh–Bac Giang, Vung Ang 2, Quang Trach I, Long Phu 1. New proposals have dwindled across Southeast Asia, driven by phaseout pledges in Indonesia and Malaysia, a moratorium on coal plant permitting in the Philippines, and the development of just transition planning in Vietnam.

New LNG-to-power projects in the Revised PDP8 include LNG Hai Phong Phase I (1,600 MW) and LNG Hiep Phuoc Phase II (1,500 MW) for 2025–2030. Cong Thanh, originally planned as a coal-fired power plant, was approved for conversion to LNG.

**Key actors**: EVN (Genco 1/2/3) operates state-owned coal and gas; PVN/PV Power manages gas-fired CCGT and Long Phú 1/Thái Bình 2 coal; Vinacomin (TKV) runs mine-mouth coal in the northeast. BOT investors include One Energy Asia (ex-AES, Mông Dương 2), Sumitomo (Vân Phong 1), Marubeni/KEPCO (Nghi Sơn 2), CSEC (Vĩnh Tân 1), Janakuasa (Duyên Hải 2), Jaks/China Energy (Hải Dương), and Mitsubishi/KEPCO (Vũng Áng 2).

## 2. Plant Narratives (Major/Disputed Assets)

**Vĩnh Tân Complex** (Bình Thuận, ~4,244 MW operating). Four co-located plants: VT1 (1,200 MW, BOT CSEC, supercritical, COD 2019), VT2 (1,244 MW, EVN/Genco 3, subcritical, COD 2015), VT4 (1,200 MW, EVN/Genco 3, supercritical, COD 2019–20), VT4 MR (600 MW, Genco 3, 2020). VT3 cancelled under PDP8.

**Long Phú 1** (Sóc Trăng, 1,200 MW). As of January 2023, the Global Coal Plant Tracker estimated Vietnam had 24.7 GW in coal capacity, with a further 6.1 GW under construction. Long Phú 1 was 77.56% complete when suspended after Power Machines (Russia) was sanctioned in 2018. Boiler installation began April 2025; PVN targets Unit 1 COD Q3 2027, Unit 2 Q4 2027. **Confidence: MEDIUM** — restart confirmed but COD aspirational.

**Ô Môn Complex** (Cần Thơ). Ô Môn I (660 MW) operates on oil/gas. Ô Môn II–IV (3×1,050 MW) depend on the long-delayed Lô B–Ô Môn gas pipeline, classified as a national priority project. **Confidence: LOW** for II–IV — no FID on gas supply chain.

**Nhơn Trạch 3&4** (Đồng Nai, 1,624 MW). Vietnam's first LNG-to-power CCGT, developed by PV Power, fed by Thị Vải LNG terminal. COD 2024–2025. **Confidence: HIGH**.
