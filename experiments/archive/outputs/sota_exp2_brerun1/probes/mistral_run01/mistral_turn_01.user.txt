## INSTRUCTIONS FOR THERMAL GENERATION ASSET INVENTORY (VIETNAM)

### SCOPE
Produce a complete, primary-sourced inventory of Vietnam's thermal generation assets (> 30 MWe), including operational, under construction, approved, planned, suspended, cancelled, and retired plants. Cover all fuels (coal, domestic gas, imported LNG) and technologies (Subcritical, Supercritical, USC, CCGT, OCGT). Include grid-connected, micro-grid, off-grid, and captive plants.

### SEARCH STRATEGY
1. **Phase 1 (Discovery)**:
   - Use web_search to identify official government sources (e.g., Ministry of Industry and Trade, Vietnam Electricity, General Department of Energy), regulator databases, and operator filings.
   - Search for recent industry reports from the IEA, World Bank, Global Energy Monitor, and reputable trade press (e.g., Reuters, S&P Global, Vietnam News).
   - Compile a list of all known plants from these sources, including those with incomplete data.

2. **Phase 2 (Verification)**:
   - For each plant, retrieve at least two independent, admissible sources. Prioritize primary sources (e.g., government approvals, operator press releases, regulator filings).
   - If only one source is available, mark confidence as MEDIUM or LOW and explain in Notes.
   - Trace all secondary sources to their original primary source. If the original source cannot be found, record 'Source = not found' and mark confidence as LOW.

3. **Phase 3 (Reconciliation)**:
   - Compare sources for consistency. Investigate and resolve discrepancies in capacity, status, COD, fuel, or owner.
   - Document all conflicts and resolutions in the Notes field. If unresolved, follow the higher-tier source and explain your reasoning.

### TABLE SCHEMA
Produce a structured table with the following columns:
- **Name (Vietnamese)**: Original Vietnamese name of the plant.
- **Name (English)**: English translation or transliteration.
- **Province**: Province where the plant is located.
- **Fuel**: Coal / Domestic gas / Imported LNG.
- **Technology**: Subcritical / Supercritical / USC for coal; CCGT / OCGT for gas.
- **Units × MW**: Number of units and capacity per unit (e.g., 2 × 600 MWe).
- **Total MWe**: Total nameplate electrical capacity (gross/net unspecified unless noted).
- **Status**: Operational / Under construction / Approved / Planned / Suspended / Cancelled / Retired.
- **Status as-of-date**: Date of the most recent admissible source attesting the status.
- **COD**: Commercial Operation Date (if applicable).
- **Owner/Developer**: Owner or developer of the plant.
- **Confidence**: HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW (see confidence rules below).
- **Source 1**: Primary or highest-tier source.
- **Source 2**: Secondary or additional source (if available).
- **Notes**: Conflicts, resolutions, assumptions, or additional context.

### CONFIDENCE RULES
- **HIGH**: >=2 independent, concordant sources, at least one primary.
- **MEDIUM**: 1 primary source, OR a sourced aggregator citing a verifiable primary.
- **LOW**: Secondary only, inferred, unresolved conflict, or 'Source = not found'.

### STOPPING CRITERIA
- **Completeness**: All known plants (> 30 MWe) are included, even if some attributes are missing or confidence is LOW.
- **Provenance**: Every claim is supported by at least one admissible source, or explicitly marked as 'Source = not found'.
- **Budget**: Stop if either the 50,000-token or $3.00 budget cap reaches 20% of its initial value.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Deliver a single Markdown document with:
1. **Sector Overview**: Electricity mix, policy framework, key institutional actors, current challenges.
2. **Per-Plant Narratives**: Concise sourced narratives for major, disputed, or ambiguous assets. Compact Notes for straightforward assets.
3. **Structured Power-Plants Table**: As defined above.
4. **Statistical Summary Tables**:
   - Capacity by fuel × status.
   - Top 15 provinces by capacity.
   - Timeline of additions by period and fuel.
   - Data-quality summary by confidence level and fuel.
5. **Annotated Bibliography**: Full citation, URL (if available), original-language title + English translation, and summary annotation for every source cited.

### BUDGET DISCIPLINE
- Use early turns for search, decomposition, and uncertainty surfacing.
- Prioritize recall, provenance, and uncertainty notes over narrative elaboration if budget becomes tight.
- Compress overview, narratives, and bibliography annotations if necessary.

### FINAL VERIFICATION
After producing the report, you will receive one verify-and-polish pass. Use this to:
- Reconcile any remaining conflicts.
- Fill gaps in provenance.
- Ensure all totals and summaries are internally consistent.
- Trim output to fit budget if needed.
