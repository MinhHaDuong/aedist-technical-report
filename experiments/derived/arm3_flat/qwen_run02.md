# Vietnam Thermal Generation Asset Inventory (>30 MWe)

## 1. Sector Overview

### Electricity Mix and Thermal Dominance
As of the transition from PDP7 (Revised) to PDP8 (approved May 2023), thermal generation remains the backbone of Vietnam’s power system, accounting for approximately 55–60% of installed capacity and over 65% of generation output. The mix has shifted structurally: domestic gas is plateauing due to declining offshore fields; imported coal surged between 2015–2020 but faces financing headwinds post-2021; and LNG is now designated as the primary transitional baseload fuel under PDP8. Oil-fired generation has been largely relegated to peaking or reserve status, with most units >30 MW either retired, converted, or operating only during supply emergencies.

### Policy Framework
*   **PDP7 Revised (Decision 428/QĐ-TTg, 2016):** Established the coal build-out that delivered most current operating assets. Many projects listed here were originally scheduled for COD 2018–2022 but experienced delays.
*   **PDP8 (Decision 500/QĐ-TTg, May 2023):** Marks a decisive pivot. No new greenfield coal plants are approved beyond those already under construction. Existing delayed coal projects face cancellation or conversion review. LNG is prioritized (~22 GW planned by 2030). Domestic gas projects are tied to upstream field development (Block B, Blue Whale/Cá Voi Xanh).
*   **Just Energy Transition Partnership (JETP):** Announced Dec 2022, provides $15.5B in financing contingent on accelerating renewables and capping coal capacity at ~30.2 GW, directly impacting the "future" pipeline in this inventory.

### Key Institutional Actors
*   **EVN (Vietnam Electricity):** State utility; owns/operates ~40% of thermal fleet via three GENCOs. Primary investor in domestic gas and some coal.
*   **PVN (PetroVietnam):** State oil & gas group; key developer of gas-fired plants (Nhon Trach, O Mon, Mien Trung) and stranded coal projects (Thai Binh 2, Long Phu 1).
*   **TKV (Vinacomin):** State mining group; operates domestic-coal plants (Na Duong, Cam Pha) and struggling projects (Quynh Lap).
*   **BOT/IPP Developers:** Foreign consortia (e.g., AES, Marubeni, JERA, Sumitomo, Janakuasa) own significant high-efficiency coal and future LNG capacity.
*   **MOIT (Ministry of Industry and Trade):** Regulator responsible for planning approvals and progress reporting (Report 32, Report 58).

### Current Challenges
1.  **Stranded/Delayed Coal Pipeline:** Multiple large BOT coal projects (Nam Dinh I, Vung Ang II, Song Hau II, Vinh Tan III) are years behind schedule due to financing withdrawal, EPC disputes, or permitting issues. PDP8 explicitly flags these for review.
2.  **Gas Supply Synchronization:** Gas plant CODs (O Mon III/IV, Dung Quat, Mien Trung) are repeatedly delayed awaiting upstream Block B and Blue Whale first gas, now expected 2026–2027+.
3.  **LNG Uncertainty:** While PDP8 lists ~15 LNG projects for 2021–2030, none have reached FID as of mid-2024. Pricing mechanisms and infrastructure remain unresolved.
4.  **Data Opacity:** Capacity figures vary across EVN ARs, PDP annexes, and MOIT reports due to differing definitions (nameplate vs. dependable), unit-level phasing, and unreported retirements. Confidence qualifications are applied throughout this inventory.

## 2. Per-Plant Narratives (Selected Key Assets)

### Operating Plants

**Nhiệt điện Phả Lại (Pha Lai Thermal Power Plant)**
*History:* Located in Hai Duong province, Phả Lại was Vietnam’s largest coal plant for decades. Unit 1 (440 MW, subcritical) commissioned 1986–1989 with Soviet technology; Unit 2 (600 MW, subcritical) commissioned 2002 with Japanese ODA. Both units use domestic coal blend. Ownership transferred to GENCO2 (EVN subsidiary).
*Issues:* Aging infrastructure; Unit 1 frequently derated. Listed at 440+600 MW consistently across EVN AR 2011, 2017, 2018. PDP8 does not list expansion; plant approaching end-of-life consideration.
*Confidence:* HIGH for capacity and owner; MEDIUM for current availability factor.

**Nhiệt điện Vĩnh Tân 2 (Vinh Tan 2 Thermal Power Plant)**
*History:* 2×622 MW supercritical units in Binh Thuan, developed by EVN (GENCO3). COD 2015 (Unit 1) and 2015 (Unit 2). First major imported-coal plant in southern Vietnam.
*Issues:* Significant environmental protests in 2014–2015 over coal dust and fly ash management led to operational restrictions and mandatory port upgrades. Capacity reported as 1,244 MW (EVN AR 2017/2018) reflecting net dependable output vs. 1,200 MW nameplate.
*Confidence:* HIGH.

**Nhiệt điện Duyên Hải 3 & 3 Mở Rộng (Duyen Hai 3 & Extension)**
*History:* Tra Vinh province. DH3: 2×622 MW SC, EVN-owned, COD 2016–2017. DH3 Extension: 1×660 MW SC, COD 2019. Combined complex is critical for southern grid stability.
*Issues:* DH3 completed 6 months ahead of PDP7A schedule (Report 32). Extension met 2019 target. Uses imported coal via dedicated deep-water port.
*Confidence:* HIGH.

**TBKHH Phú Mỹ Complex (Phu My Gas Turbine Complex)**
*History:* Ba Ria-Vung Tau. Vietnam’s largest gas power hub comprising multiple units: PM1 (1,108 MW), PM2.1 (945 MW), PM4 (477 MW), plus BOT plants PM2.2 (740 MW) and PM3 (740 MW). Commissioned 2001–2004. Originally designed for domestic Nam Con Son gas.
*Issues:* Capacity figures fluctuate across sources (EVN AR 2011 vs. 2018) due to degradation and refurbishment. Fuel transition ongoing as domestic gas declines; partial LNG blending anticipated. PM2.2 and PM3 are foreign-owned BOTs with separate PPAs.
*Confidence:* HIGH for aggregate; MEDIUM for individual unit current output.

**Nhiệt điện Thái Bình 2 (Thai Binh 2 Thermal Power Plant)**
*History:* 2×600 MW SC in Thai Binh province. PVN-owned. Originally planned COD 2017–2018 per PDP7A.
*Issues:* Severely delayed due to PVN financial crisis and EPC contractor (PVC) mismanagement. Report 32 and Report 58 both flag "undetermined completion" and "risk of further delay." Eventually achieved commercial operation Unit 1 in late 2023, Unit 2 in early 2024—approximately 6 years behind schedule. Uses domestic coal blend.
*Confidence:* HIGH for eventual COD; status updated to Operating based on 2024 commissioning reports.

### Under Construction / Delayed

**Nhiệt điện Vân Phong 1 (Van Phong 1 Thermal Power Plant)**
*History:* 2×660 MW USC in Khanh Hoa. BOT by Sumitomo/Hanoi Power. PPA signed. PDP8 Annex II Table 2 confirms under construction with 1,432 MW capacity (adjusted from original 1,320 MW).
*Issues:* Delayed ~1 year vs. PDP7A (Report 58). Environmental permitting challenges due to proximity to Van Phong Bay tourism zone. Now proceeding under PDP8 “under construction” list.
*Confidence:* HIGH.

**Nhiệt điện Vũng Áng 2 (Vung Ang 2 Thermal Power Plant)**
*History:* 2×600 MW SC in Ha Tinh. BOT by VAPCO (Vietnamese consortium). Planned COD 2021–2022 per PDP7A.
*Issues:* Delayed ~2–3 years (Report 32/58). Financing difficulties. PDP8 Annex II Table 2 retains it as “under construction” at 1,330 MW (capacity adjusted upward). Status uncertain; may require restructuring.
*Confidence:* MEDIUM. Capacity revised in PDP8; COD unclear.

**Nhiệt điện Nam Định 1 (Nam Dinh 1 Thermal Power Plant)**
*History:* 2×600 MW SC. BOT by Taekwang/ACWA. Planned COD 2021–2022.
*Issues:* Delayed 3+ years (Report 32/58). ACWA reportedly withdrew; project stalled. PDP8 Annex II Table 3 explicitly lists as “delayed, shareholder/financing difficulties.” Future highly uncertain.
*Confidence:* LOW for COD. HIGH for inclusion in troubled pipeline.

**TBKHH Ô Môn III & IV (O Mon III & IV Gas Turbine Plants)**
*History:* Can Tho. Each 1×750 MW CCGT. EVN-owned. Designed for Block B gas.
*Issues:* Repeatedly delayed in lockstep with Block B upstream. PDP7A targeted 2020–2021; Report 32 shows slippage to 2025 and 2023 respectively; Report 58 confirms 3–5 year delays. PDP8 Annex II Table 5 retains both at 1,050 MW each (upgraded from 750 MW) using Block B gas. COD contingent on first gas, now estimated 2026–2027.
*Confidence:* MEDIUM. Capacity upgraded in PDP8; COD fluid.

### Future / Planned (PDP8)

**LNG Sơn Mỹ I & II (Son My I & II LNG Power Plants)**
*History:* Binh Thuan. Son My I: 2,250 MW BOT (AES/Sojitz/Pacific). Son My II: 2,250 MW PVN. Both carried forward from PDP7A.
*Issues:* PDP8 Annex II Table 1 confirms both for 2021–2030 window. However, no FID reached. AES restructured global LNG portfolio; PVN awaiting government pricing framework. Study E-542 PL9.1 shows site potential validated.
*Confidence:* LOW for 2030 COD. HIGH for policy inclusion.

**LNG Bạc Liêu (Bac Lieu LNG Power Plant)**
*History:* 3,200 MW in Bac Lieu. Originally proposed as coal; converted to LNG. PDP8 Annex II Table 1 includes for 2021–2030.
*Issues:* Pre-FS stage per Study E-542. No developer confirmed. Massive scale requires unprecedented LNG infrastructure. Highly aspirational timeline.
*Confidence:* LOW. Included per PDP8 mandate but pre-FID.

**Nhiệt điện Quảng Trạch I (Quang Trach 1 Thermal Power Plant)**
*History:* 2×600 MW SC in Quang Binh. Transferred from PVN to EVN. PDP8 Annex II Table 2 lists as under construction at 1,403 MW (adjusted). EVN awarded EPC contract.
*Issues:* Delayed 2.5 years due to ownership transfer (Report 32). Now actively under construction. Expected COD 2025–2026.
*Confidence:* HIGH. Active construction confirmed.
