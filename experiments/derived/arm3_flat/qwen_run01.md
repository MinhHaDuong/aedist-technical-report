# Vietnam Thermal Generation Asset Inventory (>30 MWe)

## 1. Sector Overview

### Electricity Mix and Thermal Dominance
As of the latest consolidated data (2023-2024), thermal generation remains the backbone of Vietnam’s power system, accounting for approximately 55-60% of installed capacity and over 70% of annual generation. The mix has shifted significantly from domestic gas and hydro dominance in the early 2010s to imported coal and, increasingly, LNG. Domestic gas production is plateauing/declining, necessitating a strategic pivot to imported LNG for new gas-fired capacity. Coal capacity expanded rapidly between 2015–2020 but faces financing moratoriums and policy restrictions under PDP8 (Decision 500/QĐ-TTg).

### Policy Framework
*   **PDP7 Revised (2016):** The previous master plan that drove the last decade of coal expansion. Many projects listed here were originally scheduled for COD 2016–2020 but experienced significant delays.
*   **PDP8 (Decision 500/QĐ-TTg, May 2023):** The current governing framework. Key shifts include:
    *   No new coal plants after 2030; existing pipeline restricted to projects already under construction or with secured financing.
    *   Massive scale-up of LNG-to-power (23.7 GW planned by 2030), though many face FID uncertainty.
    *   Prioritization of domestic gas (Block B, Blue Whale) over imported fuel where feasible.
    *   Explicit "reserve" status for certain thermal projects contingent on renewable integration failures.

### Key Institutional Actors
*   **EVN (Vietnam Electricity):** State utility; owns/operates ~40% of thermal fleet via GENCOs 1, 2, 3. Primary off-taker.
*   **PVN (Petrovietnam):** Major developer of gas and some coal assets (e.g., Long Phu, Song Hau, Nhon Trach).
*   **TKV (Vinacomin):** Developer of domestic-coal plants (Na Duong, Cam Pha, Hai Phong III).
*   **BOT/IPP Developers:** Foreign consortia (e.g., JERA, Sumitomo, AES, Janakuasa, Taekwang) developing major export-coal and LNG projects. Critical for capital mobilization outside state balance sheet.
*   **MOIT (Ministry of Industry and Trade):** Regulatory authority overseeing planning and approval.

### Current Challenges
1.  **Project Delays:** Chronic slippage in BOT and PVN-led coal/gas projects (e.g., Long Phu I, Thai Binh II, Nam Dinh I) due to financing, EPC contractor issues, and regulatory changes. Report 32/58 annexes document delays of 3–8 years across the portfolio.
2.  **Fuel Transition Risk:** LNG projects approved in PDP8 lack finalized gas supply agreements and tariff frameworks. High exposure to global LNG price volatility.
3.  **Grid Integration:** Thermal plants increasingly required to provide flexibility rather than baseload, challenging older subcritical units designed for continuous operation.
4.  **Environmental Compliance:** Older domestic-coal and oil plants face retrofit requirements or retirement mandates (e.g., Uong Bi I, Ninh Binh).

## 2. Per-Plant Narratives (Selected Key Assets)

### Operating Assets

**Phả Lại I & II (Hải Dương)**
*History:* Phả Lại I (440 MW, 6×55MW + 1×110MW) commissioned 1986–2002; Phả Lại II (600 MW, 2×300MW) commissioned 2002. Originally Soviet/Russian technology using domestic coal.
*Issues:* Phả Lại I is aging and inefficient; frequently cited for potential retirement or conversion. Both plants now blend imported coal. Ownership transferred to GENCO2. Capacity confirmed at 1,040 MW total in EVN AR 2017/2018.
*Confidence:* HIGH (Multiple EVN AR confirmations).

**Vĩnh Tân Complex (Bình Thuận)**
*History:* Four distinct plants. VT1 (2×600MW BOT, China Southern/CHDI/Vinacomin) COD 2018. VT2 (2×622MW, EVN GENCO3) COD 2015. VT3 (3×660MW BOT, VTEC) delayed repeatedly; currently in negotiation/restructuring. VT4 (2×600MW, EVN) COD 2018; VT4 Extension (600MW) COD 2019.
*Issues:* Environmental protests in 2014–2015 forced design changes (enclosed coal conveyors). VT3 remains the most significant unresolved BOT coal project.
*Confidence:* HIGH for VT1/2/4; MEDIUM for VT3 status.

**Nhơn Trạch Complex (Đồng Nai)**
*History:* NT1 (465MW OCGT, PV Power) COD 2009. NT2 (750MW CCGT, PV Power) COD 2011. NT3&4 (2×880MW CCGT LNG, PV Power) under construction as of PDP8; originally planned for domestic gas, converted to LNG.
*Issues:* NT3&4 represent Vietnam’s first major LNG-to-power test case. Delays tied to LNG terminal infrastructure and PPA negotiations. Original PDP7A schedule was 2020–2021; now targeting 2024–2025.
*Confidence:* HIGH for NT1/2; MEDIUM for NT3&4 COD.

**Formosa Hà Tĩnh Steel Plant Power Station (Hà Tĩnh)**
*History:* Industrial captive plant integrated with steel mill. Multiple phases: Phase 1 (150MW coal) COD 2016; subsequent units using blast furnace gas and coal. Total capacity reached ~650MW+ by 2020.
*Issues:* Classified as industrial CHP/captive; not always reported in national grid statistics. Fuel mix includes metallurgical byproducts. PDP8 Annex II Table 4 lists "Formosa HT2" (650MW) as ongoing cogeneration.
*Confidence:* MEDIUM (Capacity varies by reporting boundary; primary source is PDP7A Annex I + PDP8 Annex II Table 4).

### Under Construction / Delayed Assets

**Long Phú I (Sóc Trăng)**
*History:* 2×600MW supercritical coal, PVN investor. Originally scheduled COD 2018–2019. Russian EPC contractor (Power Machines) sanctioned; project stalled at ~78% completion.
*Issues:* US sanctions on Russian contractor prevented equipment delivery. PVN sought replacement contractors. Report 32 notes "chưa xác định tiến độ hoàn thành" (completion timeline undetermined). PDP8 retains it as priority.
*Confidence:* MEDIUM (Physical progress high, but COD date highly uncertain).

**Nam Định I (Nam Định)**
*History:* 2×600MW BOT coal, Taekwang (Korea) + ACWA Power (Saudi Arabia). Planned COD 2021–2022 per PDP7A.
*Issues:* Financial close difficulties; ACWA withdrew/restructured. Listed in PDP8 Annex II Table 3 as "delayed, shareholder/financing difficulties." Report 32 estimates 3-year delay minimum.
*Confidence:* LOW-MEDIUM (Active but troubled; no firm COD).

**Sơn Mỹ I & II LNG (Bình Thuận)**
*History:* Combined 4,500MW LNG CCGT complex. Sơn Mỹ II (2,250MW, AES/PVN) and Sơn Mỹ I (2,250MW, EDF/Sojitz/Pacific). Originally domestic gas, converted to LNG.
*Issues:* Among the largest LNG projects globally. FEED completed but FID pending. PDP8 schedules them for 2026–2030 window. Study E-542 notes they are "potential" sites with capacity confirmation but no binding offtake.
*Confidence:* MEDIUM (Approved in PDP8, but pre-FID; high cancellation/restructuring risk).

### Cancelled / Suspended Assets

**Kiên Lương Coal Complex (Kiên Giang)**
*History:* Originally planned as 3,600MW coal center. License revoked by Prime Minister circa 2018–2019 due to environmental concerns and Mekong Delta protection policy.
*Status:* Replaced by LNG proposals in PDP8 study phase, but no Kiên Lương LNG appears in PDP8 Annex II Table 1 approved list.
*Confidence:* HIGH (Revocation documented in Study E-542 PL9.2 notes).

**Ninh Bình Thermal (Ninh Bình)**
*History:* 100MW domestic coal, commissioned 1970s. One of Vietnam’s oldest thermal plants.
*Status:* Scheduled for retirement in PDP7 Annex II (2021 entry shows "-100MW"). Likely decommissioned or in cold reserve by 2023. Retained in inventory as historical asset with retirement flag.
*Confidence:* HIGH (Retirement planned since PDP7 original).
