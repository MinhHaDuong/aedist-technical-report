## Per-asset / per-project explanatory notes

**Uông Bí old unit — row confidence MEDIUM.** The 105 MW old Uông Bí unit is historically important because it is one of the oldest coal assets in the EVN fleet. EVN’s 2011 annual-report table listed it as installed capacity, while Revised PDP7 later scheduled “NĐ Uông Bí ngừng phát điện” at 105 MW. I therefore record it as retired, but because the evidence pack does not include a decommissioning notice or EVN post-retirement asset register, the retirement implementation date is not independently confirmed.

**Na Dương I / Cao Ngạn / Sơn Động — row confidence MEDIUM.** These Vinacomin/TKV mine-mouth plants appear in EVN’s 2011 system table, but the provided later EVN GENCO rosters do not comprehensively cover non-EVN plants. Their existence and capacity are therefore supported, while current operating status is not freshly confirmed in the evidence pack.

**Formosa Hà Tĩnh — row confidence HIGH for HT2 existence, MEDIUM for fuel categorization.** Formosa’s captive power units span coal and steel-plant by-product gases. PDP7A separates coal-fired 150 MW units and blast-furnace-gas 100 MW units; PDP8 groups “Formosa HT2” as a 650 MW industrial cogeneration/by-product source. The inventory uses Coal as the required closest category, but notes that some capacity may use blast-furnace gas rather than coal.

**Thái Bình II — row confidence MEDIUM.** Reports 32/58 show severe delay for the 2×600 MW PVN project. The final PDP8 evidence pack does not list it as a future under-construction or troubled coal project, which supports—but does not directly prove—operating status by 2023. A commissioning certificate or PVN operating report should be consulted to raise confidence to HIGH.

**Nghi Sơn II, Hải Dương, Duyên Hải II, Sông Hậu I — row confidence MEDIUM for operating status.** Each project was at late construction or scheduled commissioning in the 2020–2022 window in the provided implementation reports or draft study, and none appears in PDP8’s 2023 lists of coal plants still under construction or delayed. I therefore mark them Operating, but the final table lacks a direct post-COD operator or regulator record, so confidence is capped.

**Vũng Áng II, Quảng Trạch I, Vân Phong I, Long Phú I — row confidence HIGH.** These are explicitly listed in PDP8 Annex II Table 2 as coal plants under construction, with current-policy capacities of 1,330 MW, 1,403 MW, 1,432 MW and 1,200 MW respectively. Where older PDP7A or Report 58 values differ, the final PDP8 capacity is chosen because it is the most recent official planning instrument in the evidence pack.

**Vĩnh Tân III, Nam Định I, Quảng Trị I, Sông Hậu II — row confidence HIGH for delayed/shelved status.** PDP8 Annex II Table 3 explicitly groups these projects as delayed and facing finance/shareholder issues. I map that condition to the GEM-style “Shelved” status because construction is not evidenced and progress is blocked, but they are not formally cancelled in the cited PDP8 text.

**Quảng Trạch II — row confidence HIGH for LNG conversion.** Older PDP7/PDP7A sources list Quảng Trạch II as coal. PDP8 Annex II Table 1 lists “LNG Quảng Trạch II” at 1,500 MW and states that Government leadership agreed to convert it to LNG via Notice 54/TB-VPCP dated 2022-02-25. The table therefore records the current asset as Imported LNG and treats the coal version as superseded within the same row.

**Quỳnh Lập I/II and Quỳnh Lập/Nghi Sơn LNG — row confidence MEDIUM/HIGH depending on row.** Older coal Quỳnh Lập projects remained uncertain in Report 58 and E-542. Final PDP8 does not retain them as active coal projects but instead includes an LNG Quỳnh Lập/Nghi Sơn 1,500 MW location option. I keep the coal rows as Shelved and the LNG row as Announced because the current policy has not fixed the exact location.

**Kiên Lương coal complex — row confidence MEDIUM for cancellation.** PDP7 had multiple Kiên Lương coal stages. E-542 states that the Kiên Lương thermal power center had an investment license revoked. This supports cancellation/supersession of the complex, although a formal revocation decision number is not included in the evidence pack.

**Long An coal-to-LNG transition — row confidence HIGH for LNG PDP8 entries, LOW/MEDIUM for older coal rows.** PDP7A and implementation reports included Long An I/II as coal projects, while E-542 noted conversion to LNG and final PDP8 lists LNG Long An I and II. The inventory therefore records LNG Long An I/II as the current assets and does not retain separate active coal Long An rows.

**Ô Môn complex — row confidence HIGH for PDP8 capacity and fuel, MEDIUM for technology detail.** PDP8 lists Ô Môn I, II, III and IV as domestic-gas plants using Lô B gas, with Ô Môn I noted as an existing plant to be converted. Older EVN tables list Ô Môn I under oil-fired capacity. Technology labels are therefore best-effort: Ô Môn II-IV are CCGT, while Ô Môn I’s exact post-conversion configuration is ambiguous in the evidence pack.

**Dung Quất / Miền Trung gas projects — row confidence HIGH for inclusion, MEDIUM for province allocation.** PDP8 lists Dung Quất I-III and Miền Trung I-II domestic-gas plants, but the table does not provide provinces. Dung Quất is assigned to Quảng Ngãi; Miền Trung I-II are left as Quảng Ngãi/Quảng Trị uncertain because older planning used “Quảng Trị hoặc Quảng Ngãi” and later gas-field routing was unsettled in the evidence pack.

**Sơn Mỹ I/II — row confidence HIGH for capacity and PDP8 status.** Both are retained in PDP8 as 2,250 MW LNG plants. Reports 32/58 show major schedule slippage and BOT/developer uncertainty, so the status is marked Permitted rather than Construction.

**Draft-study LNG option rows — row confidence LOW.** LNG Hải Phòng, Quảng Ninh 2-4, Hà Tĩnh/Vũng Áng, Chân Mây, Phong Điền, Quảng Nam, Bình Định, Vân Phong/Mỹ Giang, Mũi Kê Gà, Phú Mỹ 3.1, Bà Rịa 2, Cái Mép H, Tân Phước, Bến Tre, Sông Đốc and Tân Thuận-Đầm Dơi are included because the scope asks for future/shelved/cancelled projects and E-542 records them as potential sites. They should not be read as committed projects; many are only option-space candidates or fallback locations in PDP8.

Across the table, operating coal assets supported by EVN annual reports and PDP7/PDP7A/implementation annexes total roughly the known Vietnamese coal fleet order of magnitude, but I have not provided a statistical reconciliation table because the requested output forbids additional tables. Where capacities differ between older PDP7 schedules and later EVN/PDP8 values, I generally use the later operator or final PDP8 value and explain the discrepancy in the Notes field.
