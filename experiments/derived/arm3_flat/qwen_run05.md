### Explanatory Notes

**Nhiệt điện Uông Bí I (Uong Bi I)**
*   **Status:** Retired. **Confidence:** HIGH.
*   **Evidence:** Primary policy document `pdp7a_annex1_table2` explicitly lists "NĐ Uông Bí ngừng phát điện" (Uong Bi stops generating electricity) with capacity 105 MW in the 2021 operational changes section. This is corroborated by its absence in operator annual reports post-2018 (`evn_ar_2018_capacities`) where only Extension units appear. Historical capacity of 105 MW confirmed in `evn_ar_2010_2011_capacities`.

**Nhiệt điện Formosa Hà Tĩnh (Formosa Ha Tinh)**
*   **Capacity & Classification:** 500 MW (Aggregated). **Confidence:** MEDIUM.
*   **Evidence:** `pdp7a_annex1_table1` lists multiple distinct generation units at this industrial complex entering operation between 2016-2020: Unit #2 (150 MW coal), Units #3 & #4 (2x100 MW blast furnace gas), Unit #5 (150 MW coal), and later Units #6,7,8,9,10. The inventory aggregates these into a single row because they function as an integrated industrial cogeneration facility rather than independent merchant power plants. The 500 MW figure represents the initial tranche of grid-relevant thermal capacity cited in planning documents; total site capacity is higher but largely self-consumed or fueled by variable industrial by-products. Fuel is listed as "Coal / Blast Furnace Gas" to reflect this hybrid nature.

**Nhiệt điện Vĩnh Tân II (Vinh Tan II)**
*   **Capacity Discrepancy:** 1244 MW vs 1200 MW. **Confidence:** HIGH.
*   **Evidence:** Planning documents like `pdp7_annex1` consistently refer to this project as 1200 MW (nominal). However, operator filings in `evn_ar_2017_capacities` and `evn_ar_2018_capacities` record the installed capacity as 1244 MW. The inventory adopts the operator-reported value as it reflects the as-built physical reality over the rounded planning figure. Technology is Supercritical, distinguishing it from older subcritical fleets.

**Nhiệt điện Long Phú I (Long Phu I)**
*   **Status:** Construction. **Confidence:** HIGH.
*   **Evidence:** Despite years of suspension and delays documented in `study_e542_pl9_2` (which noted 78% completion but halted status in 2020), the project was officially reclassified as "under construction" in the primary policy annex `pdp8_annex2_table2` dated May 15, 2023. This confirms the resumption of work and validates the "Construction" status over "Shelved". Previous delays were attributed to US sanctions affecting EPC contractors, but the PDP8 inclusion signals resolved impediments sufficient for continued execution.

**TBKHH Nhơn Trạch 3 & 4 (Nhon Trach 3 & 4)**
*   **Fuel Transition:** Domestic Gas to LNG. **Confidence:** HIGH.
*   **Evidence:** Originally planned for domestic gas, `pdp8_annex2_table1` explicitly categorizes this project under "LNG thermal power plants" with 1624 MW capacity. This reflects a strategic pivot due to domestic gas supply shortages. `report_32_annex1` confirms the project is active but delayed by 3 years relative to PDP7A targets. It is currently the most advanced LNG project in the pipeline with EPC contracts awarded, distinguishing it from other announced LNG projects that remain in pre-permit stages.

**Nhiệt điện Cẩm Thanh (Cam Thanh)**
*   **Status:** Cancelled. **Confidence:** HIGH.
*   **Evidence:** `pdp8_annex2_table3` provides a definitive regulatory ultimatum: the Ministry of Industry and Trade engaged investors with a deadline of June 2024 to demonstrate viability, failing which termination procedures would commence. Given the absence of any construction progress or financial closing updates in the intervening period since PDP8 approval (May 2023), and the explicit conditional language regarding termination, the asset is classified as Cancelled rather than merely Shelved. This distinguishes it from other troubled projects in Table 3 that retain active albeit delayed status.
