### Per-asset and per-project explanatory notes

- **PDP8 temporality and latest-plan caveat.** The table above uses the supplied primary evidence pack plus a limited web check. Vietnam’s PDP8 was adjusted by Decision 768/QĐ-TTg dated **15 April 2025**, and implementation guidance under Decision 1509/QĐ-BCT dated **30 May 2025** reportedly changes aggregate thermal targets to 31,055 MW coal, 10,861–14,930 MW domestic gas and 22,524 MW LNG by 2030. Because the exact project-by-project appendices of Decision 1509 were not fully consulted in source text form here, project rows are anchored primarily to Decision 500/QĐ-TTg / PDP8 Annex II dated **15 May 2023** and earlier progress annexes. This affects current status confidence for future LNG and converted projects. ([kpmg.com](https://kpmg.com/vn/en/home/insights/2025/07/revised-pdp-8.html?utm_source=openai))

- **Retired legacy oil/gas assets — Uông Bí old, Ninh Bình, Cần Thơ, Thủ Đức, Hiệp Phước.** These assets are historically important because older PDP7/PDP7A tables show planned retirements rather than fresh decommissioning certificates. Row-level existence is **HIGH/MEDIUM** where EVN annual reports list the asset; current retired/shelved status is **MEDIUM** because it is inferred from retirement schedule entries and absence from later development lists. Fuel vocabulary is imperfect for oil-fired legacy units; the Notes field flags where “Domestic gas” is only a forced mapping to the requested column vocabulary.

- **Formosa Hà Tĩnh / Formosa HT2.** Row existence is **MEDIUM**. PDP7A lists multiple Formosa units with coal and blast-furnace-gas fuel, while PDP8 Table 4 treats Formosa HT2 as a 650 MW industrial cogeneration/by-product source. The table records it as Coal because the allowed fuel vocabulary lacks “blast-furnace gas / industrial by-product gas.” Capacity confidence is **MEDIUM**: 650 MW is directly in PDP8 Table 4, but the unit-by-unit mix is not fully reconciled.

- **Công Thanh / “Cẩm Thanh.”** Row existence is **HIGH** for a 600 MW troubled coal project, but name confidence is **MEDIUM**. PDP7A and progress reports list **NĐ Công Thanh**; PDP8 Table 3 lists **NĐ Cẩm Thanh** at 600 MW with a June 2024 termination-review note. The inventory treats “Cẩm Thanh” as a likely typo/variant of Công Thanh because capacity, context and troubled-project status align.

- **Long Phú I.** Existence and construction status are **HIGH** under PDP8 Table 2, but operating status is not claimed. Progress evidence shows extensive delay, sanctions-related stoppage and later PDP8 retention as “under construction.” COD remains policy-period rather than actual COD.

- **Sông Hậu I.** The table marks Operating with **HIGH** confidence based on progress reports and absence from PDP8’s under-construction coal list, but the supplied pack does not include a commissioning certificate. If stricter source rules are applied, status should be downgraded to **MEDIUM** until an operator commissioning release is attached.

- **Nghi Sơn II and Duyên Hải II.** Both are marked Operating by inference from 2020–2022 construction schedules and absence from PDP8 construction/troubled lists. Existence and capacity are **HIGH**; status is **MEDIUM-to-HIGH** depending on whether inference from official planning/progress sources is accepted as sufficient.

- **Quảng Trạch I and Quảng Trạch II.** Quảng Trạch I is a retained coal project under construction in PDP8 with capacity updated from older 2×600 MW to 1,403 MW. Quảng Trạch II is a major conversion case: older PDP7/PDP7A treated it as coal; PDP8 Table 1 explicitly treats it as 1,500 MW LNG and notes Government agreement to convert. Coal-status rows are not duplicated; the current asset row is LNG.

- **Vũng Áng II and Vân Phong I.** PDP8 Table 2 updates capacities from older 2×600 MW planning figures to 1,330 MW and 1,432 MW, respectively. These are therefore recorded with PDP8 capacities. Technology is marked USC for Vũng Áng II and Vân Phong I based on project characterisation outside the supplied extract; confidence on technology is lower than capacity/status.

- **Vĩnh Tân III, Nam Định I, Quảng Trị I, Sông Hậu II.** These are major delayed coal projects retained in PDP8 Table 3. Row-level existence/status is **HIGH** as “Shelved” or troubled/pre-construction. Status uses the GEM-aligned term “Shelved” because PDP8 describes delays/financing or shareholder difficulties but not formal cancellation.

- **Quỳnh Lập I/II, Vũng Áng III, Hải Phòng III, Quảng Ninh III, Kiên Lương, Tân Phước, Long An coal projects.** These projects are historically important PDP7/PDP7A coal entries. Current status is generally **Cancelled** or **Shelved** where PDP8 omission plus conversion/fuel-policy notes indicate they are no longer active as coal. Confidence varies: **MEDIUM** when only omission and older planning sources support cancellation; **HIGH** where progress reports explicitly say no site, no schedule, or difficult/not feasible.

- **Long An I/II.** Older coal projects are recorded as cancelled, while current LNG projects are recorded separately. This avoids double counting and follows the asset-row rule because fuel, capacity and policy status changed materially.

- **Ô Môn I–IV.** Ô Môn I is an existing 660 MW plant to be converted to Lô B gas under PDP8; Ô Môn II–IV are future domestic-gas CCGT projects. Older sources frequently use 750 MW per project, while PDP8 Table 5 uses 1,050 MW for II–IV. The inventory follows PDP8 for current capacity and marks earlier values only in Notes.

- **Dung Quất and Miền Trung gas projects.** These are tied to the Cá Voi Xanh gas development and have persistent schedule uncertainty. PDP8 keeps Dung Quất I–III and Miền Trung I–II; Miền Trung III is treated as shelved because it appears only as a conditional PDP7A project and is not retained in PDP8 Table 5.

- **Sơn Mỹ I/II LNG.** These are among the largest LNG projects in PDP8. Status is “Permitted” rather than “Construction” because the supplied pack supports policy inclusion and delayed progress reports, not physical construction. Owner/developer is partly legacy from PDP7A/progress reports and may have changed.

- **Nhon Trach 3&4.** The row is marked Construction because the project is retained in PDP8 and progress annexes show advanced implementation; however, the table does not rely on a fresh 2026 operator update. Capacity follows PDP8’s 1,624 MW, not older 1,500 MW or 2×750 planning figures.

- **Potential LNG sites from E-542.** Rows such as Hải Phòng 1–4, Cái Mép H, Mỹ Giang, Chân Mây, Phong Điền, Bến Tre, Sông Đốc and Tân Thuận–Đầm Dơi are marked **LOW** because they are potential sites from a draft PDP8 study or PDP8 fallback notes, not committed projects with a project-specific approval in the supplied primary pack. They are included to satisfy the “announced / shelved / cancelled / future” breadth requirement but should not be counted as firm capacity.

- **Industrial cogeneration and by-product-gas plants.** Hải Hà, Đức Giang, Hòa Phát II, Long Sơn petrochemical, Vedan and Formosa rows sit at the boundary of the requested fuel taxonomy. PDP8 Table 4 explicitly includes cogeneration, waste heat, blast-furnace gas and industrial by-products, but the requested fuel choices do not. The Notes field flags these cases, and confidence is generally **MEDIUM** unless a second source confirms.

- **Technology classifications.** Where the supplied evidence pack lists only “NĐ” / “TBKHH” without boiler class, technology is inferred from unit size, era and standard project descriptions. Such technology cells should be treated as **lower-confidence attributes** than capacity/status unless corroborated by a project technical document.
