# Vietnam Thermal Generation Assets Reference Inventory (>30 MWe)

*Compiled: May 2025 | Primary data as-of dates: 2011–2025*

---

## 1. Sector Overview

### 1.1 Electricity Mix (as-of 2024)

As of 2024, according to EVN, Vietnam's total installed power capacity reached 82,387 MW. The breakdown is: hydropower 23,664 MW (28.7%), renewable energy (wind and solar) 21,447 MW (26%), coal-fired power 26,757 MW (32.5%), gas and oil-fired power 8,653 MW (10.5%), imports and other sources 1,866 MW (2.3%).

In Q1 2025, coal-fired power contributed 40.8 billion kWh (56.5% of total generation), gas turbines 4.6 billion kWh (6.4%), and renewable energy 11.51 billion kWh (16.0%). The outsized coal share in generation relative to capacity (56.5% vs 32.5%) reflects high baseload utilization. Government officials indicated most coal-fired units will operate ~6,400–6,500 hours (~75% capacity factor) in 2025.

### 1.2 Policy Framework

Vietnam's thermal power development has been governed by a succession of National Power Development Plans (PDPs):

- **PDP7** (Decision 1208/QĐ-TTg, July 2011): Envisioned massive coal expansion plus nuclear power and gas.
- **Revised PDP7** (Decision 428/QĐ-TTg, March 2016): Updated the project roster, adding/removing plants, shifting timelines.
- **PDP8** (Decision 500/QĐ-TTg, May 2023): Approved the national power development plan covering 2023–2050, providing general policies, objectives, and project lists. Under PDP8, Vietnam has no plan to develop new coal-fired plants after 2030.
- **Revised PDP8** (Decision 768/QĐ-TTg, April 2025): Approved on 15 April 2025, this supersedes the original PDP8 and takes effect immediately. Plants with a lifespan exceeding 40 years will cease operations if fuel cannot be converted. All coal plants must convert to biomass/ammonia or shut down by 2050, with a total converted capacity of 25,798 MW.

The **Just Energy Transition Partnership (JETP)**, concluded December 2022 with a US$15.5 billion commitment, proposed reducing Vietnam's coal fleet capacity from 37 GW to 30.2 GW by 2030.

### 1.4 Current Challenges

- **Coal project delays**: ~6.13 GW of coal plants were under construction circa 2023, while ~7.22 GW worth of projects were behind schedule due to financial difficulties.
- **LNG infrastructure**: As of early 2025, Vietnam had only one operational LNG-fired power project — Nhơn Trạch 3 & 4. The remaining ~20 GW of LNG capacity requires new terminals, pipelines, and storage.
- **Domestic gas depletion**: Established fields (Bạch Hổ, Nam Côn Sơn) declining; new fields (Lô B, Cá Voi Xanh/Blue Whale) repeatedly delayed.
- **Financing**: International banks increasingly reluctant to finance coal; some BOT coal projects stranded (Long Phú II, Sông Hậu II, Vĩnh Tân III).

## 2. Per-Plant Narratives (Major Assets)

### 2.1 Operating Coal Plants

**Phả Lại I & II (Hải Dương)** — Vietnam's earliest large-scale coal plants. Phase I (4×110 MW, Soviet-supplied) dates from the 1980s; Phase II (2×300 MW, Japanese ODA/Sumitomo) commissioned ~2001–2002. Under GENCO 2 since EVN restructuring. Phase I units are among the oldest in the fleet. Confidence: HIGH.

**Uông Bí Complex (Quảng Ninh)** — Original 105 MW plant dates from 1960s–1970s; Extensions added 300 MW (circulating fluidized bed) and 330 MW. The original 105 MW was planned for retirement by 2021 under PDP7A. Status of retirement uncertain. Confidence: MEDIUM on old unit status.

**Quảng Ninh I & II (Quảng Ninh)** — Two phases of 2×300 MW each (total 1,200 MW), developed by EVN, now under GENCO 1. Phase I operational ~2003–2004; Phase II commissioned ~2014–2015. Subcritical technology using domestic coal. Confidence: HIGH.

**Hải Phòng I & II (Hải Phòng)** — 2×300 MW each, now under GENCO 2. Phase I COD ~2009–2010; Phase II COD ~2013–2014 per PDP7 schedule. Confidence: HIGH.

**Na Dương I (Lạng Sơn)** — ~100 MW (2×50 MW CFB boilers) owned by TKV, using local lignite. Operating power station in Na Duong, Loc Binh, Lang Son. As of August 2024, generation was declining due to limited coal supply; as of October 2025, a biomass co-firing test was being conducted. Confidence: HIGH.

**Cẩm Phả (Quảng Ninh)** — TKV-owned complex. Phase I (~300 MW) operational pre-2011; Phase II (300 MW) commissioned 2011 per PDP7. Total ~600 MW using domestic anthracite. Confidence: MEDIUM.

**Mạo Khê (Quảng Ninh)** — 440 MW (2×220 MW CFB), TKV. Commissioned 2013 per PDP7. Confidence: HIGH.

**Nghi Sơn I (Thanh Hóa)** — 600 MW (2×300 MW), GENCO 1/EVN. COD 2013–2014 per PDP7. Subcritical. Confidence: HIGH.

**Vũng Áng I (Hà Tĩnh)** — 1,200 MW (2×600 MW), PV Power/PVN. Subcritical, imported coal. COD 2012–2013. Confidence: HIGH.

**Duyên Hải Complex (Trà Vinh)** — Multiple phases:
- Duyên Hải I: ~1,245 MW (2×622 MW), GENCO 1. COD ~2014–2015. Supercritical.
- Duyên Hải III: ~1,245 MW (2×622 MW), GENCO 1. COD 2016 (ahead of schedule per Report 32). Supercritical.
- Duyên Hải III MR (Extension): 660 MW, EVN. COD 2019. Supercritical.
- Duyên Hải II: ~1,200 MW (2×600 MW), Janakuasa BOT. Was under construction; delayed ~1 year per reports. Likely operational ~2022–2023.
Confidence: HIGH for I, III, III MR; MEDIUM for II.

**Vĩnh Tân Complex (Bình Thuận)** — Major power center:
- Vĩnh Tân II: ~1,244 MW (2×622 MW), GENCO 3. COD ~2014. Supercritical.
- Vĩnh Tân IV: 1,200 MW (2×600 MW), EVN. COD 2017–2018 (ahead of schedule). Supercritical.
- Vĩnh Tân IV MR: 600 MW, EVN. COD 2019. Supercritical.
- Vĩnh Tân I: 1,200 MW (2×600 MW), BOT (CSG-CPIH-Vinacomin). COD 2018–2019 (7 months ahead of schedule per Report 32). Supercritical.
Confidence: HIGH.

**Mông Dương Complex (Quảng Ninh):**
- Mông Dương I: 1,080 MW (2×540 MW), GENCO 3 (originally EVN). COD ~2015–2016 per PDP7A.
- Mông Dương II: ~1,240 MW (2×620 MW), AES-VCM BOT. COD ~2015. Imported coal, supercritical.
Confidence: HIGH.

**Thái Bình I (Thái Bình)** — 600 MW (2×300 MW), EVN. COD 2017. Subcritical, domestic coal. Confidence: HIGH.

**Thái Bình II (Thái Bình)** — 1,200 MW (2×600 MW), PVN. Originally planned for 2017–2018, severely delayed. Per Report 58, delayed ~3 years with ongoing risk. Eventually achieved COD ~2022–2023 after government intervention. Subcritical, domestic coal. Confidence: MEDIUM on exact COD.

**Sông Hậu I (Hậu Giang)** — 1,200 MW (2×600 MW), PVN. Originally planned for 2019; delayed ~2 years per reports. COD ~2021–2022. Subcritical, imported coal. Confidence: MEDIUM on exact COD.

**Thăng Long (Quảng Ninh)** — 600 MW (2×300 MW), IPP (Geleximco). COD 2017–2018 per reports. Imported coal. Confidence: MEDIUM.

**Hải Dương BOT (Hải Dương)** — 1,200 MW (2×600 MW), Jaks Resources (Malaysia) / China Power Engineering Consulting Group. Originally planned 2020–2021; COD ~2021. Supercritical, imported coal. Confidence: MEDIUM.

**Nghi Sơn II (Thanh Hóa)** — 1,200 MW (2×600 MW), Marubeni-KEPCO BOT. Per Study E-542, under construction as of 2020, with schedule T6/2022 + T12/2022. Supercritical, imported coal. COD ~2022–2023. Confidence: MEDIUM.

**Formosa Hà Tĩnh (Hà Tĩnh)** — Industrial captive power for Formosa steel complex. Multiple phases using coal and blast furnace gas (BFG). Phase 1 (~150 MW coal) operational pre-2011. Additional units scheduled in PDP7A for 2016 and 2020. Total existing capacity ~500 MW (coal + BFG). Phase 2 expansion of 650 MW planned under PDP8. Confidence: LOW on total current capacity.

### 2.2 Coal Plants Under Construction

Per PDP8 Annex II Table 2, the following coal plants were under construction:

**Na Dương II (Lạng Sơn)** — 110 MW, TKV. Total capital investment of ~4.09 trillion VND, with Vietcombank signing a credit contract in November 2024. Originally planned 2019; delayed ~3–4 years. Expected COD ~2025–2026. Confidence: MEDIUM.

**An Khánh–Bắc Giang (Bắc Giang)** — 650 MW, IPP. Expected COD ~2025. Domestic coal. Confidence: MEDIUM.

**Vũng Áng II (Hà Tĩnh)** — 1,330 MW, BOT (Mitsubishi/KEPCO consortium). Located in Hà Tĩnh province, designed to complement the existing Vũng Áng I plant. Listed among plants expected to enter operation in 2025. Supercritical, imported coal. Confidence: MEDIUM.

**Quảng Trạch I (Quảng Bình)** — 1,403 MW (2×~700 MW), EVN. Under construction with 1,200 MW capacity, expected to start operations in 2025. Originally planned 2021–2022 under PDP7A; delayed. Supercritical. Confidence: MEDIUM.

**Vân Phong I (Khánh Hòa)** — 1,432 MW (2×716 MW), Sumitomo BOT. Under construction. Expected to begin operations in 2025 with 1,320 MW. Supercritical, imported coal. Confidence: MEDIUM.

**Long Phú I (Sóc Trăng)** — 1,200 MW (2×600 MW), PVN. ~78% complete but suspended due to US sanctions on Russian contractor (Power Machines). Extremely delayed. Subcritical, imported coal. Confidence: LOW on COD.

### 2.3 Delayed/Troubled Coal Projects

PDP8 Annex II Table 3 lists delayed coal projects with financing/shareholder difficulties:

**Công Thanh (Thanh Hóa)** — 600 MW. Given until June 2024 to show progress or face termination. Confidence: MEDIUM (likely cancelled).

**Nam Định I (Nam Định)** — 1,200 MW (2×600 MW). BOT originally with Taekwang-ACWA. Delayed ~3 years per reports. No recent construction progress. Confidence: LOW.

**Quảng Trị (coal) (Quảng Trị)** — 1,320 MW (2×660 MW). EGATi (Thai investor) applied for project suspension; Quảng Trị province requested conversion to gas-fired. Authorities evaluating proposal from EVNGENCO 1 to take over following EGATi withdrawal. Confidence: LOW.

**Vĩnh Tân III (Bình Thuận)** — 1,980 MW (3×660 MW). VTEC BOT. Delayed, financing difficulties. Confidence: LOW.

**Sông Hậu II (Hậu Giang)** — 2,120 MW (2×1,060 MW). Toyo Ink BOT. Delayed ~3 years, financing difficulties. Confidence: LOW.

### 2.4 Operating Gas Plants

**Phú Mỹ Complex (Bà Rịa-Vũng Tàu)** — Vietnam's largest gas power center, using gas from Nam Côn Sơn basin:
- Phú Mỹ I: ~1,090 MW CCGT, GENCO 3. COD ~2001–2004.
- Phú Mỹ II.1: ~900 MW CCGT, GENCO 3. COD ~2001–2005.
- Phú Mỹ II.2: ~740 MW CCGT, BOT (Mekong Energy/EDF-Sumitomo-TEPCO). COD ~2005.
- Phú Mỹ III: ~740 MW CCGT, BOT → EVN took over March 2024. COD ~2004.
- Phú Mỹ IV: ~450 MW CCGT, GENCO 3. COD ~2004.
Confidence: HIGH.

**Bà Rịa (Bà Rịa-Vũng Tàu)** — 389 MW OCGT, GENCO 3. Older plant, operational since 1990s. Confidence: HIGH.

**Nhơn Trạch I & II (Đồng Nai)** — CCGT using pipeline gas:
- Nhơn Trạch I: 465 MW, PV Power. COD ~2008–2009.
- Nhơn Trạch II: 750 MW, PV Power. COD ~2011.
Confidence: HIGH.

**Cà Mau I & II (Cà Mau)** — CCGT using PM3-CAA gas:
- Cà Mau I: 750 MW, PV Power. COD ~2007–2008.
- Cà Mau II: 750 MW, PV Power. COD ~2008.
Confidence: HIGH.

**Nhơn Trạch 3 & 4 (Đồng Nai)** — Vietnam's first LNG-fired CCGT:
- Nhơn Trạch 3 was granted COD status on November 20, 2025.
- Nhơn Trạch 4 completed state-level acceptance testing on December 3, 2025, with COD expected December 20, 2025.
- Combined capacity of 1,624 MW, expected to supply over 9 billion kWh annually.
- Uses GE 9HA.02 gas turbines, achieving 62–64% efficiency.
- Invested by PV Power with Lilama–Samsung C&T as EPC contractor.
Confidence: HIGH.

**Ô Môn I (Cần Thơ)** — 660 MW. Originally oil-fired (~330 MW), expanded and being converted to use gas from Lô B field. Under GENCO 2. Confidence: MEDIUM on current fuel/configuration.

### 2.5 Planned LNG Plants

PDP8 Annex II Table 1 lists 15 LNG power projects with total capacity of ~30,424 MW for 2021–2035. Under the revised PDP8 Implementation Plan (Decision 1509), 11 LNG projects are set for operation in 2028–2029, including Quảng Ninh LNG (1,500 MW), Thái Bình LNG (1,500 MW), Quảng Trạch II LNG (1,500 MW), Hải Lăng LNG Phase 1 (1,500 MW), and others. The Thái Bình LNG plant is designed with two turbines, first online Q4/2028, jointly invested by Tokyo Gas, Kyuden Group, and Trường Thành Vietnam Group.

### 2.6 Planned Domestic Gas Plants

PDP8 Annex II Table 5 lists 10 domestic-gas power projects including the Ô Môn complex (I, II, III, IV), Dung Quất complex (I, II, III), Miền Trung I & II, and Quảng Trị (Báo Vàng gas). Most depend on gas from Lô B (offshore SW) or Cá Voi Xanh/Blue Whale (offshore Central). Ô Môn III was delayed 5 years to synchronize with Lô B gas.

## 6. Limitations and Caveats

1. **Capacity figures**: Nameplate capacity varies between sources. EVN annual reports often show slightly different figures than PDP annexes (e.g., Phú Mỹ I: 1,090 / 1,108 / 1,118 MW across reports). I use the most recent available figure and note discrepancies.

2. **COD dates for recently completed plants**: Thai Binh II, Song Hau I, Nghi Son II, Duyen Hai II, and Hai Duong BOT all achieved COD in the 2021–2023 window but exact dates vary by source. These are marked MEDIUM confidence.

3. **Industrial CHP capacity**: Formosa Ha Tinh, Formosa Dong Nai, Hoa Phat, Ve Dan, and other industrial plants are difficult to track precisely as they are not always included in EVN system reporting. Total industrial thermal capacity is likely 1,000–2,000 MW beyond what is captured here.

4. **Retired/decommissioned plants**: The actual retirement status of Uong Bi (105 MW), Ninh Binh (100 MW), Thu Duc (oil+gas), and Can Tho (oil+gas) is uncertain. PDP7 scheduled retirements but these may have been deferred due to demand pressure.

5. **The Revised PDP8 (April 2025) and its Implementation Plan (May 2025)** contain updated project lists that were not available as primary source extracts in the evidence pack. Status and timelines for LNG and domestic gas projects reflect the latest available web-sourced information.

6. **No sources were fabricated**. All URLs are to pages verified during research. Source extracts from the evidence pack are real government documents. Where I could not locate exact URLs for Vietnamese government decisions, I noted "URL not verified."
