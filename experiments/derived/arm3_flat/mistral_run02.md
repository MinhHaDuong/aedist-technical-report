### Per-Asset or Per-Project Explanatory Notes

#### **Vĩnh Tân III (Vinh Tan 3)**
- **Development History:** Initially planned as a 3 × 660 MW supercritical coal plant under PDP7 (revised). Construction began in 2019, but progress has been slow due to financing and regulatory challenges.
- **Notable Issues:** The project has faced repeated delays, with expected COD pushed from 2022-2023 to 2024-2025. The developer (VTEC, a BOT consortium) has struggled with financial close and shareholder disputes.
- **Confidence:** MEDIUM for existence and status; HIGH for capacity and technology.
- **Sources:** pdp7a_annex1_table2, report_58_annex, pdp8_annex2_table3.

---

#### **Long Phú I (Long Phu 1)**
- **Development History:** Planned as a 2 × 600 MW supercritical coal plant under PDP7 (revised). Construction began in 2015 but was suspended in 2019 due to financing and regulatory issues.
- **Notable Issues:** The project was resumed in 2022 after PVN secured new financing. Expected COD is 2023, but further delays are possible due to ongoing regulatory and technical challenges.
- **Confidence:** MEDIUM for status and COD; HIGH for capacity and technology.
- **Sources:** pdp7a_annex1_table2, report_58_annex, pdp8_annex2_table2.

---

#### **Quảng Trạch I (Quang Trach 1)**
- **Development History:** Planned as a 2 × 600 MW supercritical coal plant under PDP7 (revised). Construction began in 2021 after EVN took over as the developer from PVN.
- **Notable Issues:** The project has faced delays due to land acquisition and regulatory approvals. Expected COD is 2024, but further slippage is possible.
- **Confidence:** HIGH for existence, capacity, and technology; MEDIUM for COD.
- **Sources:** pdp7a_annex1_table2, report_58_annex, pdp8_annex2_table2.

---

#### **Nhơn Trạch 3 & 4 (Nhon Trach 3 & 4)**
- **Development History:** Planned as a 2 × 750 MW CCGT LNG plant under PDP8. Construction began in 2022 after PV Power secured financing and regulatory approvals.
- **Notable Issues:** The project is a key component of Vietnam's LNG transition strategy. Expected COD is 2024, but delays are possible due to LNG supply chain uncertainties.
- **Confidence:** HIGH for existence, capacity, and technology; MEDIUM for COD.
- **Sources:** pdp8_annex2_table1, report_58_annex, pdp8_annex2_table5.

---

#### **LNG Cà Ná (LNG Ca Na)**
- **Development History:** Planned as a 2 × 750 MW CCGT LNG plant under PDP8. Pre-FS completed in 2022; permits received in 2023.
- **Notable Issues:** The project is a priority for Ninh Thuận province and is expected to be one of the first large-scale LNG plants in Vietnam. Expected COD is 2026.
- **Confidence:** MEDIUM for existence and status; HIGH for capacity and technology.
- **Sources:** pdp8_annex2_table1, study_e542_pl9_1.

---

#### **LNG Bạc Liêu (LNG Bac Lieu)**
- **Development History:** Planned as a 4 × 800 MW CCGT LNG plant under PDP8. Pre-FS completed in 2022; permits received in 2023.
- **Notable Issues:** The project is a key component of Vietnam's LNG transition strategy for the Mekong Delta. Expected COD is 2027, but financing and regulatory challenges remain.
- **Confidence:** MEDIUM for existence and status; HIGH for capacity and technology.
- **Sources:** pdp8_annex2_table1, study_e542_pl9_1.

---

#### **TBKHH Miền Trung I & II (CCGT Trung Trung Bo 1 & 2)**
- **Development History:** Planned as 2 × 750 MW CCGT plants under PDP8, synchronized with the Cá Voi Xanh gas field. Permits received in 2022; construction expected to start in 2023.
- **Notable Issues:** The projects are critical for Vietnam's domestic gas strategy. Expected COD is 2024-2025, but delays are possible due to gas field development uncertainties.
- **Confidence:** HIGH for existence, capacity, and technology; MEDIUM for COD.
- **Sources:** pdp8_annex2_table5, report_58_annex.
