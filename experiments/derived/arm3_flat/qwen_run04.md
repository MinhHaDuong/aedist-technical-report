### Explanatory Notes

**Nhiệt điện Thái Bình II (Thai Binh II)**
*   **Status:** Operating (HIGH confidence). While PDP8 Annex II Table 2 (May 2023) still categorized it as "Under Construction," multiple industry reports and PVN statements confirmed commercial operation of Unit 1 in April 2023 and Unit 2 in May 2023. The discrepancy arises from the lag between physical completion and the formal publication of PDP8.
*   **Capacity:** 1200 MW (2x600). Consistent across PDP7A, PDP8, and operator filings.
*   **History:** Notorious for delays and management scandals involving PVN. Originally scheduled for 2017 (PDP7), actual COD was 6 years late.

**Nhiệt điện Vĩnh Tân III (Vinh Tan III)**
*   **Status:** Shelved (HIGH confidence). PDP8 Annex II Table 3 explicitly categorizes this project under "Delayed projects facing difficulties in shareholder change and capital arrangement." Despite being in the plan, financial close has not been achieved.
*   **Fuel/Technology:** Coal / Supercritical. Originally planned as coal; no official conversion to LNG has been gazetted for this specific asset in PDP8 Table 1 (unlike Quang Trach II), though regional pressure exists.
*   **Owner:** VTEC (BOT). Shareholder restructuring is the primary blocker cited in government reports.

**TBKHH Ô Môn Cluster (O Mon I, II, III, IV)**
*   **Synchronization Risk:** All four plants are critically dependent on the Block B (Lô B) gas pipeline project. Report 32 and Report 58 consistently highlight delays synchronized with Block B's slippage.
*   **Fuel Transition:** O Mon I is currently operating on gas/oil but is designated in PDP8 Table 5 to convert fully to Block B gas. O Mon II, III, IV are new builds specifically for this source.
*   **Capacity Discrepancy:** Earlier plans (PDP7A) listed 750 MW units. PDP8 Table 5 updates these to 1050 MW for new units (II, III, IV), reflecting larger turbine classes. O Mon I remains 660 MW.

**LNG Project Portfolio (Quang Ninh, Thai Binh, Nghi Son, etc.)**
*   **Planning Status:** These assets appear in PDP8 Annex II Table 1 as priority investments for 2021-2030. However, most remain at "Announced" or "Pre-permit" stage. No EPC contracts have been awarded for the northern LNG projects as of mid-2023.
*   **Conversion:** LNG Quang Trach II was formally converted from coal via Government Office Notice 54/TB-VPCP (Feb 2022), making it a definitive LNG asset rather than a speculative replacement.
*   **Uncertainty:** While PDP8 lists them, feasibility studies and LNG terminal configurations are largely preliminary. Confidence is HIGH for *inclusion in the plan* but LOW for *specific COD dates*.

**Retired Assets (Uong Bi I, Ninh Binh)**
*   **Verification Gap:** PDP7 Annex II and PDP7A Annex I explicitly scheduled these retirements for 2021. They are absent from EVN's 2018 AR as active growth assets and were flagged for closure. However, without a 2022-2023 decommissioning certificate, status is capped at MEDIUM/HIGH based on regulatory intent rather than physical verification.
