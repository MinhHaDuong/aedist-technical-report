- EVN Annual Report 2010-2011
- *Citation:* Vietnam Electricity. (2011). *Báo cáo thường niên 2010-2011* [Annual Report 2010-2011]. Hanoi: EVN.
- *Annotation:* Primary operator filing providing the definitive installed capacity baseline for all EVN-owned and many non-EVN thermal assets as of December 31, 2011. Used to establish historical CODs, original nameplate capacities, and ownership for legacy plants (Uong Bi, Pha Lai, Ninh Binh, early gas turbines). Essential for verifying retirement baselines.
- Vietnam Electricity Annual Report 2017
- *Citation:* Vietnam Electricity. (2017). *Báo cáo thường niên 2017* [Annual Report 2017]. Hanoi: EVN.
- *Annotation:* Primary operator filing detailing installed capacity by GENCO subsidiary as of end-2017. Critical for tracking the transition of assets during the GENCO equitization period and confirming operational status of supercritical coal plants (Duyen Hai, Vinh Tan) commissioned between 2015-2017. Provides verified capacity adjustments for gas plants.
- Vietnam Electricity Annual Report 2018
- *Citation:* Vietnam Electricity. (2018). *Báo cáo thường niên 2018* [Annual Report 2018]. Hanoi: EVN.
- *Annotation:* Primary operator filing updating installed capacity through end-2018. Serves as the most recent comprehensive EVN-system roster in the evidence pack. Confirms consolidation of Quang Ninh, Hai Phong, and Vinh Tan complexes at full rated capacity. Validates continued operation of legacy assets prior to scheduled retirements.
- Revised National Power Development Plan VII (PDP7A) – Annex I
- *Citation:* Prime Minister of Vietnam. (2016). *Quyết định 428/QĐ-TTg phê duyệt điều chỉnh Quy hoạch phát triển điện lực quốc gia giai đoạn 2011-2020 có xét đến năm 2030* [Decision 428/QD-TTg approving Revised National Power Development Plan VII]. Hanoi: Government Office.
- *Annotation:* Primary policy document establishing the official commissioning schedule for 2016-2030. Tables 1-3 provide the authoritative planned CODs, capacities, and designated investors for all thermal projects approved during this planning cycle. Basis for identifying "Announced" and "Permitted" projects and detecting schedule deviations. Explicitly documents planned retirements (Uong Bi I, Ninh Binh).
- National Power Development Plan VII (PDP7) – Annex I & II
- *Citation:* Prime Minister of Vietnam. (2011). *Quyết định 1208/QĐ-TTg phê duyệt Quy hoạch phát triển điện lực quốc gia giai đoạn 2011-2020 có xét đến năm 2030* [Decision 1208/QD-TTg approving National Power Development Plan VII]. Hanoi: Government Office.
- *Annotation:* Original master plan superseded by PDP7A. Annex I provides historical commissioning targets for 2011-2020; Annex II outlines the original 2021-2030 vision. Essential for establishing project lineage, identifying cancelled/superseded projects, and understanding the evolution of capacity specifications (e.g., changes in unit sizing for Vinh Tan, Long Phu complexes).
- National Power Development Plan VIII (PDP8) – Annex II
- *Citation:* Prime Minister of Vietnam. (2023). *Quyết định 500/QĐ-TTg phê duyệt Quy hoạch phát triển điện lực quốc gia thời kỳ 2021-2030, tầm nhìn đến năm 2050* [Decision 500/QD-TTg approving National Power Development Plan VIII]. Hanoi: Government Office.
- *Annotation:* Current binding national energy policy as of May 2023. Tables 1-5 constitute the definitive inventory of approved thermal projects, segmented by fuel (LNG, coal under construction, domestic gas) and status (troubled/delayed). Primary source for all future pipeline assets, fuel conversions (Quang Trach II coal-to-LNG), and official recognition of project distress. Overrides all prior planning documents.
- Report 32: Progress Summary of Priority Power Projects
- *Citation:* Steering Committee for National Power Projects. (2020). *Báo cáo 32/BC-BCĐQG về tình hình thực hiện các dự án nguồn điện trọng điểm* [Report 32 on Implementation Status of Priority Power Projects]. Hanoi: Ministry of Industry and Trade.
- *Annotation:* Official government monitoring report comparing actual project progress against PDP7A targets. Provides granular status assessments ("delayed X years", "financing difficulties", "EPC tendered") that justify status classifications beyond simple binary operational/non-operational distinctions. Critical for validating "Construction" vs "Shelved" determinations for troubled coal projects.
- Report 58: Progress Summary of Priority Power Projects
- *Citation:* Steering Committee for National Power Projects. (2021). *Báo cáo 58/BC-BCĐQG về tình hình thực hiện các dự án nguồn điện trọng điểm* [Report 58 on Implementation Status of Priority Power Projects]. Hanoi: Ministry of Industry and Trade.
- *Annotation:* Updated monitoring report succeeding Report 32. Offers refined delay assessments and revised completion forecasts for priority thermal assets. Corroborates persistent delays in BOT coal projects (Nam Dinh I, Song Hau II) and gas infrastructure dependencies (O Mon, Dung Quat). Validates continuity of project distress leading into PDP8 formulation.
- PDP8 Draft Study E-542 – Annexes PL9.1, PL9.2, PL9.5A
- *Citation:* Institute of Energy. (2021). *Nghiên cứu E-542: Phụ lục chương 9 – Tiềm năng và tiến độ nhiệt điện* [Study E-542: Chapter 9 Annexes – Thermal Power Potential and Progress]. Hanoi: Ministry of Industry and Trade.
- *Annotation:* Technical study supporting PDP8 drafting. PL9.1 catalogs potential LNG sites with capacity ceilings; PL9.2 details coal project status and regional import-coal site potential as of late 2020; PL9.5A provides long-term thermal capacity scenarios through 2045. Secondary technical source that contextualizes policy decisions in PDP8 Annex II and identifies pre-policy option spaces (e.g., Hai Lang, Ca Na LNG potential). Not admissible as standalone justification but valuable for resolving ambiguities in site identification and capacity rationale.
