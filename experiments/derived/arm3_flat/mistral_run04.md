### Per-Asset or Per-Project Explanatory Notes

#### **Formosa Hà Tĩnh**
- **Development History:** Originally planned as a coal-fired cogeneration plant for Formosa Ha Tinh Steel. Units 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, and 10 were commissioned between 2016 and 2020, with Units 3 and 4 using blast furnace gas and Units 2, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, and 10 using coal.
- **Confidence:** HIGH. Primary sources (PDP7A Annex I, Report 58) confirm operational status and fuel type for each unit.
- **Sources:** pdp7a_annex1_table1, pdp7a_annex1_table2, report_58_annex.

---

#### **Long Phú I, II, III**
- **Development History:** Long Phú I (1,200 MWe) was commissioned in 2023 by PetroVietnam. Long Phú II (1,320 MWe) is shelved due to financing and shareholder difficulties. Long Phú III (1,800 MWe) is under construction by Banpu Power (Thailand), with expected COD in 2026.
- **Confidence:** HIGH for Long Phú I and III; MEDIUM for Long Phú II (shelved status).
- **Sources:** pdp8_annex2_table2, pdp8_annex2_table3, report_58_annex, study_e542_pl9_2.

---

#### **Vĩnh Tân III**
- **Development History:** Planned as a 1,980 MWe coal-fired plant (3 × 660 MWe) under BOT arrangement. The project is shelved due to financing and shareholder difficulties.
- **Confidence:** MEDIUM. Primary sources (PDP8 Annex II, Report 58) confirm shelved status.
- **Sources:** pdp8_annex2_table3, report_58_annex, study_e542_pl9_2.

---

#### **Sông Hậu II**
- **Development History:** Planned as a 2,000 MWe coal-fired plant (2 × 1,000 MWe) under BOT arrangement. The project is shelved due to financing and shareholder difficulties.
- **Confidence:** MEDIUM. Primary sources (PDP8 Annex II, Report 58) confirm shelved status.
- **Sources:** pdp8_annex2_table3, report_58_annex, study_e542_pl9_2.

---

#### **Quảng Trị I**
- **Development History:** Planned as a 1,320 MWe coal-fired plant (2 × 660 MWe) under BOT arrangement. The project is shelved due to financing and shareholder difficulties.
- **Confidence:** MEDIUM. Primary sources (PDP8 Annex II, Report 58) confirm shelved status.
- **Sources:** pdp8_annex2_table3, report_58_annex, study_e542_pl9_2.

---

#### **Long An I & II**
- **Development History:** Both projects were originally planned as coal-fired plants (1,200 MWe and 1,600 MWe, respectively). Both have been cancelled and converted to LNG.
- **Confidence:** HIGH. Primary sources (PDP7A Annex I, Report 58, Study E542) confirm cancellation and conversion.
- **Sources:** pdp7a_annex1_table3, report_58_annex, study_e542_pl9_2.

---

#### **Hải Hà I-IV (Cogeneration)**
- **Development History:** Four coal-fired cogeneration units (150 MWe each for Units I and II; 600 MWe each for Units III and IV) located in Quảng Ninh. Units I and II are operational; Units III and IV are under development.
- **Confidence:** HIGH for operational units; MEDIUM for future units.
- **Sources:** pdp8_annex2_table4, study_e542_pl9_2, report_58_annex.

---

#### **LNG Projects (Quảng Ninh, Thái Bình, Nghi Sơn, Quảng Trạch II, Hải Lăng, Cà Ná, Sơn Mỹ II, BOT Sơn Mỹ I, Hiệp Phước, Long An I, Bạc Liêu, Long Sơn)**
- **Development History:** These projects are part of Vietnam's strategy to transition from coal to LNG. All are permitted and in various stages of development, with expected CODs ranging from 2026 to 2035.
