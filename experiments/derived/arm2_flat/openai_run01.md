### Per-asset/project explanatory notes

- **Phú Mỹ complex** is split into Phú Mỹ 1, 2.1, 2.1 Extension, 2.2 BOT, 3 BOT, and 4 because CODs, financing/ownership, BOT arrangements, and O&M responsibilities differ. EVNGENCO3 materials confirm the group operates Phú Mỹ plants and took O&M/repair service roles for Phú Mỹ 3 in 2024 and Phú Mỹ 2.2 handover preparation. 
- **Ô Môn group** is split because Ô Môn 1 is existing, while Ô Môn 2–4 are separate Block B gas-to-power projects with different developers and schedules. PDP8 implementation lists Ô Môn 1 as an existing plant, and lists Ô Môn 2, 3, and 4 as domestic-gas projects. 
- **Quảng Trạch 1 vs Quảng Trạch 2**: Quảng Trạch 1 remains a coal plant under construction in PDP8 implementation, while Quảng Trạch 2 appears as a 1,500 MW LNG project. They are separated by fuel, status, and planning treatment. 
- **Quảng Trị coal and Quảng Trị CCGT** are different planning concepts. The 1,320 MW coal project is treated as Shelved because PDP8 implementation records an investor suspension request and provincial proposal to convert to gas; the 340 MW Quảng Trị CCGT is a PDP8 domestic-gas project using Báo Vàng gas. 
- **Nam Định 1, Vĩnh Tân 3, and Sông Hậu 2** are not marked Cancelled because the retrieved official text says unimplemented delayed projects would be terminated under law but does not itself provide a final cancellation/termination instrument for each project. 
- **Long An 2 LNG** remains LOW confidence because the 2024 implementation table clearly shows Long An I LNG only; Long An II appears in broader project discourse and PDP8-era leads but may be a later phase rather than a separately executable 2021–2030 asset.
- **Older small thermal plants** such as Thủ Đức, Cần Thơ/Trà Nóc, Ninh Bình, and portions of Uông Bí are retained where plant-level capacity exceeds 30 MWe, but their current dispatch/retirement status is less certain than for newer PDP8 or operator-confirmed assets.
- **Industrial by-product/cogeneration projects** listed in PDP8 implementation Table 5, such as Formosa HT2, Đức Giang CHP, Hải Hà 1 CHP, and Hòa Phát II waste-heat recovery, are not inserted into the table because the required fuel taxonomy is limited to Coal / Domestic gas / Imported LNG and these projects are by-product, waste-heat, or industrial CHP assets rather than coal/gas/LNG generation rows under the specified categories.
