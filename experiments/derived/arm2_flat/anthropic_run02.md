### NOTES ON MAJOR / DISPUTED / AMBIGUOUS ASSETS

1. **Ninh Bình (#4)**: World Bank repurposing study identified this as Vietnam's oldest operating coal plant. Potential retirement candidate in JETP discussions.
2. **Uông Bí old units**: Pre-1975 units (~105 MW) excluded as likely retired/below 30 MW individual threshold. Only the 300 MW expansion unit (#3) is listed.
3. **An Khánh 1 (Thai Nguyen, ~50 MW CFB)**: Excluded — borderline capacity, private industrial, insufficient sourcing.
4. **Cẩm Phả (#8)**: 600 MW Phase I confirmed. Older mine-mouth units (~80 MW) may exist but are likely retired/below threshold.
5. **Mông Dương 2 (#27)**: AES sold its 51% stake in November 2023; now controlled by One Energy Asia (Bain Capital portfolio).
6. **Long Phú 1 (#34)**: Was ~70% complete when Russian EPC contractor Power Machines was forced off by US sanctions (2018–19). Construction restarted in early 2025 with new domestic/international EPC consortium. PVN targeting 2027 COD.
7. **Vĩnh Tân 3 (#37)**: Mitsubishi withdrew Feb 2021 after HSBC exited financing. No replacement investor confirmed. Listed in revised PDP8 reserve projects. Status: Shelved.
8. **Nam Định 1 (#38)**: Originally 1,200 MW coal BOT (ACWA Power/Taekwang). ACWA withdrew due to decarbonization policy. Provincial government and Gulf Energy (Thailand) have proposed LNG conversion. No construction ever began.
9. **Vũng Áng 2 (#32)**: Unit 1 achieved COD July 2025. Unit 2 in commissioning. Last coal BOT to reach financial close in Vietnam.
10. **Nhơn Trạch 3&4 (#65-66)**: Vietnam's first LNG-to-power plants. Inaugurated by Prime Minister December 2025. Each 750 MW CCGT, supplied via Thị Vải LNG terminal.
11. **Bạc Liêu LNG (#79)**: 3,200 MW in four 800 MW blocks. Listed in revised PDP8 (Apr 2025). Grid connection agreement signed Mar 2026. No PPA finalized; no construction started.
12. **Thủ Đức (#63) / Hiệp Phước (#64)**: Aging gas turbine plants in HCMC metro area. Status uncertain — likely operating at very low capacity factors or in peaking/reserve role. LOW confidence on both capacity and status.
13. **Formosa Hà Tĩnh (~500+ MW) and Hòa Phát Dung Quất (~500+ MW)**: Captive coal plants for integrated steel mills. Excluded from grid-connected inventory but noted as significant thermal capacity.
14. **Ô Môn II–IV (#67-69)**: Await Block B gas (Lot B, O Mon pipeline). JICA ODA financing secured for O Mon II. Block B FID repeatedly delayed; current target ~2026 FID.
15. **Block B gas chain** total ~3,810 MW and **Blue Whale gas chain** total ~3,750 MW per Decision 2634/QĐ-BCT.
