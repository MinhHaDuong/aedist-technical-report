### ASSET NOTES (Major / Disputed / Ambiguous)

1. **Uông Bí (#1)**: Vietnam's oldest coal plant (1966). Original Soviet-built units (4×25 MW, later expanded). A 330 MW extension was commissioned ~2014. Total reported between 440–630 MW depending on which old units are still counted. LOW confidence — needs field verification.

2. **Thái Bình II (#17)**: Inaugurated April 27, 2023; 1,200 MW total capacity developed by PVN. One of Vietnam's most notorious delayed projects, originally scheduled for 2017. PVC construction scandal caused years of delay.

3. **Vân Phong I (#32)**: COD March 13, 2024; designed capacity 1,432 MW; BOT by Van Phong Power Company, wholly owned subsidiary of Sumitomo Corporation. Two capacity figures circulate: 1,320 MW (2×660 MW net) vs 1,432 MW (gross). Table uses 1,320 MWe net.

4. **Nghi Sơn II (#19)**: 1,200 MW BOT project; KEPCO 50%, Marubeni 40%, Tohoku Electric 10%; Unit 1 COD January 2022, Unit 2 COD end-July 2022. Some sources cite 1,330 MW designed capacity vs 1,200 MW actual.

5. **Long Phú I (#36)**: Construction halted at 78% due to US sanctions on Russian EPC contractor Power Machines. Restarted under government Decision 262/QD-TTg; Unit 1 expected Q2 2027, Unit 2 Q3 2027.

6. **Nhơn Trạch III & IV (#65–66)**: Vietnam's first LNG-powered plants with combined capacity of 1,624 MW; NT3 achieved COD November 20, 2025. NT4 completed state acceptance testing December 3, 2025, with COD expected December 20, 2025. Landmark projects for Vietnam's gas transition strategy.

7. **Mông Dương II (#15)**: Owned by AES (51%), Posco Energy (30%), CIC (19%); two 620 MW units; COD 2016. Largest single BOT coal project by capacity.

8. **Hải Dương (#31)**: 1,200 MW BOT; JAKS Resources (30%) and CPECC (70%); Unit 1 COD end-2020, Unit 2 early-2021.

9. **Cancelled coal projects (#38–51)**: PDP8 (Decision 500/QD-TTg, May 2023) formally cancelled approximately 14,000 MW of previously planned coal capacity. Several projects were converted to LNG (Quỳnh Lập I, Công Thanh).

10. **Phú Mỹ complex (#52–57)**: Six CCGT plants totaling ~4,200 MW, all commissioned 2002–2005. Vietnam's largest gas-fired cluster. Individual capacities carry LOW confidence due to conflicting sources; totals are consistent.

11. **Formosa Hà Tĩnh (#21)**: Industrial captive coal plant serving the Formosa steel complex. Capacity variously reported as 350–650 MW. Excluded from EVN dispatch but significant for emissions inventory.

12. **Bạc Liêu LNG (#69)**: 3,200 MW project by Delta Offshore Energy (Singapore). Despite signing PPA framework in 2020, project has seen minimal progress. Likely stalled/at risk.

### KNOWN LIMITATIONS

- **Phu My complex capacities** (#52–57): Individual station MWe figures carry LOW confidence; aggregated from parametric knowledge without primary verification this session.
- **Small/old TKV plants** (#5–9): Cao Ngan, Son Dong, Mao Khe, Cam Pha unit configurations are parametric estimates only.
- **Captive industrial plants**: Only Formosa Ha Tinh identified; other captive coal/gas plants (e.g., at cement works, sugar mills) below threshold or unidentified.

- **Vinh Tan 3**: No credible evidence this project was ever formally proposed or approved; omitted from table.
