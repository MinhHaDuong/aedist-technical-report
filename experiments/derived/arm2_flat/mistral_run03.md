## **Per-Asset Notes**

### **Quang Trach 1 Thermal Power Plant**
- **Capacity:** Officially 1,300 MWe (2 × 650 MWe), confirmed by MOIT and EVN.
- **Status:** Unit 1 connected to grid April 2026; Unit 2 expected mid-2026.

### **Vung Ang II Thermal Power Plant**
- **Technology:** Ultra-supercritical coal, the most advanced in Vietnam.
- **Ownership:** BOT model, Mitsubishi (60%), KEPCO (40%).
- **Status:** Unit 1 commercial operation July 2025.

### **Nam Dinh 1 Thermal Power Plant**
- **Status:** Shelved; proposed for conversion to LNG. At risk of termination due to financing and policy shifts.

### **Vung Ang 3 Thermal Power Plant**
- **Status:** Cancelled under PDP8; proposed for conversion to gas.

### **Nhon Trach 3 & 4 LNG Power Plants**
- **Fuel:** First LNG power plants in Vietnam; Nhon Trach 4 connected to grid July 2025.

### **Long Son, Son My I & II, Bac Lieu LNG Power Plants**
- **Status:** Permitted; included in Revised PDP8 for 2030.
