**Concise notes for major, disputed, ambiguous, or historically important assets**

PDP8 and adjusted-PDP8 planning sources are authoritative for the inclusion of planned gas/LNG/coal projects, but the accessible text of Decision 1509 states that the detailed project lists are in appendices not translated in the retrieved page. Therefore, projects depending only on Decision 1509’s inaccessible appendices are marked LOW unless also corroborated by Decision 262 excerpts or operator materials. 

For Vinacomin/TKV’s small-to-medium coal fleet, the retrieved Vinacomin-linked industry source specifically identifies Na Duong 110 MW, Cao Ngan 115 MW, Son Dong 220 MW, Cam Pha 680 MW, Mao Khe 440 MW, and Nong Son 30 MW as CFB coal plants. Nong Son is excluded from the table because the requested threshold is >30 MWe, not ≥30 MWe. 

Nhon Trach 3 and Nhon Trach 4 are listed separately because they are distinct units/projects within one LNG-to-power development package. PV Power materials identify ongoing EPC/PPA/GSA implementation for Nhon Trach 3&4; the combined table capacity is 1,624 MWe. 

O Mon 1 is classified as Domestic gas despite historic fuel-oil operation because Decision 262/PDP8 implementation treats the O Mon complex in the domestic gas/Block B chain. This row is therefore an operating plant with a fuel-conversion/status note rather than a pure greenfield domestic-gas row. 

Quang Tri appears twice for different concepts: the 1,320 MW coal BOT project, whose extension/review status appears in a PDP8 implementation appendix excerpt, and the 340 MW Quang Tri combined-cycle gas turbine project using Báo Vàng domestic gas. These are not duplicate rows. 

Kiên Giang 1 and Kiên Giang 2 are retained as shelved domestic-gas historical candidates because the MOIT/VEPG PDP8 background report notes inadequate gas supply for Kien Giang I&II. They should not be treated as active firm PDP8 projects without a newer provincial/MOIT approval. 

Capacity reconciliation within the table: Pha Lai 1 plus Pha Lai 2 equals 1,040 MWe; Duyen Hai 1 equals 2 × 622.5 = 1,245 MWe; Duyen Hai 3 equals 2 × 622.5 = 1,245 MWe; Vinh Tan 2 equals 2 × 622 = 1,244 MWe; Nhon Trach 3 plus Nhon Trach 4 equals 1,624 MWe; Long Son 1 plus Long Son 2 equals 3,000 MWe; Son My 1 plus Son My 2 equals 4,500 MWe.
