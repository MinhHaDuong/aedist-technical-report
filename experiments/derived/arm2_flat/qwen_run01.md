*   *Note on PDP8 Caps:* PDP8 mandates a national coal capacity cap of 30,127 MW and an LNG target of 22,400 MW by 2030. The figures above represent the verified utility-scale and major BOT assets; the delta to the national caps comprises smaller industrial cogeneration, captive power, and waste-heat assets not individually itemized in primary public utility registries.

**2. Status & Temporality Definitions**
*   **Operating:** Achieved Commercial Operation Date (COD) and actively dispatching. *As-of Date:* December 2023 (based on audited annual financial/operational reporting cycles of EVN and PV Power).
*   **Under Construction:** Financial close achieved, EPC contracts awarded, and active civil works/commissioning underway. Includes projects with historical delays that remain in the active PDP8 implementation schedule (e.g., Long Phu 1). *As-of Date:* Q1 2024.
*   **Announced:** Explicitly listed in PDP8, secured provincial investment registration or land use rights, but pre-Final Investment Decision (FID). *As-of Date:* Q2 2024.
*   **Cancelled:** Explicitly removed from the national master plan or rejected by provincial authorities prior to PDP8 finalization. *Status Change Date:* May 2023 (issuance of Decision 500/QĐ-TTg).

**3. Source Provenance Hierarchy**
*   **Primary Sources (URLs in Table):** Point directly to the Vietnamese National Legal Library (`thuvienphapluat.vn`) for statutory master plans (Decisions 500 and 262), the Ministry of Industry and Trade (`moit.gov.vn`) for BOT registry, provincial government portals for localized investment approvals/cancellations, and official corporate investor relations portals (`evn.com.vn`, `pvpower.vn`) for audited capacity and operational data.
*   **Secondary Sources (URLs in Table):** Point to international development partners (JICA) and corporate parent disclosures (AES) used strictly to cross-verify BOT ownership structures and foreign direct investment statuses where local primary registries lack English-language granularity.
