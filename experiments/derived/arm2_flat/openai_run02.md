### Per-asset/project explanatory notes

- **Legacy and small assets**: Ninh Binh, old Uong Bi, Thu Duc, and Cai Lan are retained because tracker or historical sources identify >30 MWe thermal capacity, but unit-level and retirement details are weaker than for modern plants; confidence is capped accordingly.

- **Nong Son removed from inventory**: Vinacomin’s CFB summary lists Nong Son as **30 MW**, which does not satisfy the user’s **>30 MWe** threshold. It is therefore excluded from the corrected table.

- **O Mon complex**: O Mon 1 is classified as **Domestic gas** because it belongs to the Block B–O Mon gas-to-power chain even though fuel-oil operation has occurred pending gas availability. O Mon 2–4 statuses reflect revised PDP8 / 2025 implementation information and 2025 EPC or construction reporting, not final COD.

- **Nhon Trach 3–4**: Treated as imported-LNG CCGT and split into two 812 MW rows to reconcile with the widely reported **1,624 MW** total. The status is Operating as of the 2026 trade report of commissioning/commercial operation, supported by PV GAS’s LNG supply contract source.

- **Coal pipeline projects**: Rows marked Shelved or Cancelled rely mainly on GEM’s PDP8 coal briefing/tracker where no individual Vietnamese cancellation decision was found. These rows preserve historical project coverage but are intentionally LOW or MEDIUM confidence.

- **Revised PDP8 temporality**: Future LNG and retained coal projects use **2025-05-30** where their current planning status is drawn from the revised PDP8 implementation plan or legal summaries of it. Original PDP8 Decision 500/2023 was superseded by Decision 768/2025, and implementation guidance followed under MOIT Decision 1509/2025.

- **Capacity reconciliation**: Unit products reconcile with Total MWe where unit ratings are known. Rows with “not found” unit configuration use the cited project total; gross/net is generally unspecified unless the source says otherwise.
