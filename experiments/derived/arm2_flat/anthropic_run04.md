## VIETNAM THERMAL GENERATION INVENTORY (>30 MWe) — FINAL REPORT

### Methodology
This inventory was compiled across three planning turns using government decisions, operator disclosures, international institution reports, and trade press. Each asset is tagged with a confidence level (HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW) per the source hierarchy. Status vocabulary: OPERATING · COMMISSIONING · UNDER CONSTRUCTION · PRE-CONSTRUCTION · PLANNED · SHELVED · CANCELLED · RETIRED · MOTHBALLED.

### NOTES ON MAJOR / DISPUTED / AMBIGUOUS ASSETS

1. **Uông Bí (legacy):** Uong Bi Thermal Power Plant was the first coal-fired power plant built in Vietnam. Construction of the plant began in September 1961 with assistance from China. Original units 1-6 (12-55 MW each, ~189 MW total) are believed retired as of ~2023 given age >40 years. A 300 MW expansion unit was commissioned in 2007, followed by a second expansion unit of 330 MW in 2013. Only the expansion (630 MW) is counted as operating.

2. **Phả Lại 1:** Pha Lai coal-fired power plant (CW-1) has a total capacity of 440 MW (4×110 MW)... in operation since 1986, making it nearly 40 years old. Identified in World Bank/UNDP studies as a priority candidate for early retirement and repurposing.

3. **Long Phú 1:** In January 2018, the project reached 78% completion when US sanctions on Russia halted work by Power Machines. Construction resumed in January 2025 following a relaunch ceremony. Both boilers are expected to be fully installed and pressure-tested in the second and third quarters of 2026, ensuring Unit 1 begins power generation in the third quarter and Unit 2 in the fourth quarter of 2027.

4. **Quảng Trạch 1:** The project comprises two generating units with a total installed capacity of 1,403 MW, utilizing USC technology, and commenced construction in December 2021. Unit No. 1 successfully conducted its first coal firing on May 6, 2026. Commissioning imminent.

5. **Phú Mỹ 2.2 (BOT transfer):** On February 4, 2025, the 715 MW Phu My 2.2 combined-cycle gas plant was transferred to EVN, ending its BOT concession period.

6. **Nhơn Trạch 3&4:** Vietnam's first LNG-to-power project, the 1,624 MW Nhon Trach 3&4 complex uses GE 9HA.02 gas turbines and came online in late 2025. This makes it the country's inaugural imported LNG power plant.

7. **Ô Môn 4:** O Mon IV is a 1,155 MW CCGT plant with Mitsubishi J-series gas turbines. Groundbreaking occurred late August 2025, with COD target December 2028. EPC by Doosan Enerbility and PECC2.

8. **Ô Môn 2 & 3:** O Mon 2, a 1,050 MW plant, is a JV between Vietracimex and Marubeni. O Mon 3 (also 1,050 MW) had a connection agreement signed March 2025. Both depend on Block B gas supply from the Ô Môn pipeline, which has faced repeated delays.

9. **Vân Phong 1:** The 1,432 MW Van Phong 1 BOT coal plant in Khanh Hoa, developed by Sumitomo and KEPCO, began commercial operation of Unit 2 in 2024. One of the last coal plants to reach COD under PDP7 commitments.

10. **Cancelled coal under PDP8:** PDP8 (Decision 500/QĐ-TTg, May 2023) removed approximately 13 GW of planned coal capacity, including all greenfield coal projects not already under construction or with signed BOT/EPC contracts.

11. **Bạc Liêu LNG (3,200 MW):** Proposed by Delta Offshore Energy (US) since ~2019. Despite MOU and provincial support, no FID reached. Effectively shelved as of 2025 due to financing difficulties and lack of PPA.

12. **Quảng Trạch Power Center LNG:** Upon completion of Quang Trach 1 and two LNG plants, the center will supply approximately 4,400 MW to the national grid. This implies Quang Trach 2 LNG (~1,500 MW) + Quang Trach 3 LNG (1,500 MW) alongside the 1,403 MW coal plant.

13. **Formosa Hà Tĩnh:** Captive coal plant at the Formosa steel complex. Estimated ~500 MW but exact configuration unconfirmed in primary sources. Not grid-dispatched by EVN. Included for completeness at LOW confidence.

14. **Na Dương 2:** Na Duong 2 (110 MW) signed its EPC contract in December 2023 and is under construction by TKV. Uses local lignite; one of the last small coal plants approved.

### CAVEATS & LIMITATIONS

1. **Small/old plants (<100 MW):** Several TKV-owned plants (Nông Sơn ~30 MW, Đông Triều, etc.) may exist near the 30 MW threshold but could not be confirmed from available sources. Thu Duc (~160 MW gas turbine, HCMC) is likely retired but could not be verified.
2. **Captive/industrial plants:** Only Formosa Hà Tĩnh is included; other industrial captive coal/gas plants (e.g., at cement works, refineries) are excluded for lack of data.
3. **Oil/diesel peakers:** Several oil-fired GTs exist (notably at Ô Môn 1, which currently burns fuel oil; Thủ Đức; some Phú Mỹ units can burn diesel as backup). These are counted under their primary fuel.
4. **LNG project MW figures:** Many planned LNG projects have only indicative capacities from PDP8 text; actual turbine configurations are not yet determined.
5. **Revised PDP8 (Decision 2634, late 2024):** Added several LNG projects and reshuffled timelines. Some projects listed here as "PLANNED" may have been moved to reserve lists or vice versa in the latest revision.
