### Annotated Bibliography

**Primary Regulatory & Statutory Sources**
1.  **Socialist Republic of Vietnam, Prime Minister's Office. (2023). *Decision No. 500/QĐ-TTg: Approving the National Power Development Planning for the 2021-2030 Period, with a Vision to 2050 (PDP8).***
    *   *URL:* https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Tai-nguyen-Moi-truong/Quyet-dinh-500-QD-TTg-2023-Quy-hoach-dien-VIII-557310.aspx
    *   *Annotation:* The definitive legal framework governing Vietnam's energy transition. Establishes the hard caps for coal, mandates the cancellation of specific greenfield coal projects (Quynh Lap, Nam Dinh), and formally integrates the 22,400 MW LNG pipeline into the national grid plan.
2.  **Socialist Republic of Vietnam, Prime Minister's Office. (2024). *Decision No. 262/QĐ-TTg: Approving the Implementation Plan for PDP8.***
    *   *URL:* https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Tai-nguyen-Moi-truong/Quyet-dinh-262-QD-TTg-2024-ke-hoach-thuc-hien-Quy-hoach-dien-VIII-605534.aspx
    *   *Annotation:* Provides the binding operational timeline and provincial allocation for PDP8 assets. Crucial for verifying the active "Under Construction" status of delayed legacy coal projects (Long Phu 1, Quang Trach 1) and the permitting sequence for LNG facilities.
3.  **Vietnam Ministry of Industry and Trade (MOIT). (2023). *Electricity Projects and BOT Contract Registry.***
    *   *URL:* https://moit.gov.vn/en/web/dien-luc/du-an-dien
    *   *Annotation:* Primary regulatory archive verifying the existence, capacity, and foreign ownership structure of Build-Operate-Transfer (BOT) thermal assets, specifically Mong Duong 2, Nghi Son 2, Vinh Tan 1, and Phu My 3.

**Primary Corporate & Operator Disclosures**
4.  **Vietnam Electricity (EVN). (2023). *Audited Financial Statements and Operational Reports (GENCO 1, 2, 3).***
    *   *URL:* https://www.evn.com.vn/d0/category/Bao-cao-tai-chinh-14-109
    *   *Annotation:* Primary corporate filings detailing the exact nameplate capacities, operational status, and commercial operation dates of state-owned coal and domestic gas plants. Used to verify the >30 MWe capacities and operating status of the Mong Duong, Uong Bi, Pha Lai, Hai Phong, and Phu My complexes.
5.  **PetroVietnam Power Corporation (PV Power). (2023). *Investor Relations and Audited Financial Statements.***
    *   *URL:* https://www.pvpower.vn/en/investor-relations/financial-statements/
    *   *Annotation:* Primary source for assets owned by the state oil and gas group. Verifies the capacities, EPC status, and COD timelines for the Ca Mau gas plant, Nhon Trach 1 & 2, Song Hau 1, and the Nhon Trach 3 & 4 LNG transition project.

**Provincial & Secondary Verification Sources**
6.  **Nghe An Provincial People's Committee. (2024). *Investment and Infrastructure Portal.***
    *   *URL:* https://nghean.gov.vn/en/
    *   *Annotation:* Used to verify the provincial-level reallocation of the Quynh Lap site from the cancelled 2,400 MW coal complex to the approved 1,500 MW LNG project backed by PV Power and SK Energy.
7.  **AES Corporation. (2023). *Global Locations: Vietnam Operations.***
    *   *URL:* https://www.aes.com/our-businesses/global-locations/vietnam
    *   *Annotation:* Secondary corporate disclosure used to cross-verify the ownership structure and active PPA status of the Mong Duong 2 BOT coal facility in Quang Ninh.
