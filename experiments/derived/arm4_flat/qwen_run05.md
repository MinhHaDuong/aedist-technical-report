### Per-Asset Explanatory Notes

*   **Uông Bí 1, Ninh Bình, Thủ Đức, Hiệp Phước**: Historical subcritical coal and oil/gas peaking plants explicitly scheduled for retirement under PDP7 and PDP7a due to age, inefficiency, and urban environmental concerns.
*   **Thái Bình II & Long Phú I**: PVN-developed coal projects severely delayed. Thái Bình II suffered from financial mismanagement and contractor issues. Long Phú I was halted due to US sanctions affecting the original Russian EPC contractor (Power Machines).
*   **Quảng Trạch I & II**: Originally assigned to PVN, Quảng Trạch I was transferred to EVN due to PVN's capital constraints. Quảng Trạch II was subsequently approved for conversion from coal to LNG by the Prime Minister (VPCP 54/TB-VPCP).
*   **Sơn Mỹ I & II**: A major 4.5 GW LNG hub in Bình Thuận. The project has faced prolonged delays in BOT negotiations, PPA finalization, and gas supply agreements with international developers (AES, Sojitz).
*   **Hải Phòng III & Kiên Giang I/II**: Shelved projects. Hải Phòng III faced strong local opposition and provincial commitments to phase out coal. Kiên Giang was shelved due to insurmountable domestic fuel supply issues.
*   **Vĩnh Tân III & Sông Hậu II**: Large-scale BOT coal projects facing severe financing and shareholder restructuring difficulties. PDP8 explicitly categorizes them as delayed projects struggling with capital arrangement.
*   **Ô Môn Cluster (II, III, IV) & Dung Quất/Miền Trung Clusters**: Totaling >5 GW, these plants are strictly tied to the upstream development of the Lot B and Blue Whale (Cá Voi Xanh) gas fields. Delays in upstream gas infrastructure directly dictate the power plants' COD schedules.
