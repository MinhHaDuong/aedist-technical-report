# VIETNAM THERMAL POWER PLANT INVENTORY (>30 MWe) — VERIFIED

*Report date: 23 May 2026 | Data as-of: Q1 2026 unless otherwise noted*

---

## (A) SECTOR OVERVIEW

Vietnam's thermal fleet provides ~49% of system generation. The governing planning instrument is **PDP8** (Decision 500/QĐ-TTg, 15 May 2023), amended by **Decision 768** (15 April 2025). Under the December 2022 JETP, peak coal capacity is capped at ~30.2 GW, with no new coal plants permitted after 2030 and plants over 40 years old to be retired or converted. The Ô Môn gas complex and Dung Quất cluster await upstream gas (Lô B and Cá Voi Xanh, respectively). Vietnam's first LNG-to-power plant (Nhơn Trạch 3) achieved first fire in early 2025.
