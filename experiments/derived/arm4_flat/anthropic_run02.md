## STATISTICAL SUMMARY

**Operating fleet:** 35 utility-scale plants + 2 operating captive/cogeneration plants = **37 operating coal-fired facilities**, totalling approximately **27,036 MW** (26,236 MW utility + ~800 MW captive). To date, about 30 coal-fired power plants have been developed and are operating (Mondaq/Russin Vecchi; "about 30" likely excludes small and captive plants, consistent with this inventory when grouping complexes like Vinh Tan 4+MR, Cam Pha 1&2, and Duyen Hai units).

**Under construction:** 5 plants, **4,693 MW**. Of these, Vũng Áng 2 is in commissioning and Quảng Trạch 1 achieved first coal firing in May 2026.

**Pipeline to 2030 maximum (if all UC plants complete):** ~30,929 MW utility-scale + captive.

Both the original and revised PDP8 restrict coal to projects under construction, capping 2030 capacity at ~31 GW. The 2025 plan sets a firmer roadmap for fuel conversion: all coal plants must convert to biomass/ammonia or shut down by 2050. Vietnam does not have a plan to develop new coal-fired plants after 2030.

**Retirement schedule:** Pha Lai (440 MW) and Ninh Binh (100 MW) set to cease operation by 2030 if unable to upgrade. By 2050: 600 MW Uong Bi, 600 MW Hai Phong 1, 1,200 MW Quang Ninh, 115 MW Cao Ngan, 600 MW Cam Pha 1&2, and 220 MW Son Dong to be replaced by clean energy.

## NOTES ON COMPLEX ASSETS

**N1 — Uông Bí complex:** Three distinct generations. Old plant (4×12 MW + 2×~28 MW = 105 MW, COD 1964-76) may have been retired as of December 2023. Vietnam's oldest power station has passed a lifespan of 40 years already. MR1 (300 MW, ~2007) and MR2 (330 MW, 2011) remain operating. January 2025 total capacity listed as 630 MW.

**N2 — Vinh Tan complex:** Four separate plants at one site in Bình Thuận. VT1 BOT (1,200 MW, CSG-CPIH, 2018-19), VT2 (1,244 MW, GENCO3, 2014-15), VT4 (1,200 MW, EVN, 2017-18), VT4 MR (600 MW, EVN, 2019). Total operating: 4,244 MW. VT3 (1,980 MW BOT) cancelled.

**N3 — Duyên Hải complex:** Three plants in Trà Vinh. DH1 (1,245 MW, GENCO1, 2014-16), DH3 (1,245 MW, GENCO1, 2016), DH3 MR (660 MW, EVN, 2019), DH2 BOT (1,200 MW, Janakuasa, ~2022). Total operating: 4,350 MW.

**N4 — Quảng Trạch complex:** Unit 1 of Quảng Trạch 1 successfully conducted its first coal firing on May 6th. Total installed capacity 1,403 MW, USC technology. Quang Trach 2 LNG contract signed February 9, 2026. Quảng Trạch 2 coal version was cancelled per PDP8; PDP8 listed Quang Trach II among projects that would not move forward using coal.

**N5 — Long Phú 1:** Remains in PDP8 under-construction list but effectively stalled since 2018 due to US sanctions on Russian contractor Power Machines. As of March 2025, there was no further news on financing the project. Revised PDP8 target COD: 2027. Completion highly uncertain.

**N6 — Cẩm Phả complex capacity:** EVN AR 2010-11 lists 600 MW total under "Cam Pha" (Vinacomin). PDP7 lists "Cẩm Phả II: 300 MW" entering operation 2011. Retirement article confirms "600 MW Cam Pha 1&2." Treat as single 600 MW complex.

**N7 — Capacity discrepancies:** Vũng Áng 2 listed as 1,330 MW (PDP8) vs 2×600 = 1,200 MW (PDP7A) — actual BOT units are 2×665 MW. Van Phong 1 listed as 1,432 MW (PDP8) vs 2×660 MW (PDP7A) — actual units are 2×716 MW. Quảng Trạch 1 listed as 1,403 MW (PDP8) vs 1,200 MW (many secondary sources) — confirmed at 1,403 MW per May 2026 first-firing report.
