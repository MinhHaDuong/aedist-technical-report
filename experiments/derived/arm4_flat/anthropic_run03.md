## Per-Asset Notes

**#1, #6 — Retirements.** PDP7A Annex I Table 2 explicitly listed "NĐ Uông Bí ngừng phát điện 105 MW" under 2021 entries. PDP7 Annex II likewise listed Ninh Bình 100 MW for retirement in 2021. Both absent from EVN AR 2018 GENCO rosters for Uông Bí original; Ninh Bình still listed under GENCO 3 in 2018 at 100 MW. No formal retirement gazette found; status capped at MEDIUM.

**#16 — Thái Bình 2.** Severely delayed from PDP7 target 2014–15 to actual 2022–23. Report 32 stated "Chưa xác định tiến độ hoàn thành." Study E-542 PL9.5A includes it at 1,200 MW as domestic coal. Media sources confirm completion late 2022/early 2023. MEDIUM due to reliance on media rather than official gazette for COD.

**#20 — Mông Dương 1.** EVN AR 2018 records 1,080 MW but PDP7 Annex I planned 2×500 = 1,000 MW (COD 2016–17). The 1,080 MW figure from the operator report is used as authoritative.

**#27 — Vân Phong 1.** Vietnam's first and only USC coal plant. PDP8 Table 2 listed it under construction (May 2023). Sumitomo and MOIT confirmed COD January 24, 2024. 2×716 = 1,432 MW. URL for MOIT confirmation not independently verified.

**#37 — Formosa Hà Tĩnh Phase 1.** PDP7A Table 1 itemised: #2 (150 MW coal, 2016), #3–4 (2×100 MW BFG, 2016), #5 (150 MW coal, 2016). EVN AR 2011 recorded an earlier 150 MW unit. Total ~500 MW is approximate; exact split between coal-fired and blast-furnace-gas units ambiguous. Not a standard grid utility plant.

**#38 — Hải Hà 1.** PDP8 Table 4 lists 300 MW as existing cogeneration in the Texhong industrial zone. PDP7A targeted 3×50 = 150 MW for 2019. The 300 MW figure from PDP8 may reflect expansion. LOW confidence due to single-source and no operator confirmation.

**#43 — Long Phú I.** Study E-542 (Oct 2020) reported 78% physical completion but suspended due to US sanctions on Power Machines (Russia). PDP8 Table 2 still lists it under construction at 1,200 MW. Revised PDP8 reportedly targets 2026 COD but this is highly uncertain. LOW confidence.

**#44–47 — Shelved coal.** PDP8 Table 3 classified these as "chậm tiến độ, gặp khó khăn trong thay đổi cổ đông, thu xếp vốn." Deadline June 2024 applied to Công Thanh only. No investor or construction progress reported for the other four as of any source reviewed. Revised PDP8 (Apr 2025) did not remove these from the plan but no advancement confirmed.

**#48 — Công Thanh.** PDP8 Table 3 set June 2024 deadline. Revised PDP8 (Decision 768, Apr 2025) converted site to LNG 1,500 MW. Coal version is Cancelled.

**#51–53 — Phú Mỹ complex.** Capacity figures differ between EVN AR 2011 and 2018 (PM1: 1,090→1,118; PM2.1: 900→945; PM4: 450→477). Later figures used; difference likely reflects uprate/re-measurement.

**#63 — Ô Môn I.** EVN AR 2018 lists 660 MW under "Oil fired (FO)" and separately 155 MW Cần Thơ under "Gas turbine (oil)." PDP8 Table 5 lists Ô Môn I at 660 MW for conversion to Block B gas. Fuel conversion contingent on Block B pipeline (est. end 2026).

**#64–65 — Nhơn Trạch 3 & 4.** PDP8 Table 1 lists combined 1,624 MW. PVN confirmed inauguration December 14, 2025. Split as 2×812 MW (812 = 1,624/2). GE 9HA.02 gas turbines; Vietnam's first LNG-fired power complex. LNG from Thị Vải terminal.

**#66–67 — Ô Môn III & IV.** Both dependent on Block B gas supply. Ô Môn III: investor changed from EVN to PVN (June 2023); grid connection agreement signed March 2025. Ô Môn IV: ADB loan approved; Doosan EPC contract signed June 2025; groundbreaking August 2025. PDP8 Table 5 lists both at 1,050 MW each.

**#69–73 — CVX-dependent plants.** All five Dung Quất and Miền Trung units depend on Cá Voi Xanh (Blue Whale) gas field FID, not yet taken. Collective 3,750 MW. All pre-permit status; LOW confidence on any timeline.

**#88 — LNG Bạc Liêu.** Investment certificate granted 2020; 3,200 MW; US$4 billion. Grid connection agreement signed March 2026. No PPA finalised; no construction commenced. MEDIUM due to documented regulatory progress but no financial close.

**Known omissions:** Pure oil-fired plants (Thủ Đức oil 165 MW, Hiệp Phước 375 MW, Cần Thơ oil 33 MW) excluded per fuel-scope definition (coal, gas, LNG). Hải Hà 2–4 cogeneration (3×600 = 1,800 MW) deferred to 2031–35 per PDP8 Table 4; not tabled as status is Announced with no investor activity. Vê Đan (72 MW gas cogen, evn_ar_2011) omitted: industrial captive, insufficient data for current status.
