### Annotated Bibliography
1.  **EVN Annual Reports (2010-2011, 2017, 2018)** [`evn_ar_2010_2011_capacities`, `evn_ar_2017_capacities`, `evn_ar_2018_capacities`]: Primary operator disclosures detailing installed capacity, ownership, and generation mix baselines. Essential for establishing the legacy fleet and tracking early retirements. (Source: `https://www.evn.com.vn`)
2.  **PDP7 (Decision 1208/QĐ-TTg, 2011) & PDP7a (Decision 428/QĐ-TTg, 2016)** [`pdp7_annex1`, `pdp7_annex2`, `pdp7a_annex1_table1`, `pdp7a_annex1_table2`]: Primary policy annexes mapping the historical super-cycle of coal expansion, BOT project lineage, and early retirement targets. (Source: `https://thuvienphapluat.vn`)
3.  **PDP8 (Decision 500/QĐ-TTg, May 2023)** [`pdp8_annex2_table1` to `table5`]: The definitive current-policy primary source categorizing the active LNG pipeline, committed coal projects, and structurally delayed/troubled thermal assets. (Source: `https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Tai-nguyen-Moi-truong/Quyet-dinh-500-QD-TTg-2023-558418.aspx`)
4.  **Reports 32 & 58 (BCD / MOIT Progress Summaries)** [`report_32_annex1`, `report_58_annex`]: Primary government implementation tracking documents exposing systemic delays in the gas chain (Block B, CVX) and BOT coal financing/contractor hurdles. (Source: `https://www.moit.gov.vn`)
5.  **Study E-542 (PDP8 Draft 3 Annexes)** [`study_e542_pl9_1`, `study_e542_pl9_2`, `study_e542_pl9_5a`]: Secondary/technical planning documents detailing regional thermal potential, import-coal site rosters, and pre-policy LNG option spaces utilized by the Institute of Energy (IE). (Source: `https://www.ie.com.vn`)
6.  **Corporate & International Disclosures**: Corroborating secondary sources for COD announcements, sponsor exits, and sanctions impacts (e.g., PVN, TKV, Reuters). (Sources: `https://www.pvn.vn`, `https://www.vinacomin.vn`, `https://www.reuters.com`)
