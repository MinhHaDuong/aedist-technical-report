**Per-asset explanatory notes**

- **Legacy retirements:** Uông Bí old, Thủ Đức, and Ninh Bình have older operating evidence and policy retirement lines. The table uses the policy retirement year where available, but confidence is capped where no current operator retirement notice was verified.
- **Coal projects cancelled or superseded by PDP8:** Quỳnh Lập I/II, Hải Phòng III, Quảng Ninh III, Kiên Lương, Tân Phước I/II, and Bạc Liêu coal are retained because they existed in PDP7/PDP7A lineage; their current status is Cancelled or Shelved because PDP8 did not carry them forward as coal-construction projects and in several cases replaced them with LNG or reserve-location treatment.
- **PDP8 delayed/troubled coal projects:** Vĩnh Tân III, Nam Định I, Sông Hậu II, Công Thanh/Cẩm Thanh, and Quảng Trị are not classified as Construction unless PDP8 or a later primary source explicitly shows construction.
- **Quảng Trạch I:** kept as Construction as of November 2025 because EVN reported commissioning progress but not commercial operation.
- **Nhơn Trạch 3 and 4:** updated to Operating as of January 2026 based on PV Power’s COD announcement.
- **Industrial/cogeneration assets:** Formosa Hà Tĩnh, Hải Hà, and Đức Giang are included because PDP8 treats them as power sources >30 MWe even though they are not ordinary utility thermal plants.
- **LNG reserve sites:** Chân Mây, Mũi Kê Gà, Bến Tre, and Hải Phòng are LOW confidence because available admissible evidence supports only reserve/potential or implementation-plan status, not a fully approved plant configuration.
