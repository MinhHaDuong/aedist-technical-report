### **Explanatory Notes (Major, Disputed, or Ambiguous Assets)**

1. **Formosa Hà Tĩnh (150 MWe)**
   - **Note:** Formosa Hà Tĩnh is a captive power plant for the Formosa Ha Tinh Steel complex. It is included here as it is grid-connected and >30 MWe, but its primary purpose is industrial self-supply.
   - **Sources:** [pdp7a_annex1_table1](#chunk-4), [report_58_annex](#chunk-15).

2. **Ô Môn I (330 MWe, Oil → Gas)**
   - **Note:** Originally oil-fired, Ô Môn I is slated for conversion to gas (Lô B) per PDP8 Annex II Table 5. The conversion is planned but not yet implemented.
   - **Sources:** [evn_ar_2010_2011_capacities](#chunk-1), [pdp8_annex2_table5](#chunk-13).

3. **Hiệp Phước (375 MWe, Oil → LNG)**
   - **Note:** Hiệp Phước is partially converted to LNG per PDP8 Annex II Table 1. The exact capacity converted is unclear; only 825 MWe is approved for LNG expansion.
   - **Sources:** [evn_ar_2010_2011_capacities](#chunk-1), [pdp8_annex2_table1](#chunk-9), [study_e542_pl9_1](#chunk-16).

4. **Delayed/Disputed Coal Projects (Nam Định I, Quảng Trị, Vĩnh Tân 3, Sông Hậu 2, Cẩm Thanh)**
   - **Note:** These projects are listed in PDP8 Annex II Table 3 as facing financing or shareholder difficulties. Their future is uncertain; some may be cancelled or converted to LNG.
   - **Sources:** [pdp8_annex2_table3](#chunk-11), [report_58_annex](#chunk-15).

5. **Announced Projects (No Site Approval/No Investor)**
   - **Note:** Projects such as Hải Phòng III, Quảng Ninh III, Rạng Đông, Kim Sơn, Thái Bình III, Vũng Áng III, and others are listed in PDP7 or PDP8 studies but lack site approval, investors, or face local opposition. Their realization is considered low-confidence.
   - **Sources:** [pdp7a_annex1_table3](#chunk-6), [study_e542_pl9_2](#chunk-17), [report_58_annex](#chunk-15).

6. **LNG Projects (Long Sơn, Long An II)**
   - **Note:** Long Sơn and Long An II are explicitly delayed in PDP8 Annex II Table 1 (TB-VPCP 64/2023). Their commissioning dates are pushed to 2031 and 2035, respectively.
   - **Sources:** [pdp8_annex2_table1](#chunk-9), [study_e542_pl9_1](#chunk-16).
