### Statistical Summary
*   **Operating Grid-Connected Thermal**: 29,809 MW (Coal: 21,689 MW | Gas/Oil: 8,120 MW)
*   **Operating/Captive Industrial Thermal**: 925 MW (Formosa, Lee & Man, Ve Dan)

*   **Delayed / Troubled / Shelved Pipeline**: 18,650 MW (Coal: 8,550 MW | LNG: 3,200 MW | Upstream Gas Pending: 6,900 MW)
*   **Retired / Mothballed (Historical Baseline)**: 778 MW

### Per-Asset Notes for Major or Disputed Assets
*   **Long Phu 1 (1200 MW)**: PVN project severely delayed due to US sanctions on Russian contractor Power Machines, halting critical turbine deliveries. PVN is actively seeking legal pathways to replace the contractor; COD pushed to 2025+.
*   **Vung Ang II (1330 MW)**: Listed in PDP8 Table 2 as "under construction," but Japanese sponsors (One Energy, Mitsubishi) officially withdrew due to decarbonization policies and inability to secure international financing. Effectively shelved as a coal plant.
*   **O Mon II, III, IV & Mien Trung / Dung Quat Clusters**: Totaling >6 GW, these gas projects are legally approved but physically delayed pending the development of upstream gas infrastructure (Block B & O Mon gas pipeline, and Blue Whale / Ca Voi Xanh fields).
*   **Nam Dinh I, Song Hau II, Vinh Tan III**: BOT coal projects trapped in PDP8 Table 3. Sponsors (ACWA Power, Toyo Ink) have withdrawn or failed to achieve financial close due to international coal finance bans, environmental pushback, and PPA disputes.
*   **Bac Lieu LNG (3200 MW)**: Faces severe PPA negotiation deadlocks and financing hurdles. Widely recognized by industry analysts as delayed to post-2030 or at high risk of cancellation/restructuring.
*   **Retired/Mothballed Fleet (Uong Bi 1, Ninh Binh, Hiep Phuoc, Thu Duc, Can Tho)**: Legacy subcritical coal and older oil/gas turbines retired or mothballed between 2015-2021 to comply with environmental mandates, economic dispatch priorities, and urban air quality regulations.
