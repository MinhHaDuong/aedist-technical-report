### Per-Asset Notes

1. **Uông Bí Original**: PDP7/7A planned 2021 retirement. EVN AR 2018 omits this 105 MW entry while retaining the 300+330 MW extensions. GEM data lists total Uong Bi complex at 630 MW (=300+330), confirming retirement of original units.

2. **Ninh Bình**: Still operating as of 2025 per World Bank repurposing study. Planned retirement by 2030 if unable to upgrade emissions technology.

3. **Vân Phong I**: About 30 coal-fired power plants have been developed and are operating as of the Plan. Vân Phong I achieved COD March 2024 — Vietnam's first USC coal plant, built by Sumitomo under BOT. Nameplate 1,432 MW (2×716 MW).

4. **Vũng Áng II**: USC technology. Unit 1 COD July 2025. Unit 2 in commissioning. Total 1,330 MW. Samsung C&T/KEPCO consortium BOT.

5. **Long Phú I**: 78% complete, suspended since 2019 due to US sanctions on Russian contractor Power Machines. PVN targeting 2027 restart but financing unresolved. Confidence LOW for completion timeline.

6. **Nhơn Trạch 3&4**: PM attended inauguration ceremony on December 14, 2025. NT3 received COD on Nov 21, 2025. Total capacity 1,624 MW using GE 9HA.02 gas turbines. Combined capacity 1,624 MW, expected to supply over 9 billion kWh annually.

7. **PDP8 Table 3 shelved projects** (Công Thanh, Nam Định I, Quảng Trị, Vĩnh Tân III, Sông Hậu II): This total capacity does not include the capacity of delayed/abandoned projects (Cong Thanh, Nam Dinh I, Quang Tri, Vinh Tan III, Song Hau II). Vietnam does not have a plan to develop new coal-fired plants after 2030.

8. **Gas fleet (MEDIUM confidence items)**: Operating gas plants are based on EVN AR 2017/2018 evidence pack data. Capacities may have been derated or uprated since 2018; exact 2025 capacities not web-verified.

9. **Revised PDP8 (Decision 768, April 2025)**: On April 15, 2025 the Prime Minister issued Decision 768 approving the Amended PDP8. Domestic gas capacity by 2030 adjusted to 10,861–14,930 MW. Power generated by LNG plants is expected to reach 22,525 MW by 2030. Coal plant roster unchanged from Decision 500.

10. **Aggregate totals** (operating coal ~27,200 MW across ~34 plants; operating gas ~7,800 MW across ~12 plants; under construction coal ~3,360 MW across 4 plants; 1 suspended coal 1,200 MW; 5 shelved/cancelled coal ~7,220 MW).
