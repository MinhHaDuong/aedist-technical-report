### Annotated Bibliography

*   **[1] https://evn.vn/ar/2011** | `evn_ar_2010_2011_capacities`
    *   *EVN Annual Report 2010-2011.* Primary baseline for historically operating thermal assets, exact unit capacities, and ownership structures (EVN vs. GENCOs vs. TKV vs. BOT) as of Dec 31, 2011.
*   **[2] https://evn.vn/ar/2017** | `evn_ar_2017_capacities`
    *   *Vietnam Electricity Annual Report 2017.* Operator-side baseline roster detailing the post-restructuring GENCO 1/2/3 allocations and commissioned coal/gas assets up to 2017.
*   **[3] https://evn.vn/ar/2018** | `evn_ar_2018_capacities`
    *   *Vietnam Electricity Annual Report 2018.* Updated operator roster confirming the persistence of legacy plants like Ninh Bình and Uông Bí extensions.
*   **[4] https://moit.gov.vn/pdp7** | `pdp7_annex1`, `pdp7_annex2`
    *   *PDP7 (Decision 1208/QĐ-TTg, 2011).* The foundational policy document that dictated Vietnam's coal and gas build-out over the 2011-2020 decade. Provides original commissioning schedules and retirement mandates.
*   **[5] https://moit.gov.vn/pdp7a** | `pdp7a_annex1_table1`, `pdp7a_annex1_table2`, `pdp7a_annex1_table3`
    *   *Revised PDP7a (Decision 428/QĐ-TTg, 2016).* The revised master plan adjusting the 2016-2030 pipeline, introducing early LNG concepts, and delaying several BOT coal projects.
*   **[6] https://moit.gov.vn/pdp8** | `pdp8_annex2_table1` to `table5`
    *   *PDP8 (Decision 500/QĐ-TTg, May 2023).* The current master plan governing all new and ongoing power infrastructure. Annex II Tables 1-5 are the definitive sources for the LNG pivot, the status of delayed coal BOTs, and domestic gas synchronization schedules.
*   **[7] https://moit.gov.vn/rpt32** | `report_32_annex1`
    *   *Report 32 Annex 1 (National Steering Committee for Power Development).* Implementation progress tracking document detailing the gap between PDP7a planned CODs and actual site conditions/EPC bottlenecks.
*   **[8] https://moit.gov.vn/rpt58** | `report_58_annex`
    *   *Report 58 Annex.* Subsequent implementation progress evaluation providing the "ground truth" on project delays, financing bottlenecks, and explicit delay penalties for major IPPs and SOEs.
*   **[9] https://ie.com.vn/e542** | `study_e542_pl9_1`, `study_e542_pl9_2`, `study_e542_pl9_5a`
    *   *Study E-542 (PDP8 Draft 3 Annexes, Institute of Energy, 2020-2021).* Provides the technical and spatial option space that preceded PDP8, detailing regional potential for LNG import hubs and coal plant siting.
