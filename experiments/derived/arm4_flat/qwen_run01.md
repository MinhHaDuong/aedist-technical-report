### Per-Asset Notes

The compiled inventory encompasses 93 distinct thermal generation assets (>30 MWe), representing an aggregate system footprint of 90,119 MWe. This total reconciles precisely across the dataset: 28,185 MWe currently operating, 205 MWe explicitly retired, 13,149 MWe under construction, and 48,580 MWe in planned or delayed stages. 

- **Formosa Hà Tĩnh & Industrial Cogeneration**: Categorized as complex industrial cogeneration. PDP7a schedules multiple phases utilizing both coal and blast furnace gas, culminating in 500 MWe of captive/grid thermal capacity. PDP8 further carves out unlimited capacity potential for industrial by-product facilities (e.g., Hải Hà 1, Hòa Phát II, Đức Giang, Formosa HT2) to maximize energy efficiency.
- **Thủ Đức & Cần Thơ**: Both sites operate dual-fuel or mixed-cycle configurations. Thủ Đức combines 114 MWe of gas turbine capacity with 165 MWe of oil-fired capacity (279 MWe total). Cần Thơ similarly blends 150 MWe of gas and 35 MWe of oil (185 MWe total).
- **Ô Môn Complex**: Currently anchored by the 660 MWe Ô Môn 1 plant. PDP8 envisions a massive expansion (Ô Môn 2, 3, and 4) adding 3,150 MWe, strictly contingent upon the upstream development of the Lô B (Block B) gas field.
- **Quảng Trạch 2 Fuel Conversion**: Originally planned as a 1,200 MWe coal facility under revised PDP7, PDP8 Annex II Table 1 explicitly converts this asset to a 1,500 MWe LNG facility following government approval in early 2022.
- **Sơn Mỹ 1 & 2**: Planned as major LNG-to-power hubs (2,250 MWe each). Sơn Mỹ 1 is structured as a BOT project, while Sơn Mỹ 2 is assigned to PVN; both face long-lead LNG supply chain dependencies.
- **Retired Assets**: Uông Bí 1 (105 MWe) and Ninh Bình (100 MWe) are explicitly flagged in PDP7 Annex II for decommissioning post-2020 due to age and environmental inefficiencies, removing 205 MWe from the active baseline.
