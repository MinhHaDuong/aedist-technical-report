### Per-Asset Explanatory Notes

**Quảng Trạch II**
Originally slated as a coal-fired plant under PDP7a, PDP8 (Decision 500/QĐ-TTg) explicitly flags Quảng Trạch II for conversion to LNG. EVN is currently evaluating fuel supply chain feasibility. The 1500 MW capacity reflects the updated LNG target, and the technology column reflects this transitional planning status.

**Long Phú I**
Developed by PVN, this 1200 MW project has suffered severe delays primarily due to US sanctions impacting the original EPC contractor (Technopromexport) and subsequent difficulties in securing replacement contractors and financing. PDP8 retains it in the "under construction" priority list, but commercial operation dates remain highly fluid.

**Sơn Mỹ I & II**
These massive 4500 MW combined LNG/CCGT complexes in Bình Thuận are cornerstones of PDP8's gas expansion. Originally assigned to AES and other consortiums under BOT frameworks, they face ongoing delays tied to LNG import terminal infrastructure and PPA negotiations. They are classified as "Announced" with a 2030 horizon.

**Ô Môn Gas Cluster (I, II, III, IV)**
The Ô Môn complex in Cần Thơ is fundamentally tied to the development of the Block B & O Mon gas pipeline project. While Ô Môn I is operating (partially on oil/diesel fallback), units II, III, and IV are strictly gated to the upstream gas field development. Report 32 and Report 58 explicitly note 3-to-5-year delays to synchronize with the Block B gas timeline.

**Nam Định I & Sông Hậu II**
Both are large-scale BOT coal projects (1200 MW and 2120 MW respectively) that have stalled due to a combination of shrinking international coal finance, shareholder restructuring, and provincial environmental pushback. PDP8 Table 3 categorizes them explicitly as delayed projects facing severe capital and shareholder difficulties.

**Cẩm Thanh**
A 600 MW coal project in Quảng Nam that has failed to reach financial close or begin construction. The Ministry of Industry and Trade (MOIT) issued a strict ultimatum in PDP8 documentation: if no progress is made by mid-2024, the project's investment registration will be terminated. It is marked as "Shelved".

**Cẩm Phả III**
Planned by TKV as a 440 MW expansion (2x220 MW) in PDP7a for 2020, the project has failed to secure a confirmed site or environmental approvals. Report 32 notes it has "no site confirmed" and is delayed indefinitely.
