### Explanatory notes

**Capacity reconciliation.** Operating coal rows sum to **26,781 MWe**, within **24 MWe** of EVN’s reported **26,757 MW** coal-fired capacity at end-2024 in the EVN 2025 annual-report system table. The small difference is attributable to source rounding and plant-level gross/net conventions: Na Dương 1 is given as 111 MW in EVN 2010-2011 but normalized here to 110 MW, several PDP sources use 600 MW while operator sources use 622/622.5 MW for some units, and Vân Phong 1 is represented as 1,432 MW from PDP8/E-542 rather than a rounded 1,320 MW. The operating domestic-gas rows excluding oil-to-gas Ô Môn 1 and retired legacy turbines sum to **6,374 MWe**; adding Ô Môn 1’s 660 MW future gas conversion and the retired/simple-cycle legacy rows explains most of EVN’s broader “gas + oil fired” category, which EVN reports as **8,653 MW** for 2024.

**Status temporality.** Operating statuses are as of **2024-12-31** unless a later row-specific event is stated. PDP8 future-project statuses use **2024-04-01** when anchored to Decision 262/QĐ-TTg, the PDP8 implementation-plan decision date. PDP8 original-list-only or potential-site rows use **2023-05-15**, the PDP8 approval date. Older draft-study-only rows use **2020-12-01**.

**Cẩm Thanh / Công Thanh.** The troubled 600 MW Thanh Hóa coal project appears as “Cẩm Thanh” in PDP8 table 3 but as “Công Thanh” in earlier PDP7A and progress-report materials. It is represented as one row to avoid double-counting until a later official document proves they are separate projects.

**Ô Môn 1.** The plant is an existing oil-fired asset, but PDP8 explicitly includes it in the domestic-gas list with a note that it will convert to Lô B gas. It is therefore classified as **Domestic gas / Permitted** rather than as an operating oil asset.

**Long Phú 1.** PDP8 still lists Long Phú 1 among coal plants under construction, but E-542 records that it was largely complete and suspended. Without a verified restart document, it is classified as **Shelved**.

**LNG potential sites.** Chân Mây, Mũi Kê Gà, Tân Phước, Bến Tre and new Cà Mau LNG are included only as **Announced / LOW** because PDP8 identifies them as potential backup locations rather than firm priority projects.
