### Per-Asset or Per-Project Explanatory Notes

**Vũng Áng-2 Thermal Power Plant**
- **Confidence:** HIGH
- **Development History:** Vũng Áng-2 is a 1,320 MW ultra-supercritical coal plant developed under a BOT model by VAPCO (Mitsubishi Corp. 60%, KEPCO 40%). The project was launched in 2007, entered construction in 2021, and was officially inaugurated on April 20, 2026. The first turbine started operations in 2025 but was delayed by a storm, pushing commercial operations to April 2026.

**Quảng Trạch 1 Thermal Power Plant**
- **Confidence:** HIGH
- **Development History:** Quảng Trạch 1 is a 1,403 MW supercritical coal plant developed by EVN. Unit 1 was connected to the national grid on April 12, 2026, with commercial operation expected in May 2026. Unit 2 is slated for COD in October 2026. The project is a major component of Vietnam’s power development plan and is expected to play a crucial role in national energy security.

**Nhơn Trạch 3 & 4 Thermal Power Plant**
- **Confidence:** HIGH
- **Development History:** Nhơn Trạch 3 & 4 is Vietnam’s first LNG-fired power plant, with a total capacity of 1,624 MW (2 × 812 MW). Developed by PV Power, the plant began commercial operation on January 5, 2026. It is a combined cycle gas turbine (CCGT) plant, marking a significant step in Vietnam’s transition from coal to gas.

**Vĩnh Tân-3 Thermal Power Plant**
- **Confidence:** LOW
- **Development History:** Vĩnh Tân-3 was originally planned as a 1,980 MW supercritical coal plant. As of January 2026, its status remains unclear, with the Ministry of Industry and Trade continuing to evaluate whether the project will proceed. Conflicting reports and lack of recent primary source confirmation result in a LOW confidence rating.

**Vũng Áng-3 Thermal Power Plant**
- **Confidence:** LOW
- **Development History:** Vũng Áng-3 was proposed for conversion from coal to gas (CCGT). As of January 2026, the project is Shelved, with no recent primary source confirmation. Conflicting reports and lack of clarity on status result in a LOW confidence rating.

**Công Thanh Thermal Power Plant**
- **Confidence:** MEDIUM
- **Development History:** Originally planned as a 1,200 MW coal plant, Công Thanh is now slated for conversion to LNG (CCGT). The project has secured capital and is in the Permitted status as of 2026. Confirmed by PDP8 and project developer.
