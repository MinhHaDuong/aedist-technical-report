### Explanatory Notes for Selected Assets

**Nhiệt điện Long Phú 1 (Long Phu 1 TPP):** Status is **Construction** with **MEDIUM** confidence. While physical works are partially complete and PV Power’s Q1 2024 investor update confirms ongoing site activity, the original Russian EPC contractor (Power Machines) was effectively suspended following international sanctions in 2022. As of June 2024, no admissible primary source (MOIT dispatch or government contract award notice) has confirmed a replacement EPC or revised completion timeline. The status relies on commercial operator reporting without independent regulatory validation within the past 12 months, triggering the MEDIUM ceiling per quality dimensions. Capacity (2×660 MW supercritical) remains HIGH confidence based on original approved FEED and PV Power audited disclosures.

**Nhiệt điện Sông Hậu 2 (Song Hau 2 TPP):** Status is **Pre-permit** with **LOW** confidence. This asset exemplifies provenance failure: while widely referenced in tertiary databases and trade press as “delayed” or “restructured,” no primary government document (MOIT approval, provincial investment certificate, or PDP8 annex entry) published after 2019 has been located to confirm its current legal standing. The project underwent developer changes (from Toyo Ink to potential Petrovietnam-led restructuring), but without an admissible source confirming the latest configuration or permit status, both existence as an active project and all attributes are marked LOW. It is retained in the inventory rather than omitted per scope requirements, but users should treat it as unverified.

**Nhà máy Điện LNG Bạc Liêu (Bac Lieu LNG Power Plant):** Status is **Announced** with **LOW** confidence. Despite significant media coverage and a 2019 MoU with the provincial government, this 3,200 MW LNG proposal was notably absent from the approved PDP8 (Decision 500/QĐ-TTg, May 2023), which serves as the definitive national generation plan. No subsequent MOIT approval or inclusion in adjusted planning documents has been found. The last admissible primary reference is a provincial support letter from 2020, now exceeding the 48-month freshness threshold. The project may be functionally cancelled or indefinitely deferred, but without a formal cancellation decree, it is recorded as Announced with LOW confidence. Users should distinguish this from other LNG projects that received explicit PDP8 listing.

**Nhiệt điện Vân Phong 1 (Van Phong 1 TPP):** Status is **Cancelled** with **HIGH** confidence. This represents one of the most significant thermal asset status changes in recent Vietnamese energy policy. Originally a permitted 1,320 MW imported coal BOT project led by Sumitomo Corporation, it was formally excluded from the national generation plan via Decision 500/QĐ-TTg (PDP8) in May 2023. Khanh Hoa Provincial Official Letter 2023 subsequently confirmed site reallocation for an integrated LNG/green hydrogen industrial complex. Both sources are independent primaries concordant on cancellation, satisfying HIGH confidence criteria. This asset is included despite cancellation per scope requirements covering shelved/cancelled projects.

**Nhà máy Điện khí Phú Mỹ 2.2 (Phu My 2.2 Gas Power Plant):** This row warrants note due to its unique **BOT/IPP** structure distinguishing it from adjacent state-owned Phu My units. Operated by Mekong Energy Company (a joint venture involving EDF and Tepco), it was Vietnam’s first major foreign IPP gas plant under a Build-Operate-Transfer contract. While operational status and capacity (960 MW CCGT) are HIGH confidence based on operator disclosures and BOT contract archives, the asset faces transitional uncertainty: the original BOT term approaches expiration circa 2028, and transfer-to-state conditions remain under negotiation per industry filings. Users analyzing long-term fleet availability should account for this contractual horizon, which does not affect current operational status but introduces medium-term disposition risk not captured in standard status fields.
