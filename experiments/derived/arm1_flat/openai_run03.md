### Per-asset or per-project explanatory notes

- **Pha Lai, Uong Bi, Ninh Binh and other legacy northern coal stations.** Row-level existence is **HIGH** for Pha Lai and Uong Bi because EVN’s official annual-report/system materials and EVN coal-fleet environmental documentation confirm continuing coal-fired capacity at system level and EVN plant coverage. Unit-level COD and legacy unit configuration are **MEDIUM** where not repeated in recent official sources; older coal units may have been derated, mothballed, or operated seasonally.

- **Vinacomin/TKV small coal fleet: Na Duong, Cao Ngan, Son Dong, Cam Pha and Mao Khe.** Existence is **MEDIUM to HIGH**: EVN’s annual report gives official ownership/category capacity for Vinacomin as part of the national power system, while PDP8 implementation identifies Na Duong II as a future project and thereby confirms the existing Na Duong context. Individual plant capacities are widely published but not all were matched to primary plant sheets in this pass, so non-Na Duong rows are kept at **MEDIUM** rather than **HIGH**.

- **Vinh Tan power center.** The table separates Vinh Tan 1, Vinh Tan 2, Vinh Tan 4 and Vinh Tan 3 because they have different ownership, financing, operating status and project histories. Vinh Tan 3 is **MEDIUM** for existence and planned capacity because it remains in the Decision 262 coal-difficulty list, but its current status is classed **Shelved**: official text records delay and difficulty, while no formal cancellation instrument was verified.

- **Duyen Hai power center.** Duyen Hai 1, Duyen Hai 3 and Duyen Hai 3 Extension are separated because the extension is a distinct one-unit project with different technology and COD. Row confidence is **HIGH** for operating status because EVN system documents and EVN coal-fleet sources corroborate the complex.

- **Van Phong 1.** Row-level existence/status/capacity is **HIGH** because the PDP8 implementation coal appendix explicitly lists Van Phong 1 at 1,432 MW and “being operated” in 2024. The status as-of-date is therefore tied to Decision 262/implementation-plan publication, not an independently checked plant COD certificate.

- **Vung Ang 2, Quang Trach 1 and Long Phu 1.** These are retained coal projects in the current implementation plan. Capacity and expected COD are **HIGH** where the official implementation appendix gives values; status is **HIGH** for Quang Trach 1 as “under construction” in the appendix, **HIGH/MEDIUM** for Vung Ang 2 and Long Phu 1 depending on whether the claim is “retained in plan” or actual site progress. Long Phu 1 has exceptional schedule risk from earlier EPC/sanctions and financing delays.

- **Cong Thanh.** Existence and capacity are **MEDIUM** because Decision 262’s coal appendix lists 600 MW as a difficult project. Future fuel is **LOW**: the appendix note says Thanh Hoa proposed conversion to gas turbine, so the coal classification is retained only because the official coal list still names the project in that category.

- **Quang Tri coal project.** The row is **MEDIUM**: the Decision 262 appendix records 1,320 MW and says the investor requested to stop, with a deadline for implementation or legal termination review. Because this wording stops short of a verified cancellation decision, the project is classified **Shelved**, not **Cancelled**.

- **Nam Dinh 1 and Song Hau 2.** These are major delayed BOT coal projects. Capacity is **MEDIUM to HIGH** from the official implementation coal appendix, but status is **MEDIUM** because “behind schedule/facing difficulties” does not map perfectly to a GEM status. “Shelved” is the best fit absent evidence of active construction or formal cancellation.

- **Formosa Ha Tinh Power Plant.** This is included to avoid omission of large captive thermal capacity. Confidence is **LOW** because no admissible current primary source was located in this pass for exact electrical capacity, unit count, grid export status, or current operating configuration.

- **Phu My complex.** The table separates Phu My 1, 2.1, 2.2, 3 and 4 because ownership differs substantially, including BOT plants. Row-level existence is **HIGH** from EVN gas/system context, but some unit configurations are **MEDIUM** because official annual reports aggregate gas capacity rather than reproducing every block.

- **O Mon 1.** The fuel classification is intentionally **Domestic gas** even though the plant historically used fuel oil before full Block B gas availability. Decision 262’s domestic-gas project table lists O Mon I as an existing 660 MW plant to use Block B gas, so the row follows the planning basis. Current fuel use at any month may differ.

- **O Mon 2, 3 and 4.** These are Block B gas-chain projects. Capacity, province and planned operation years are **HIGH** from Decision 262 summaries. Actual construction status is conservative: O Mon 4 may have advanced after 2024, but because this inventory did not verify a primary construction notice, status remains **Permitted** as of the official implementation-plan date.

- **Dung Quat 1-3, Central Region 1-2 and Quang Tri CCGT.** These future domestic gas plants are **HIGH** for inclusion and headline capacity because Decision 262 lists them; they are **LOW to MEDIUM** for exact site, investor and final unit configuration because the plan itself says exact scale will be determined during project implementation.

- **Nhon Trach 3 and 4.** These are the first major imported-LNG power projects in Vietnam’s plan and are treated separately as two plant rows. The table uses **Construction** as of 2024 because the official implementation plan and Decision 262 summaries support inclusion and near-term schedule. Later market reports suggest operation or commissioning may have occurred, but a primary Vietnamese COD document was not verified here.

- **Bac Lieu LNG.** The project is **MEDIUM** for existence/capacity as a major PDP8 LNG project but **MEDIUM/LOW** for schedule. It has faced prolonged PPA, tariff and guarantee issues; without a recent primary construction notice, the table uses **Permitted** rather than **Construction**.

- **Son My 1 and Son My 2 LNG.** Capacity is **HIGH** because PDP8 materials list each at 2,250 MW. Status is **Permitted** rather than **Construction** because implementation depends on LNG terminal, PPA and financing milestones that were not primary-verified as complete in this pass.

- **Ca Na LNG, Thai Binh LNG and Nghi Son LNG.** These rows are **LOW**. They are included because they are known planning/proposal leads and revised PDP8 increased LNG ambitions, but exact project handles, investor assignments and current legal status were not located in primary-source form. They should be treated as placeholders for follow-up verification, not bankable project records.

- **Cancelled coal rows: Quynh Lap 1, Long Phu 2 and Long Phu 3.** These are included because they are historically important PDP7-era projects. Classification as **Cancelled** is based on exclusion from the retained PDP8 coal project list and the policy shift away from new coal. Confidence is **MEDIUM** for Quynh Lap 1 and **LOW** for Long Phu 2/3 because the exact cancellation decisions or investor withdrawal notices were not verified.
