### **Per-Asset Explanatory Notes**

#### **Vũng Áng 3 Thermal Power Plant**
- **Status:** Cancelled under PDP8.
- **Confidence:** LOW. The project was proposed as a 2×600 MW USC coal plant, but was cancelled in favor of conversion to gas. No construction started, and the project does not appear in PDP8. Sources: MOIT, Samsung, and Ha Tinh Provincial People’s Committee statements (2020–2021) 21,40.

#### **Long Phú 1, 2, 3 Thermal Power Plants**
- **Status:** Long Phú 1 and 3 are shelved; Long Phú 2 is cancelled.
- **Confidence:** LOW. All three projects were proposed as supercritical coal plants (1,200–1,800 MW), but were not included in PDP8. Long Phú 2 was formally cancelled in 2020. Long Phú 3 was proposed for investor transfer to China Southern Grid, but no progress has been reported since 2020 28,42.

#### **Vĩnh Tân 3 Thermal Power Plant**
- **Status:** Shelved.
- **Confidence:** LOW. The project was proposed as a 3×660 MW supercritical coal plant, but was not included in PDP8. No construction has started, and the project is not listed in the PDP8 Implementation Plan 8,16,43.

#### **Quảng Ninh 3 Thermal Power Plant**
- **Status:** Cancelled.
- **Confidence:** LOW. The project was proposed as a 2×600 MW supercritical coal plant, but was not included in PDP8 and has seen no progress since 2019 86.

#### **Hải Phòng 3 Thermal Power Plant**
- **Status:** Cancelled.
- **Confidence:** LOW. The project was proposed as a 2×600 MW supercritical coal plant, but was not included in PDP8. In 2022, it was reported that the project would be converted to gas, but no construction has started 41.

#### **Sông Hậu 2 Thermal Power Plant**
- **Status:** Cancelled.
- **Confidence:** LOW. The project was proposed as a 2×1,000 MW supercritical coal plant, but was not included in PDP8 and has seen no progress since 2016 75,84.

#### **Phả Lại 3 Thermal Power Plant**
- **Status:** Shelved.
- **Confidence:** LOW. The project was proposed as a 1×600 MW supercritical coal plant, but was not included in PDP8 and has seen no progress since 2021 43.

#### **Công Thanh Thermal Power Plant**
- **Status:** Shelved.
- **Confidence:** LOW. The project was proposed as a 2×600 MW USC coal plant, but PDP8 confirms conversion to gas. No construction has started 14.

#### **Long An 1 & 2 LNG Power Plants**
- **Status:** Shelved.
- **Confidence:** LOW. The projects were proposed as 2×1,500 MW CCGT LNG plants, but financing has not been finalized and the projects are not included in PDP8 27.
