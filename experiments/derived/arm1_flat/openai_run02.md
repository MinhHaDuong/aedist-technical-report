### Per-asset / project explanatory notes

**Pha Lai 1–2.** Row-level existence is **LOW** only because an admissible primary page was not located during this run, not because the asset is doubtful. Pha Lai is Vietnam’s historically important northern coal station; the two-phase split is retained because Pha Lai 1 and Pha Lai 2 differ materially in unit size and commissioning period. Capacity and COD should be treated as secondary/legacy values until confirmed from EVNGENCO2 or Pha Lai Thermal Power JSC disclosures.

**Vinacomin / TKV CFB plants: Na Duong, Cao Ngan, Son Dong, Cam Pha, Mao Khe and Nong Son.** Existence/capacity confidence is **MEDIUM** for the >30 MW plants because a Vinacomin-origin report identifies the CFB fleet and capacities: Na Duong 110 MW, Cao Ngan 115 MW, Son Dong 220 MW, Cam Pha 680 MW, Mao Khe 440 MW and Nong Son 30 MW. The Japanese JOGMEC item repeats the same data, so it is useful corroboration for discovery but not independent for HIGH confidence. Technology is recorded as “Subcritical” because the requested vocabulary does not include CFB; technically, these are circulating-fluidized-bed coal units. ([vietnamenergy.vn](https://vietnamenergy.vn/vinacomin-has-successfully-applied-cfb-technology-in-the-thermal-power-plants-18781.html?utm_source=openai))

**Mong Duong 1 and Mong Duong 2.** Mong Duong 1 is an EVNGENCO3 asset and current operation is supported by EVNGENCO3 performance reporting; however, the exact 1,080-MW unit configuration was not captured from a plant page, so capacity remains lower confidence than status. Mong Duong 2 is a BOT coal project; the row is **LOW** because no admissible operator or government source was located here for owner/COD/capacity.

**Vinh Tan complex.** Vinh Tan 2 is the strongest-sourced row: EVNGENCO3 states that Vinh Tan Thermal Power Company manages Vinh Tan 2 with 1,244 MW, and discusses 2024 operating performance. Vinh Tan 4 is sourced to EVNGENCO3’s project/O&M statements as 1,200 MW, but the owner/O&M boundary is less clean. Vinh Tan 3 is classified as **Cancelled** because it does not appear in PDP8’s active coal buildout, but a project-specific cancellation order was not verified; if strict legal cancellation is required, downgrade to **Shelved**. ([genco3.com](https://www.genco3.com/en-US/introduction/operations.html?utm_source=openai))

**PV Power gas fleet: Ca Mau 1–2, Nhon Trach 1–2, Nhon Trach 3–4.** Ca Mau 1–2 and Nhon Trach 2 are **HIGH** confidence because PV Power’s plant pages give capacity, location, owner and COD. Nhon Trach 1 capacity is inferred from PV Power’s statement that Ca Mau 1&2, Nhon Trach 1 and Nhon Trach 2 total 2,700 MW, combined with sourced Ca Mau 1&2 at 1,500 MW and Nhon Trach 2 at 750 MW; existence/status is strong, capacity is inferential. Nhon Trach 3–4 are **HIGH** for existence/development because PV Power identifies the project as Vietnam’s first LNG-fuelled power-plant project, at 1,624 MW total / 812 MW each, with EPC, financing and PPA milestones; exact COD remains uncertain. ([pvpower.vn](https://pvpower.vn/en/post/pv-power-takes-the-initiative-in-power-plant-optimal-operating-solutions-5397.htm?utm_source=openai))

**Phu My complex.** This is intentionally split into Phu My 1, 2.1, 2.2, 3 and 4 because ownership, BOT structure and commissioning histories differ. EVNGENCO3 evidence confirms continuing Phu My operations and 2024 LNG consumption by the Phu My plants, and separately mentions O&M for Phu My 3, but the exact unit-by-unit capacity allocation was not fully primary-verified in this run. Treat the individual capacity cells as **LOW–MEDIUM** while the existence/status of the complex is **MEDIUM**. ([genco3.com](https://www.genco3.com/baocaothuongnien_EN/tong-quan-ve-evngenco3.html?utm_source=openai))

**O Mon / Block B gas chain.** O Mon 1 is operating but historically constrained by lack of domestic gas and use of oil/alternate fuels; it is nevertheless classified under Domestic gas because the asset belongs to the Block B/O Mon gas-to-power chain. O Mon IV is more strongly supported by PDP8 implementation summaries, which list O Mon IV at 1,050 MW in Can Tho for 2028. O Mon II and III remain implementation-sensitive and should be rechecked against EVN/MOIT procurement documents. ([frasersvn.com](https://www.frasersvn.com/legal-updates-and-publications/pdp-viii-implementation-plan?utm_source=openai))

**PDP8 LNG projects.** The LNG rows for Quang Ninh, Thai Binh, Nghi Son, Quang Trach II, Quynh Lap/Nghi Son, Hai Lang Phase 1 and Son My II are **HIGH** for planned capacity/status because PDP8 Appendix II lists them by name, capacity and development window. Their owner/developer fields are much weaker unless a project-specific primary source is cited; where not verified, “to be determined” is used. PDP8’s Appendix II explicitly notes that Quang Trach II was converted to LNG and that Quynh Lap/Nghi Son remains a potential-location item. ([vepg.vn](https://vepg.vn/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PDP8_full-with-annexes_EN.pdf?utm_source=openai))

**Coal projects converted, removed, shelved or cancelled under PDP8.** Quynh Lap coal, Quang Trach II coal and several earlier PDP7 coal proposals are treated as cancelled/superseded because PDP8 constrains future coal and replaces some sites with LNG. For rows without a project-specific cancellation order, confidence is capped at **MEDIUM** or **LOW** and the Notes field distinguishes “removed/replaced in planning” from formal cancellation.

**Captive and industrial thermal plants.** Formosa Ha Tinh and similar captive units are included because the scope requests thermal assets >30 MWe at any lifecycle stage, not only grid-connected EVN plants. Evidence is **LOW** here because official industrial-generation capacity pages were not located.
