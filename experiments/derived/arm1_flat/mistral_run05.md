### Per-Asset or Per-Project Explanatory Notes

**Vung Ang 2 Thermal Power Plant**
- **Confidence:** HIGH
- **Development History:** The Vung Ang 2 project was launched in 2007 and entered construction in 2021. It is a 1,320 MW ultra-supercritical coal-fired power plant developed under a BOT model by a joint venture between Mitsubishi Corporation (60%) and KEPCO (40%). The first turbine started operations in 2025 but was delayed by a storm. The plant was officially inaugurated on April 20, 2026, with full commercial operation expected in Q3 2025. The project is part of Vietnam’s national power development plan (PDP VIII) and is expected to transition to biomass and ammonia by 2050 as per Vietnam’s sustainable development goals52,85,89.

**Nhon Trach 3 & 4 Power Plant**
- **Confidence:** HIGH
- **Development History:** Nhon Trach 3 & 4 is Vietnam’s first LNG-fueled combined-cycle gas turbine (CCGT) power plant, with a total capacity of 1,600 MW. The plant began commercial operation on January 5, 2026, and is owned by PetroVietnam Power Corporation (PV Power). It was built by Samsung C&T and Lilama, using GE Vernova’s 9HA.02 gas turbines. The plant is designed for fuel flexibility, capable of co-firing up to 50% hydrogen and transitioning to 100% hydrogen in the future, aligning with Vietnam’s net-zero goals. The project marks a significant step in Vietnam’s transition from coal to gas and renewable energy sources21,51,23,25.

**Quang Trach 1 Thermal Power Plant**
- **Confidence:** HIGH
- **Development History:** Quang Trach 1 is a 1,200 MW supercritical coal-fired power plant under construction in Quang Binh province. The project is being developed by PetroVietnam (PVN) and is expected to connect its first unit to the national grid in April 2026, with commercial operation beginning in May 2026. Unit 2 is expected to follow in October 2026. The plant has faced delays due to land acquisition and financing challenges but is now on track for completion as per recent government directives and EVN reports48,57,83.

**Vinh Tan 3 Thermal Power Plant**
- **Confidence:** LOW
- **Development History:** Vinh Tan 3 was planned as a 1,200 MW supercritical coal-fired power plant in Binh Thuan province. The project has been categorized as "under development" by Mitsubishi, but as of January 2026, its fate remains unclear. The Ministry of Industry and Trade (MOIT) is still evaluating whether to proceed with the project, which has faced financing difficulties and is not included in the latest PDP8. Confidence is LOW due to the lack of recent progress and unresolved status67,19.

**Hai Phong 3 Thermal Power Plant**
- **Confidence:** LOW
- **Development History:** Originally planned as a 1,500 MW coal-fired power plant, Hai Phong 3 has been shelved and is now considering a pivot to LNG. The project was delayed due to financing and regulatory challenges, and as of 2026, there is no clear timeline for its development. Confidence is LOW due to the lack of recent progress and uncertainty regarding its fuel source and status44.

**Pha Lai 3 Thermal Power Plant**
- **Confidence:** LOW
- **Development History:** Pha Lai 3 was proposed as a 600 MW supercritical coal-fired power plant in Hai Duong province. The project was delayed due to difficulties in mobilizing capital and is not included in the PDP8. It is now considered shelved, with no clear path forward. Confidence is LOW due to the lack of recent progress and financing challenges43.
