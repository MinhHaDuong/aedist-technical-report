### Explanatory Notes for Selected Assets

**Nhà máy Nhiệt điện Thái Bình 2**  
*Confidence: HIGH.* This 1200 MWe supercritical coal plant represents the most significant thermal commissioning in Vietnam’s recent history. Full commercial operation was declared in April 2023 after extensive delays stemming from EPC contractor (Lilama) capacity issues and financing restructuring. Primary confirmation derives from PV Power Board Resolution 15/NQ-HĐQT and NLDC commercial operation notices. While some secondary sources cite partial load testing in late 2022, the authoritative COD for inventory purposes is the full commercial acceptance date in 2023. The plant is critical for northern grid reliability and is fully accounted for in PDP8 baseload projections.

**Dự án Nhiệt điện Long Phú 1**  
*Confidence: LOW.* Although officially listed as “Construction,” this asset warrants a LOW confidence designation regarding its timeline and effective status. The Russian EPC contractor Power Machines has been unable to complete turbine supply due to international sanctions and payment mechanism failures. PV Power’s April 2024 shareholder minutes acknowledge “unresolved external impediments” without providing a revised COD. No independent primary source confirms active installation work in 2024. The project remains legally alive but functionally stalled; users should treat any pre-2027 COD estimate as speculative. This contrasts with Nam Dinh I, where EPC remobilization is physically verified.

**Nhà máy Điện khí Phú Mỹ 3**  
*Confidence: MEDIUM.* Operational status is unambiguous and confirmed via NLDC dispatch data. However, current ownership documentation meets only MEDIUM confidence criteria. Originally developed as a BOT by a BP/EDF/Sumitomo consortium, BP divested its stake circa 2018. While industry trackers attribute current ownership to a combination of remaining original sponsors and new investors, no 2023–2024 primary corporate filing or Vietnamese business registration update has been independently verified for this inventory. The hard ceiling rule applies: absent fresh primary confirmation of equity structure, confidence cannot exceed MEDIUM despite high operational certainty.

**Dự án Nhiệt điện Bạc Liêu (LNG)**  
*Confidence: LOW.* This 3200 MWe proposal appears in numerous tertiary databases and developer marketing materials but lacks corresponding primary government endorsement in the finalized PDP8 (Decision 500/QĐ-TTg) or subsequent MOIT implementation directives. The developer’s website lists ambitious timelines, but provincial and national planning documents do not corroborate these milestones. Recorded here as “Announced” rather than omitted, per scope requirements to include known projects even without primary sourcing, but all attributes carry LOW confidence. Users should distinguish this from the similarly named but distinct Bac Lieu *wind* projects which are well-documented.

**Nhơn Trạch 3 & 4 LNG Power Plant**  
*Confidence: HIGH.* As Vietnam’s first LNG-to-power facility, this asset’s status is extensively documented. EPC progress reports through June 2024 confirm mechanical completion targets for late 2024, but PV Power’s own investor briefings acknowledge slippage of full COD to 2025 due to LNG import infrastructure synchronization delays. The table records “Construction” status as-of June 2024 with planned COD 2025, reflecting the most recent primary guidance. Earlier secondary sources citing 2024 COD are superseded by operator disclosures. This project serves as the benchmark for all subsequent LNG power developments in the inventory.
