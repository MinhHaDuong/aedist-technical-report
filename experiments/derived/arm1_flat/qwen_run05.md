### Explanatory Notes for Selected Assets

**Nhà máy Nhiệt điện Long Phú 1 (Long Phu 1 TPP)**  
*Status: Construction | Confidence: MEDIUM.* The project’s EPC contractor, Russian firm Power Machines, ceased performance in 2022 following international sanctions related to the Ukraine conflict. While Petrocons (state-owned contractor) has publicly stated intent to resume works with alternative contractors as of Q1/2024 board resolutions, no official Ministry of Industry and Trade (MOIT) dispatch confirming EPC reassignment or revised completion timeline has been published in over 12 months. Status remains “Construction” rather than “Shelved” because the project retains its PDP8 listing and site permits remain valid, but confidence is capped at MEDIUM due to absence of fresh regulatory confirmation. Capacity (1320 MWe) is HIGH confidence per original investment certificate and PDP8 annex.

**Nhà máy Nhiệt điện Vân Phong 1 (Van Phong 1 TPP)**  
*Status: Cancelled | Confidence: HIGH.* This 1320 MWe BOT coal project was formally excluded from the Power Development Plan VIII (PDP8) approved under Decision 500/QĐ-TTg on May 15, 2023. Developer Sumitomo Corporation confirmed withdrawal in its May 2023 IR briefing, citing inability to reconcile coal financing with decarbonization commitments and Vietnamese regulatory shifts. Cancellation is among the most definitively sourced in the inventory, supported by both national policy document and corporate primary disclosure.

**Nhà máy Điện khí Bạc Liêu (Bac Lieu LNG-to-Power)**  
*Total Capacity: 3200 MWe vs. 800 MWe | Confidence: LOW.* Original provincial investment certificate (2020) approved four phases totaling 3200 MWe. However, PDP8 (Decision 500/QĐ-TTg, May 2023) includes only an initial 800 MWe phase without a specified COD year, omitting subsequent phases entirely. No reconciling document from MOIT or Bac Lieu PPC explaining the reduction or phasing adjustment has been located. The row reflects the originally announced capacity but flags the discrepancy. Users should treat any reference to 3200 MWe as aspirational until PDP8 implementation decrees clarify scope.

**Nhà máy Nhiệt điện Nam Định 1 (Nam Dinh 1 TPP)**  
*Status: Shelved | Confidence: LOW.* Approved in principle in 2017 with Taekwang and ACWA Power as developers, the project never progressed to environmental impact assessment approval. It is absent from PDP8’s final approved project list. However, no formal cancellation decision from MOIT or Nam Dinh Provincial People’s Committee has been identified in primary sources. Current status classification relies solely on trade press (VnExpress, 2021) and tertiary trackers referencing it. Until an official administrative document confirms termination, status remains uncertain.

**Nhà máy Nhiệt điện Ô Môn III & IV (O Mon III & IV)**  
*Status: Permitted | Confidence: MEDIUM.* Both plants are listed in PDP8 as domestic gas/LNG CCGT projects dependent on Block B – O Mon gas chain development. EVN Genco 2’s 2024 strategic plan classifies them as “permitted and awaiting FID,” but this status is contingent on upstream gas project sanctioning, which itself faces repeated delays. No independent MOIT permit issuance record for 2023–2024 has been cross-referenced. If Block B FID slips beyond 2025, these assets may effectively revert to Pre-permit. Capacity (750 MWe each) is stable across all sources.
