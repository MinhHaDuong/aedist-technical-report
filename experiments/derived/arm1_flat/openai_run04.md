### Per-asset / project explanatory notes

- **Pha Lai 1 and Pha Lai 2 — MEDIUM existence/status confidence.** Pha Lai is historically important as Vietnam’s first large coal complex. EVNGENCO2’s 2024 AGM report, via EVN, specifically mentions a fuel-conversion study for **Pha Lai 1**, which supports continuing relevance but also signals environmental/operational pressure. Capacity and COD are retained from EVN plant-list material; because the most accessible capacity source is older than 48 months, fine-grained operating status is capped at MEDIUM/LOW depending on unit.

- **Na Duong II — HIGH existence/status confidence, MEDIUM capacity confidence.** The Lạng Sơn official document cites PDP8 implementation work and states Na Duong II as under construction, but it contains both **110 MW** and **100 MW** references. I used **110 MW** in the capacity column because it appears in the “thermal plant under construction” framing; the inconsistency is recorded in the Notes cell. ([caoloc.langson.gov.vn](https://caoloc.langson.gov.vn/upload/105426/20240807/T4-chi_dao_thuc_hien_Quy_hoach_dien_VIII__Ke_hoach_thuc_hien_dien_VIII_fe976.pdf?utm_source=openai))

- **Mong Duong 1 — HIGH confidence.** EVNGENCO3’s recent annual-report materials repeatedly discuss Mong Duong 1 as an operating managed plant, including ash/slag utilization, heat-rate reduction, and financing/loan restructuring, supporting both existence and current operation. ([genco3.com](https://www.genco3.com/data/files/20250623/638862878615362930_0.pdf?utm_source=openai))

- **Vinh Tan complex — HIGH for EVNGENCO3 assets; MEDIUM/LOW for BOT/cancelled units.** Vinh Tan 2 is strongly supported by EVNGENCO3, which states **2×622 MW** and early-2015 commercial operation. Vinh Tan 4 and Vinh Tan 4 Extension are operating, with EVNGENCO3 reporting O&M contract activity. Vinh Tan 3 is treated as cancelled/superseded because final PDP8 implementation materials retain no such new coal unit and instead constrain coal to the committed fleet; this is a planning inference, so the cancelled row is MEDIUM rather than HIGH. ([genco3.com](https://www.genco3.com/baocaothuongnien_EN/tong-quan-ve-evngenco3.html?utm_source=openai))

- **Long Phu projects — mixed confidence.** Long Phu 1 remains a committed coal project in planning summaries, but its EPC history and sanctions-related delays make status uncertain; I mark it Construction with MEDIUM confidence. Long Phu III appeared in draft PDP8 as a **2,000 MW coal** project, but final PDP8 implementation materials no longer support it as an active coal project, so it is recorded as Cancelled with MEDIUM confidence. ([vepg.vn](https://vepg.vn/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/5.9.2021-Draft-PDP8_En.pdf?utm_source=openai))

- **Phu My complex — HIGH for operating existence; MEDIUM for some unit aggregation.** EVNGENCO3’s historical overview states the Phu My complex includes **Phu My 2.1**, **Phu My 2.1 Extension**, **Phu My 1**, and **Phu My 4**, while 2024 EVNGENCO3 materials discuss Phu My operations and LNG use in the dry season. I separated BOT Phu My 2.2 and Phu My 3 because BOT/O&M arrangements are distinct; Phu My 2.1 and its extension are aggregated because the consulted primary text does not cleanly separate them. ([genco3.com](https://www.genco3.com/baocaothuongnien_EN/tong-quan-ve-evngenco3.html?utm_source=openai))

- **O Mon gas chain — HIGH for O Mon I, MEDIUM for future O Mon II–IV.** PDP8 implementation summaries identify **O Mon I, 660 MW** as in operation and using gas from Block B. EVNGENCO2’s 2024 AGM comments also mention the fuel-conversion project for O Mon I. Future O Mon II–IV are linked to the Block B gas chain; status and COD are schedule-sensitive and therefore MEDIUM. ([frasersvn.com](https://www.frasersvn.com/legal-updates-and-publications/pdp-viii-implementation-plan?utm_source=openai))

- **Nhon Trach 3 and 4 — HIGH project existence; MEDIUM current schedule.** PDP8 implementation materials list **Nhon Trach 3 and 4** at **1,624 MW** total, with originally stated operation milestones of Q4/2024 and Q2/2025. PV Power annual-report material confirms the developer context. Because those milestones have been moving, the table uses Construction with a later planned COD range and notes the schedule slippage. ([vepg.vn](https://vepg.vn/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/2_VN_VEA-_-Hoi-nghi-_5_2023.pdf?utm_source=openai))

- **Imported LNG portfolio — MEDIUM overall, LOW where location/name is ambiguous.** PDP8 implementation and revised-PDP8 summaries list Vietnam’s major LNG projects, including Quang Ninh, Thai Binh, Nghi Son, Quang Trach II, Hai Lang, Ca Na, Son My I/II, Hiep Phuoc, Long An I/II, Bac Lieu, Long Son, and Cong Thanh conversion. Several are “Announced” rather than banked/constructed, and PDP8 materials themselves often flag “after 2030” feasibility risk; therefore, most are MEDIUM for existence in planning but not for COD. ([vepg.vn](https://vepg.vn/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/2_VN_VEA-_-Hoi-nghi-_5_2023.pdf?utm_source=openai))

- **Cong Thanh — MEDIUM confidence.** This is historically important because it was originally a coal project and was later moved toward LNG. Revised PDP8 legal summaries and energy-plan summaries both mention the conversion and associated LNG terminal concept; capacity is recorded at **1,500 MW**. ([frasersvn.com](https://www.frasersvn.com/vi/legal-updates-and-publications/the-revised-pdp-8?utm_source=openai))

- **Captive and industrial thermal assets — LOW where included.** Formosa Ha Tinh and Hiep Phuoc are included because they are material thermal assets above the threshold, but unit-level primary documentation was not verified in this pass. Their rows are intentionally marked LOW rather than omitted.
