### Explanatory Notes

**Nhà máy Nhiệt điện Thái Bình 2 (Thai Binh 2)**
*   **Status/COD:** HIGH confidence. Unit 1 achieved commercial operation April 28, 2023, and Unit 2 on November 27, 2023, per MOIT Official Letter 2845/BCT-ĐTĐL and PV Power shareholder resolutions. This resolves years of uncertainty regarding the project’s completion under Petrocons management.
*   **Ownership:** MEDIUM confidence. Originally a Petrovietnam (PVN) subsidiary project, management was transferred to the State Capital Management Committee (CMSC) during restructuring. While PV Power remains the operational reference in financial disclosures, ultimate equity ownership is in transition. Sources reflect this hybrid state; PV Power FS 2023 still consolidates results but notes divestment intent.

**Dự án Nhiệt điện Long Phú 1 (Long Phu 1)**
*   **Status:** LOW confidence. Listed as "Construction" based on Petrocons Q1/2024 quarterly report which retains the project in "ongoing works." However, independent verification of physical progress is absent since Russian contractor Technopromexport faced sanctions-related disruptions in 2022. No MOIT dispatch in 2024 confirms active civil works. The status may effectively be "Shelved" de facto, but without formal government declaration, "Construction" is retained with LOW confidence.
*   **COD:** Unspecified. Original 2023 target missed. No revised date published in admissible primary source.

**Nhà máy Điện khí Nhơn Trạch 3 & 4 (Nhon Trach 3 & 4 LNG)**
*   **Fuel/Tech:** HIGH confidence. Confirmed as Vietnam’s first LNG-import CCGT project via PV Power Board Resolution 12/NQ-ĐHĐCĐ 2024 and EPC contract award documentation.
*   **COD:** MEDIUM confidence. Originally targeted for 2024, but PV Power’s 2024 shareholder resolution acknowledges slippage to 2025 due to global supply chain delays for Siemens turbines. Exact month unconfirmed; year-only precision reflected.

**Dự án Nhiệt điện Vân Phong 1 (Van Phong 1)**
*   **Status:** MEDIUM confidence. Construction resumed in October 2022 after suspension, confirmed by MOIT Dispatch 1892/BCT-ĐTĐL. However, no official progress update has been published in >12 months. Per quality dimensions, status attested only by source older than 24 months would be capped LOW; here the 2023 dispatch keeps it within 24-month window but approaching threshold. Freshness risk noted.

**Nhà máy Điện khí Bà Rịa (Ba Ria GT)**
*   **Capacity:** MEDIUM confidence. Aggregate 276 MW derived from EVNGENCO3 website asset summary. Individual unit configuration (4×60 + 1×36 vs. alternative configurations) varies across tertiary sources. No audited financial statement breaking down unit-level nameplate capacity post-refurbishment was located. Value accepted as best-available but flagged for granularity gap.
