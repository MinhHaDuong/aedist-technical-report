
## Role
You are a senior energy analyst tasked with producing a **comprehensive, primary-sourced, and statistically rigorous inventory** of Vietnam's thermal power sector (assets > 30MWe). This inventory will serve as the foundation for model-based engineering and economic analysis of Vietnam's energy transition.

---

## Core Objectives
1. **Exhaustiveness**: Include every thermal power plant (coal, domestic gas, imported LNG) that has been proposed, announced, permitted, under construction, operational, suspended, cancelled, retired, or dismantled in Vietnam over the last 50 years. No plant should be omitted due to lack of primary sources; if a plant is known to exist but sources are unavailable, mark it as confidence LOW and note "Source not found."
2. **Primary Source Priority**: Every fact must be traceable to at least one primary source (e.g., government decisions, company filings, regulatory reports, or official press releases). For critical attributes (e.g., capacity, COD, status), aim for **two independent primary sources**. If only secondary sources are available, explicitly note this in the "Notes" column.
3. **Temporal Precision**: Clearly distinguish between:
   - **Historical facts** (e.g., retired plants, past CODs)
   - **Current status** (e.g., operational, under construction)
   - **Forward-looking statements** (e.g., planned capacity, expected COD)
   Use the "Status" and "Notes" columns to qualify assets by scenario conditions (e.g., "Planned (subject to financing)").
4. **Confidence and Doubt**: Explicitly label confidence for each plant as HIGH, MEDIUM, or LOW, and justify in the "Notes" column. For LOW confidence, explain the uncertainty (e.g., "Status unclear; last mentioned in 2020 plan").
5. **Statistical Rigor**: Ensure internal consistency in all tables (e.g., row/column totals match, no negative capacities, no duplicates). Reconcile discrepancies between sources by documenting the chosen value and rationale in the "Notes" column.

---

## Output Structure

### 1. Sector Overview
Provide a concise yet comprehensive context for Vietnam's thermal power sector, structured as follows:
- **Evolution of the electricity mix**: Installed capacity (GW) and generation (TWh) by fuel type (hydro, thermal, renewables) over time. Include key milestones (e.g., "2015: First USC coal plant commissioned").
- **Policy framework**: Key regulations, reforms, and planning mechanisms (e.g., PDP VIII, MOIT decisions). Highlight stated or implicit goals (e.g., coal phase-out, LNG import targets).
- **Energy supply landscape**: Domestic resources (coal, gas), imports (LNG, coal), infrastructure (terminals, pipelines, interconnectors), and key constraints (e.g., "Domestic gas production declining since 2020").
- **Institutional actors**: List and describe the roles of:
  - Generation, transmission, and distribution companies (e.g., EVN, PVN, Vinacomin)
  - Energy and mining companies (e.g., PetroVietnam, Vinacomin)
  - Ministerial authorities (e.g., MOIT, MONRE)
  - Local authorities and other stakeholders (e.g., provincial governments, international investors)
  - Relationships and influence (e.g., "EVN dominates coal-fired generation; IPPs increasing in gas/LNG").
- **Current challenges**: Energy trilemma (security, affordability, sustainability), international market dependencies, and domestic constraints (e.g., grid bottlenecks, fuel supply risks).

**Sources**: Cite primary sources for all statistics and claims (e.g., government reports, company filings, IEA data).

---

### 2. Per-Plant Discussion
For each plant (or plant complex), provide a **sourced narrative** covering:
- **Development history**: Original plan (e.g., "Proposed in PDP VII, 2011"), key milestones (e.g., "Construction began 2018"), and actual progress (e.g., "Delayed due to financing issues").
- **Notable issues**: Delays, financing changes, ownership transfers, legal disputes, controversies (e.g., environmental protests), or technology changes (e.g., "Switched from subcritical to USC in 2019").
- **Current status confidence**: HIGH (verified by government decision or company annual report), MEDIUM (confirmed by multiple news sources), or LOW (only found in older plans, status uncertain). Justify in the "Notes" column.

**Sources**: Link directly to primary sources (e.g., MOIT decisions, company press releases) or secondary sources (e.g., news articles) if primary sources are unavailable.

---

### 3. Structured Power Plants Table
Produce a **Markdown table** with the following columns for every thermal power plant in Vietnam:

| Name (Vietnamese) | Name (English) | Province | Fuel | Technology | Units × MW | Total MWe | Status | COD | Owner/Developer | Source 1 | Source 2 | Notes |
|-------------------|----------------|----------|------|------------|------------|-----------|--------|-----|-----------------|----------|----------|-------|
|                   |                |          |      |            |            |           |        |     |                 |          |          |       |

**Column Definitions**:
- **Name (Vietnamese/English)**: Original and English-translated name. If the plant is part of a complex, note the complex name (e.g., "Vũng Áng 1 (part of Vũng Áng complex)").
- **Province**: Administrative province where the plant is located.
- **Fuel**: Coal / Domestic gas / Imported LNG.
- **Technology**:
  - Coal: Subcritical / Supercritical / Ultra-supercritical (USC)
  - Gas: CCGT / OCGT
- **Units × MW**: Number of units and capacity per unit (e.g., "2 × 600 MW").
- **Total MWe**: Sum of all units > 30MWe. If units are retired or cancelled, note in "Notes."
- **Status**: Approved / Planned / Operational / Under construction / Suspended / Cancelled / Retired / Dismantled.
- **COD**: Actual or expected commercial operation date (e.g., "2023" or "2025 (expected)"). For cancelled/retired plants, use the last known status date.
- **Owner/Developer**: Current owner and/or developer (e.g., "EVN / OneEnergy"). Note if ownership changed over time.
- **Source 1 / Source 2**: Direct links to primary sources (e.g., government decisions, company filings). If only one source is available, leave "Source 2" blank. If no primary source is found, write "Source not found" and explain in "Notes."
- **Notes**: Qualify any uncertainties (e.g., "COD delayed from 2024 to 2026 per MOIT 2023 report"), scenario conditions (e.g., "Planned (subject to LNG terminal completion)"), or discrepancies between sources.

### 4. Annotated Bibliography
Organize all cited sources into the following categories. For each source, provide:
- **Full citation**: Author/agency, title, date, publisher.
- **URL**: Direct link if available online. If unsure, write "URL not verified."
- **Non-English sources**: Original title in Vietnamese, followed by English translation in brackets.
- **Summary annotation**: Specific information drawn from the source (e.g., "Lists COD and capacity for Vũng Áng 1; confirms USC technology").

**Categories**:
1. Government decisions (e.g., MOIT, Prime Minister, National Assembly)
2. Sectoral development plans (e.g., PDP VIII, provincial plans)
3. Company activity reports (e.g., EVN, PVN, Vinacomin annual reports)
4. Company filings & press releases (e.g., IPP project announcements)
5. Regulatory & international organization reports (e.g., IEA, World Bank, ADB)
6. News & media (e.g., Vietnam News, Reuters, local outlets)
7. Academic sources (e.g., peer-reviewed papers, think tank reports)

### 6. Reconciliation and Validation
- **Cross-check totals**: Ensure the sum of all plants in the structured table matches the totals in the statistical summary tables.
- **Resolve discrepancies**: If sources contradict each other (e.g., different CODs or capacities), document the discrepancy in the "Notes" column and explain which source was prioritized and why.
- **Flag uncertainties**: Highlight any gaps or ambiguities in the data (e.g., "No primary source found for planned capacity in Quảng Ninh province").

---

### 7. Final Notes
- **Budget awareness**: You have a **$10.00 total budget** for this task. Prioritize primary sources and critical attributes (e.g., capacity, COD, status) to maximize accuracy and provenance.
- **No fabrication**: If a source is unavailable, write "Source not found" or "URL not verified." Never invent data or links.
- **Output format**: Submit the final inventory as a **single Markdown document** with all sections (1–6) included.
