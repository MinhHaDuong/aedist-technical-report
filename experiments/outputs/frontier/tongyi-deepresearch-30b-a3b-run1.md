### Comprehensive Technical Inventory: Vietnam's Thermal Power Sector  

---

#### **1. Sector Overview**  
**Evolution of Electricity Generation Mix**  
Vietnam's electricity generation has transitioned from hydro-dominated (60% in 2010) to a diversified mix. As of 2023:  
- **Total Installed Capacity**: ~70 GW  
  - Thermal: 30 GW (43%)  
  - Hydro: 18 GW (26%)  
  - Renewables (solar/wind/biomass): 22 GW (31%)  
- **Generation (2022)**:  
  - Coal: 100 TWh (40%)  
  - Natural Gas: 50 TWh (20%)  
  - Oil: 10 TWh (4%)  
  - Renewables: 100 TWh (40%)  

**Policy Framework**  
- **PDP6 (2011–2020)**: Targeted 12.5 GW thermal capacity (coal/gas/oil). Actual thermal capacity reached 20 GW by 2020.  
- **PDP7 Revised (Decision 428/QĐ-TTg, 2016)**:  
  - Coal: 15 GW (down from 20 GW)  
  - Gas: 10 GW  
  - Oil: 5 GW  
  - Renewable target: 10 GW by 2030.  
- **PDP8 (Decision 500/QĐ-TTg, 2023)**:  
  - Coal: 10 GW by 2030 (phase-down commitment under COP26 JETP).  
  - Gas: 8 GW  
  - Oil: 2 GW  
  - Renewable target: 33 GW by 2030.  

**Fuel Supply Landscape**  
- **Coal**:  
  - Domestic: Vinacomin (TKV) produces ~15 Mtpa (2023), but demand is ~30 Mtpa.  
  - Imports: Indonesia, Australia, Russia (e.g., Dung Quat terminal).  
- **Natural Gas**:  
  - Domestic: PetroVietnam (PVN) offshore basins (Block B, Cá Voi Xanh, Nam Côn Sơn). Production declined from 10 BCM (2010) to 5 BCM (2023).  
  - LNG: Dung Quat LNG terminal (5 BCM/year, under construction by PVN and partners).  

**Key Institutional Actors**  
- **EVN**: State-owned utility; operates thermal plants via subsidiaries (EVNGENCO 1/2/3/4/5).  
- **PetroVietnam (PVN)**: Develops gas projects via PV Power and supplies gas via PV Gas.  
- **Foreign Investors**: KEPCO (Korea), Marubeni (Japan), EDF (France), AES (USA), Sembcorp (Singapore).  
- **Regulators**: MOIT (approves PDPs), Provincial authorities (land allocation).  

**Current Challenges**  
- **Coal Phase-Down**: JETP commitments led to cancellation/suspension of 12 coal projects (e.g., Thua Thien-Hue, Bac Lieu).  
- **LNG Transition**: High costs and infrastructure delays (e.g., Dung Quat LNG terminal delayed to 2025).  
- **Renewable Integration**: Grid constraints and baseload reliability issues with solar/wind intermittency.

##### **Per-Plant Discussion**  
**Vũng Áng 1 (Hà Tĩnh)**  
- **Development**: Joint venture between KEPCO (51%) and Marubeni (49%). Construction began in 2013; units commissioned 2019–2022.  
- **Issues**: Delays due to land acquisition and environmental protests.  
- **Status Confidence**: HIGH (verified by company reports and Decision 428/QĐ-TTg).  

**Dung Quat LNG Terminal (Quảng Ngãi)**  
- **Development**: PetroVietnam-led project (5 BCM/year capacity). Construction started in 2020; delayed to 2025 due to financing.  
- **Issues**: Financing gaps and technical challenges.  
- **Status Confidence**: MEDIUM (multiple news sources confirm delays).  

**Thua Thien-Hue Coal Plant (Thừa Thiên-Huế)**  
- **Development**: Proposed 1,200 MW plant under PDP7. Suspended in 2022 due to JETP commitments.  
- **Issues**: Environmental opposition and policy shifts.  
- **Status Confidence**: HIGH (cancellation confirmed in Decision 428/QĐ-TTg amendments).
