### 1. Sector Overview  

#### Evolution of Electricity Generation Mix  
Vietnam's power sector transformed from hydro-dominated (90% in 1990) to thermal-heavy by 2020. Key statistics (2022):  
- **Installed Capacity**: 77.8 GW  
  - Coal: 26.7 GW (34.3%)  
  - Gas: 7.2 GW (9.2%)  
  - Hydro: 22.1 GW (28.4%)  
  - Renewables (solar/wind): 20.6 GW (26.5%)  
- **Generation**: 261.2 TWh  
  - Coal: 127.3 TWh (48.7%)  
  - Gas: 35.1 TWh (13.4%)  
  - Hydro: 69.8 TWh (26.7%)  
  - Renewables: 28.9 TWh (11.1%)  
*Source: EVN Annual Report 2022 [Source 1]*  

#### Policy Framework  
- **PDP7 (2011)**: Targeted 75 GW by 2020 (coal: 36.5%). Revised in 2016 due to delays.  
- **PDP7 Revised (2016, Decision 428/QĐ-TTg)**: 60 GW by 2020, 129.5 GW by 2030 (coal: 55.3 GW).  
- **PDP8 (2023, Decision 500/QĐ-TTg)**:  
  - 2030: 150.5 GW (coal: 30.1 GW, LNG: 22.4 GW, gas: 14.0 GW)  
  - 2050: Net-zero emissions; coal phased to 0 GW.  
*Source: PDP8 Document [Source 2]*  

#### Fuel Supply Landscape  
- **Domestic Coal**: Vinacomin/TKV produces 40–45 MT/year (2022), but 50% of coal for power is imported (Indonesia, Australia).  
- **Domestic Gas**: PetroVietnam operates:  
  - Nam Côn Sơn Basin (supplies Phú Mỹ, Nhơn Trạch).  
  - Block B (delayed to 2026; feeds O Mon 3&4).  
  - Cá Voi Xanh (Blue Whale, 10 BCM/year; FID delayed).  
- **LNG Terminals**:  
  - Operational: Thi Vai (PV Gas, 1 MTPA).  
  - Under construction: Sơn Mỹ (AES, 6 MTPA), Hiệp Phước (T&T Group).  
*Source: PV Gas Annual Report 2022 [Source 3]*  

#### Key Institutional Actors  
- **EVN**: Dominant utility; subsidiaries EVNGENCO 1/2/3 operate 15.2 GW thermal.  
- **PetroVietnam**: PV Power (4.3 GW thermal), PV Gas (gas/LNG infrastructure).  
- **BOT Investors**: AES (Mông Dương 2), JERA (Vân Phong 1), Marubeni (Nghi Sơn 2), Sembcorp (Mỹ Xuân).  
- **Regulators**: MOIT (policy), provincial PCIs (permitting).  

#### Current Challenges  
- **Coal Phase-Down**: JETP commitment (COP26) requires $15.5B to retire coal early; PDP8 caps coal at 30.1 GW by 2030.  
- **LNG Economics**: High global prices threaten project viability (e.g., Sơn Mỹ 2.2 GW delayed).  
- **Grid Constraints**: Renewable curtailment (13% solar/wind in 2022) vs. baseload gaps in dry seasons.

#### Per-Plant Discussion  
- **Vinh Tan 1 (Bình Thuận)**: Operational since 2018. Developed by EVNGENCO 3. Faced environmental protests over coal dust. COD delayed by 2 years due to financing. *Confidence: HIGH* [4][5].  
- **Mong Duong 2 (Quảng Ninh)**: BOT operational since 2015. AES-led consortium. First BOT to use domestic coal. No major delays. *Confidence: HIGH* [9][10].  
- **Thai Binh 2 (Thái Bình)**: Under construction. PV Power project. Delayed 5 years due to financing and contractor disputes (Doosan E&C). COD revised to 2025. *Confidence: HIGH* [17][18].  
- **Long Phu 1 (Sóc Trăng)**: Suspended. PDP7 project. PV Power halted due to coal supply uncertainty and PDP8 de-prioritization. *Confidence: MEDIUM* [19][20].  
- **Nam Dinh 1 (Nam Định)**: Cancelled. BOT terminated in 2020 after 7-year delay; MOIT cited "lack of progress". *Confidence: HIGH* [21][22].  
- **Nhon Trach 3&4 (Đồng Nai)**: Under construction. PV Power’s first new gas plant since 2010. Uses domestic gas from Nam Côn Sơn. On schedule. *Confidence: HIGH* [27][28].  
- **Bac Lieu LNG (Bạc Liêu)**: Approved. Delta Offshore’s 3.2 GW project. FID delayed to 2024 due to PPA negotiations. *Confidence: MEDIUM* [31][32].  
- **Son My 2 (Bình Thuận)**: Planned. AES/PVN BOT. Faces headwinds from LNG price volatility. PDP8 timeline at risk. *Confidence: MEDIUM* [33][34].
