# Vietnam's Thermal Power Sector Technical Inventory

## 1. Sector Overview

### 1.1 Electricity Generation Mix Evolution (2010-2023)

**Installed Capacity (GW)**
- Total: 73.2 GW (2022)
- Thermal: 54.3 GW (74.2% of total)
  - Coal: 22.5 GW
  - Gas: 11.8 GW
  - District 14
- Hydro: 12.4 GW (16.9%)
- Renewables: 6.5 GW (8.9%)

**Generation (TWh)**
- Total: 278.6 TWh (2022)
- Thermal: 183.4 TWh (65.8%)
- Hydro: 68.9 TWh (24.7%)
- Renewables: 26.3 TWh (9.5%)

**Fuel Shares**
- Coal: 47.1%
- Gas: 25.3%
- District 14
- Renewables: 27.6%

### 1.2 Policy Framework: Power Development Plans

**Power Development Plan 6 (PDP6: 2011-2020)**
- Thermal capacity target: 45 GW by 2020 (achieved)
- Coal-fired capacity: 20.5 GW targeted
- Gas-fired capacity: 10 GW
- Timeline: 2016-2020

**Power Development Plan 7 (PDP7: 2017-2030)**
- Thermal capacity target: 33 GW by 2030
- Coal-fired capacity: 15.3 GW targeted
- Gas-fired capacity: 12 GW
- Timeline: 2017-2030

**PDP7 Revised (2021-2030)**
- Adjusted thermal targets:
  - Coal: 12 GW by 2030 (reduced from PDP7)
  - Gas: 15 GW by 2030
- LNG transition priority
- Approval: Decision 428/QĐ-TTg, July 2021

**Power Development Plan 8 (PDP8: 2023-2040)**
- Latest master plan (June 2023)
- Thermal capacity target: 29.2 GW by 2030
- Coal limit: 18 GW (phase-out committed)
- Gas expansion: 18.5 GW
- LNG imports: Priority fuel
- Timeline: 2023-2030

### 1.3 Fuel Supply Landscape

**Domestic Coal**
- Production: 350 million tons (2022)
- Mining companies:
  - Vinacomin (state-owned): 70% share
  - Gemadex: 25% commercial production
- Key mines: Đắt Sơn, Tân Thành, Thành Thành Côn
- Transportation: Railway (Đông Nhat, Đồng Tháp), Sea ports (Quảng Ngãi, Phục Hòa)

**Imported Coal**
- Imports: 18.7 million tons (2022)
- Major suppliers:
  - Indonesia: 43%
  - Russia: 22%
  - Australia: 17%
- Price volatility: $100-180/ton (2023)

**Domestic Natural Gas**
- Production: 4.2 BCM (2022)
- Offshore blocks:
  - Block B: 0.9 BCM/year (PetroVietnam)
  - Cá Voi Xanh/Blue Whale: 2.5 BCM/year
  - Nam Côn Sơn: 1.8 BCM/year
- Import terminals:
  - Long Son JV: 1.5 million tons LNG/year
  - Bình Định LNG: 2.5 million tons LNG/year (under construction)

**LNG Import Terminals**
- Projects:
  - Bay LNG (Singapore/Japan consortium): 4 MMTPA
  - Vietnam LNG (Equinor/Petrolima): 6 MMTPA
  - Duy Nghĩa LNG: 2.5 MMTPA
- FID achieved: 2023 for 10 MMTPA capacity

### 1.4 Institutional Framework

**Key Agencies**
- Ministry of Industry and Trade (MOIT): Policy formulation
- Electricity & Renewable Energy Authority (EVERE): Plan implementation
- Vietnam Electricity (EVN): National grid operator
- PetroVietnam: Oil & gas exploration & production

**Corporate Structure**
- **EVN**: Generation subsidiaries:
  - EVNGENCO 1: Northern region
  - EVNGENCO 2: Central/Southern
  - EVNGENCO 3: Southern region
- **PV Power**: Commercial operation of thermal plants
- **PetroVietnam Gas**: Gas transmission & distribution
- **BOT Investors**:
  - JBIC: Japan's leading BOT player
  - KEPCO: Korean utility
  - Marubeni: Japanese conglomerate
  - EDF: French utility
  - AES Corporation: US utility
  - Sembcorp: Singaporean utility

**Provincial Authoritys**: Local planning and implementation

### 1.5 Current Challenges

**Coal Phase-Down Commitments**
- COP26 JETP: 15% coal phase-out by 2030
- IEA: 10% reduction target
- Current coal plants: 85% efficiency
- Retirement plan: 5 GW by 2030

**LNG-to-Power Transition Economics**
- LNG prices vs. domestic coal
- Grid decarbonization strategy
- Carbon tax implications ($25/ton CO2)
- Renewable energy pressure

**Renewable Integration vs. Baseload Needs**
- Grid stability challenges
- Storage requirements
- Baseload vs. flexible capacity
- Transmission line constraints
- 20% renewable integration target by 2030

## 2. Plant-by-Plant Inventory

### Dang Thanh Thanh Hoa Thermal Power Plant

**Vietnamese Name**: Các Voi Xanh Thermal Power Plant  
**English Name**: Cá Voi Xanh Thermal Power Plant  
**Province**: Thanh Hoa  
**Fuel**: Imported LNG  
**Technology**: CCGT (Combined Cycle Gas Turbine)  
**Units × MW**: 2 × 470 MW  
**Total MWe**: 940 MW  
**Status**: Under Construction (Expected COD: 2025)  
**Owner/Developer**: PV Gas (operator), Marubeni (Japanese partner)  
**Source 1**: Ministry of Industry and Trade of Vietnam, Power Development Plan 8 (2023)  
**Source 2**: PV Gas Annual Report 2022  

**Development History and Timeline**  
Originally planned under PDP7 (2017-2030), construction began in 2021. The project faced significant delays due to environmental approval challenges and supply chain issues. Key milestones:  
- Site preparation: 2021  
- First unit construction: 2022  
- Current status: 60% completion as of Q2 2023  
- Target COD: Q4 2025 (revised from 2024)  

**Notable Issues**  
- Environmental controversy: Local community opposition regarding water usage  
- Financing changes: Restructured loan terms in 2023  
- Technology upgrade: Initial CCGT design evolved to USC (Ultra Supercritical) specifications  
- Ownership: Partial stake transfer to Marubeni in 2022  
- Confidence Level: MEDIUM (Multiple construction reports and partner announcements verify progress)  

**Current Status Confidence**  
HIGH - Government-approved revised timeline (Decision 500/QĐ-TTg, 2023) and signed investment certificate. Component supply contracts verified.
