# Vietnam thermal generation assets (>30 MWe): primary-sourced reference inventory  
**Scope:** coal, domestic-gas, imported-LNG thermal generation assets in Vietnam, including operating, retired, under-construction, delayed, cancelled/superseded, and pre-FID/planning-cycle projects where they appeared in formal planning.  
**Status date:** best effort to **23 May 2026**.  
**Important caveat:** This is a consolidated reference inventory built from the evidence pack plus current official/near-official web checks. It is **not a legally authoritative register**. Several industrial captive/CHP assets and early-stage LNG/coal site options have weak public primary documentation; these are retained with **LOW** confidence rather than omitted.

---

## 1. Sector overview

### 1.1 Electricity mix and thermal role

Vietnam’s installed power system reached roughly **82.4 GW in 2024**, with **coal-fired capacity 26.8 GW** and **gas + oil-fired capacity 8.65 GW** in EVN’s 2024 reporting; EVN’s 2025 annual-report extract gives the fuel-type breakdown for 2023–2024 as: hydropower 23.7 GW, coal 26.8 GW, gas + oil 8.65 GW, renewables 21.45 GW, others 0.64 GW, and imports 1.22 GW in 2024. ([en.evn.com.vn](https://en.evn.com.vn/userfile/files/2026/4/AnnualRepot2025_V23-20260408155435105.pdf?utm_source=openai))  

Coal remains Vietnam’s largest dispatchable thermal category. Gas generation historically centered on domestic offshore gas in the Southeast and Southwest basins — Phú Mỹ, Bà Rịa, Nhơn Trạch, Cà Mau, Ô Môn — while the next buildout phase under PDP8 is dominated by **LNG-to-power** and by delayed **domestic-gas CCGTs** tied to Lô B and Cá Voi Xanh gas developments. PDP8 Decision 500/QĐ-TTg listed **22.4 GW of LNG thermal projects**, **domestic-gas projects including Ô Môn II–IV, Dung Quất I–III, Miền Trung I–II and Quảng Trị**, and a residual pipeline of coal projects under construction or delayed. [pdp8_annex2_table1; pdp8_annex2_table2; pdp8_annex2_table3; pdp8_annex2_table5]

### 1.2 Policy framework

Vietnam’s thermal fleet has been shaped by four planning cycles:

1. **PDP7, Decision 1208/QĐ-TTg, 21 July 2011** — included large coal additions through 2030, domestic-gas CCGTs, nuclear power, and many later-superseded coal projects. [pdp7_annex1; pdp7_annex2]  
2. **Revised PDP7, Decision 428/QĐ-TTg, 18 March 2016** — rescheduled many coal and gas projects, added/shifted BOT coal projects and early LNG/gas concepts. [pdp7a_annex1_table1; pdp7a_annex1_table2; pdp7a_annex1_table3]  
3. **PDP8, Decision 500/QĐ-TTg, 15 May 2023** — pivoted away from a large new coal pipeline, retained coal only where under construction or explicitly delayed/troubled, and prioritized LNG, domestic gas and flexible/renewable integration. [pdp8_annex2_table1–5]  
4. **PDP8 Implementation Plan, Decision 262/QĐ-TTg, 1 April 2024**, and later PDP8 revisions — converted PDP8 into project lists and provincial allocation schedules. Secondary legal summaries state that Decision 262 identifies the implementation route for PDP8 and includes status updates for LNG, domestic gas and coal projects; EVN’s 2024 system capacity data are consistent with coal capacity remaining flat at 26.8 GW. ([mayerbrown.com](https://www.mayerbrown.com/en/insights/publications/2024/04/implementation-plan-to-vietnams-pdp8-released?utm_source=openai))  

A 2025 revised PDP8 legal summary reports that the revised plan retains coal around **31.1 GW by 2030** but targets **zero coal by 2050**, and keeps LNG thermal around **22.5 GW** by 2030. Because the accessible source is a legal-summary secondary source rather than the Vietnamese official gazette text, this inventory treats the **PDP8 evidence-pack tables and Decision 262** as the main planning baseline, and treats 2025 revision details as contextual. ([kpmg.com](https://kpmg.com/vn/en/home/insights/2025/07/revised-pdp-8.html?utm_source=openai))

| Actor | Role in thermal power |
|---|---|
| **Prime Minister / Government Office** | Approves PDPs, project additions, conversions, BOT policy decisions. |
| **Ministry of Industry and Trade — MOIT** | Power planning, implementation monitoring, electricity-market and fuel-supply oversight. |
| **EVN / EVNGENCO 1–3** | Major owner/operator of coal, gas and oil/gas turbines; buyer under PPAs; grid/system operator functions via subsidiaries. |
| **PVN / PV Power / PV Gas** | Domestic gas supply chain and gas-fired generation; owner/developer of Nhơn Trạch, Cà Mau, Ô Môn-related projects, Thái Bình II, Sông Hậu I, Long Phú I. |
| **Vinacomin / TKV** | Domestic coal mining and several mine-mouth coal plants: Na Dương, Cao Ngạn, Cẩm Phả, Sơn Động, Mạo Khê, plus planned Na Dương II etc. |

### 1.4 Current challenges

* **Fuel security:** coal supply constraints and price volatility have affected dispatch; EVN’s 2023 overview notes coal and gas supply issues and lower-than-planned gas consumption. ([en.evn.com.vn](https://en.evn.com.vn/d6/news/Overview-of-national-power-sources-in-2023-66-142-4147.aspx?utm_source=openai))  
* **Delayed gas developments:** Ô Môn II–IV are tied to Lô B; Dung Quất/Miền Trung projects are tied to Cá Voi Xanh. Reports 32 and 58 repeatedly mark delays as “đồng bộ khí” — synchronized with gas-field progress. [report_32_annex1; report_58_annex]  
* **Coal project finance and social/environmental opposition:** PDP8 separates coal plants into “under construction” and “delayed/difficult” groups; Nam Định I, Quảng Trị, Vĩnh Tân III and Sông Hậu II are explicitly listed as delayed/troubled. [pdp8_annex2_table2; pdp8_annex2_table3]  
* **LNG bankability:** PDP8 lists many LNG projects, but most remain pre-COD and require LNG terminal, PPA, fuel pass-through and credit arrangements. [pdp8_annex2_table1]  
* **System balancing:** renewables have grown rapidly, but dispatchable coal/gas remain central for reliability pending storage, transmission and market reforms.

## 2. Confidence coding

| Confidence | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **HIGH** | Plant appears in at least one operator annual report, PDP annex, implementation report or official/near-official source with matching capacity/status; no serious conflict. |
| **MEDIUM** | Plant appears in formal planning or implementation reports but details such as province, exact unit rating, technology or status are inferred or conflicted. |
| **LOW** | Plant is known from planning cycles, industrial context or secondary references but lacks exact public primary confirmation, has ambiguous naming, or may be a superseded/cancelled option. |

## 4. Concise per-plant narratives

### 4.1 Core operating coal fleet

* **Uông Bí complex** — Vietnam’s legacy northern coal plant at Uông Bí comprises an old 105 MW block and later 300/330 MW extensions. EVN annual reports list 105 MW plus 330 MW in 2011 and 300+330 MW extensions by 2017–2018; Revised PDP7 explicitly planned retirement of the 105 MW old unit around 2021. [evn_ar_2010_2011_capacities; evn_ar_2017_capacities; evn_ar_2018_capacities; pdp7a_annex1_table2]  
* **Phả Lại I–II** — A major EVNGENCO2 coal complex in Hải Dương, with 440 MW Phase I and 600 MW Phase II. EVN reports consistently list 1,040 MW total. [evn_ar_2010_2011_capacities; evn_ar_2018_capacities]  
* **Ninh Bình** — 100 MW legacy coal plant. PDP7 contemplated retirement, but EVN’s 2018 roster still listed the plant under GENCO3. [pdp7_annex2; evn_ar_2018_capacities]  
* **Hải Phòng I–II** — 1,200 MW total, 4×300 MW, commissioned across the PDP7 period; EVN’s 2018 roster lists both phases at 600 MW each. [pdp7_annex1; evn_ar_2018_capacities]  
* **Quảng Ninh I–II** — 1,200 MW total in Quảng Ninh, 4×300 MW, operated by GENCO1. EVN 2017/2018 rosters list 600 MW each. [evn_ar_2017_capacities; evn_ar_2018_capacities]  
* **Na Dương I, Cao Ngạn, Sơn Động, Cẩm Phả, Mạo Khê** — Vinacomin/TKV’s domestic-coal plants, mostly mine-mouth or coal-area assets. EVN 2011 lists Na Dương 111 MW, Cao Ngạn 115 MW, Cẩm Phả 600 MW, Sơn Động 220 MW; PDP7 lists Mạo Khê 440 MW entering service in 2013. [evn_ar_2010_2011_capacities; pdp7_annex1]  
* **An Khánh I and Thăng Long** — IPP domestic-coal projects. PDP7 lists An Khánh I 2×50 MW in 2012–2013; Revised PDP7 and Report 58 list Thăng Long 2×300 MW, with Report 58 saying it had been/was being energized. [pdp7_annex1; pdp7a_annex1_table1; report_58_annex]  
* **Mông Dương I–II** — Mông Dương I is EVNGENCO3’s 1,080 MW plant; Mông Dương II is the 1,200 MW AES BOT plant. EVN reports Mông Dương I at 1,080 MW; PDP7 lists Mông Dương II 1,200 MW. [evn_ar_2018_capacities; pdp7_annex1]  
* **Vũng Áng I** — PVN’s 1,200 MW coal plant in Hà Tĩnh, with two 600 MW units listed in PDP7 in 2012–2013. [pdp7_annex1]  
* **Nghi Sơn I–II** — Nghi Sơn I is EVN’s 600 MW plant; Nghi Sơn II is a 1,200 MW BOT project. PDP8 still listed Nghi Sơn II in the under-construction coal category, while earlier progress reports expected 2022 completion. [evn_ar_2018_capacities; pdp8_annex2_table2; study_e542_pl9_2]  
* **Vĩnh Tân complex** — Includes Vĩnh Tân I BOT 1,200 MW, Vĩnh Tân II 1,244 MW, Vĩnh Tân IV 1,200 MW and Vĩnh Tân IV Extension 600 MW. Report 58 records Vĩnh Tân I as ahead of schedule and Vĩnh Tân IV / IV Extension as on or ahead of schedule; EVN 2018 lists Vĩnh Tân II and IV. [evn_ar_2018_capacities; report_58_annex]  
* **Duyên Hải complex** — EVN’s Duyên Hải I 1,245 MW, Duyên Hải III 1,245 MW and Duyên Hải III Extension 660 MW are operating; Duyên Hải II BOT was delayed about one year but part of the formal BOT pipeline. [evn_ar_2018_capacities; report_58_annex; pdp7a_annex1_table2]  
* **Thái Bình I–II** — Thái Bình I is EVN’s 600 MW operating plant. Thái Bình II, PVN’s 1,200 MW project, suffered major delays in Report 58 but is now treated as operating/recently completed; primary COD confirmation should be checked separately if exact unit COD is required. [evn_ar_2018_capacities; report_58_annex]  
* **Sông Hậu I** — PVN’s 1,200 MW coal project in Hậu Giang was listed as delayed in Reports 32/58 and under construction in the 2020 E542 study; it is now treated as operating, but exact COD is not in the supplied primary pack. [report_58_annex; study_e542_pl9_2]  
* **Vân Phong I** — PDP8 lists the 1,432 MW Vân Phong I coal project under construction; E542 described it as 2×716 MW with PPA signed and expected 2023–2024. [pdp8_annex2_table2; study_e542_pl9_2]  

### 4.2 Coal projects under construction or delayed in current policy

* **Na Dương II, An Khánh–Bắc Giang, Vũng Áng II, Quảng Trạch I, Vân Phong I, Long Phú I** are the six coal projects PDP8 explicitly grouped as “coal thermal plants under construction.” [pdp8_annex2_table2]  
* **Nam Định I, Quảng Trị, Vĩnh Tân III, Sông Hậu II** are PDP8’s main delayed/difficult coal projects, together with “Cẩm Thanh/Công Thanh” ambiguity. Their continued status is uncertain and bankability is weak. [pdp8_annex2_table3]  
* **Long Phú I** is especially problematic: Report 58 cited delays and sanctions-related risk; PDP8 nevertheless retained it as under construction. [report_58_annex; pdp8_annex2_table2]  
* **Quảng Trạch I** moved from PVN to EVN and was delayed relative to Revised PDP7. PDP8 updates its capacity to 1,403 MW. [report_32_annex1; pdp8_annex2_table2]  

### 4.3 Superseded and cancelled coal options

Many PDP7 and Revised PDP7 coal projects are retained here because the user requested **past, present and future assets including cancelled/pre-FID projects if they appeared in formal planning cycles**. These include Hải Phòng III, Quỳnh Lập I–II, Vũng Áng III, Long Phú II–III, Kiên Lương, Tân Phước, Long An coal, Bạc Liêu coal, Bình Định coal, An Giang coal, Yên Hưng/Uông Bí III and generic “north/south coal” blocks. Most are now **LOW confidence** as active assets and should be understood as **planning-cycle artefacts**, not live projects. [pdp7_annex2; pdp7a_annex1_table2; pdp7a_annex1_table3; study_e542_pl9_2]

### 4.4 Domestic-gas operating fleet

* **Phú Mỹ complex** — Vietnam’s principal legacy gas-generation cluster. EVN reports Phú Mỹ 1, 2.1, 4 and BOT Phú Mỹ 2.2/3 separately; capacity values vary by annual report due to derating/uprating conventions. [evn_ar_2010_2011_capacities; evn_ar_2018_capacities]  
* **Bà Rịa** — 389 MW legacy gas-turbine plant, listed consistently in EVN annual reports. [evn_ar_2010_2011_capacities; evn_ar_2018_capacities]  
* **Nhơn Trạch I–II** — PV Power’s existing gas CCGTs: Nhơn Trạch I 465 MW and Nhơn Trạch II 750 MW. [evn_ar_2010_2011_capacities; pdp7_annex1]  
* **Cà Mau I–II** — PV Power’s twin 750 MW gas plants, 1,500 MW total, supplied historically by PM3/Cà Mau gas. [evn_ar_2010_2011_capacities]  
* **Ô Môn I** — 660 MW old oil/gas plant planned for conversion to Lô B gas under PDP8. [evn_ar_2018_capacities; pdp8_annex2_table5]  
* **Thủ Đức and Cần Thơ gas turbines** — legacy OCGT/oil-gas assets still listed in EVN rosters; some old units are likely low-utilization reserve plants. [evn_ar_2010_2011_capacities; evn_ar_2018_capacities]  

### 4.5 Domestic-gas future fleet

PDP8 lists domestic-gas thermal projects totaling roughly 10–15 GW depending on exact unit configuration: Ô Môn II–IV, Dung Quất I–III, Miền Trung I–II and Quảng Trị. The principal risk is upstream gas schedule: Lô B for Ô Môn, Cá Voi Xanh for Dung Quất/Miền Trung, and Báo Vàng for Quảng Trị. [pdp8_annex2_table5; report_58_annex]

### 4.6 LNG fleet and future LNG projects

PDP8’s LNG table includes Quảng Ninh, Thái Bình, Nghi Sơn, Quảng Trạch II, Quỳnh Lập/Nghi Sơn, Hải Lăng Phase 1, Cà Ná, Sơn Mỹ I–II, Long Sơn, Nhơn Trạch 3–4, Hiệp Phước I, Long An I–II and Bạc Liêu. Of these, **Nhơn Trạch 3–4** is the most advanced physical power-plant construction; most others remain pre-COD and are exposed to LNG terminal/PPA/bankability risks. [pdp8_annex2_table1; report_58_annex]

### 4.7 Industrial captive and CHP assets

Formosa Hà Tĩnh, Formosa Đồng Nai, Vedan, Hải Hà CHP, Đức Giang CHP, Hòa Phát II waste-gas power and Lee & Man are included because the scope covers off-grid/captive/cogeneration if >30 MWe. These have weaker public primary documentation than grid-connected EVN/PVN/BOT assets. PDP8 Table 4 provides the best policy source for industrial cogeneration/by-product projects. [pdp8_annex2_table4; pdp7a_annex1_table1]

## 7. Key reconciliation notes

1. **Operating coal total:** The structured table’s operating coal capacity is reconciled against EVN’s 2024 national total of **26.757 GW coal-fired capacity**; small differences arise from captive plants, newly commissioned plants, deratings and whether oil/coal industrial CHP is included. ([en.evn.com.vn](https://en.evn.com.vn/userfile/files/2026/4/AnnualRepot2025_V23-20260408155435105.pdf?utm_source=openai))  
2. **Gas + oil:** EVN groups gas and oil together at **8.653 GW** in 2024; this inventory separates domestic gas from LNG where possible, but older plants such as Thủ Đức, Cần Thơ, Ô Môn I and Hiệp Phước blur oil/gas classification. ([en.evn.com.vn](https://en.evn.com.vn/userfile/files/2026/4/AnnualRepot2025_V23-20260408155435105.pdf?utm_source=openai))  
3. **Capacity conflicts:** Where EVN annual reports differ — e.g., Phú Mỹ 1 at 1,090/1,108/1,118 MW, Phú Mỹ 2.1 at 896/900/945 MW, Duyên Hải III at 1,244/1,245 MW — the table preserves ranges or uses the most recent EVN figure. [evn_ar_2010_2011_capacities; evn_ar_2017_capacities; evn_ar_2018_capacities]  
4. **Superseded coal projects:** Old PDP7/PDP7A coal projects are included because they appeared in formal planning cycles, but many are no longer active under PDP8. These rows are marked LOW or MEDIUM.  
5. **Industrial CHP:** Captive thermal plants are less transparent than grid-connected plants; PDP8 Table 4 is the strongest planning source, but plant-level COD and operating status often require company filings or provincial permits not present in the evidence pack.
