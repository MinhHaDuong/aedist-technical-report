# Vietnam thermal generation assets >30 MWe — primary-sourced reference inventory

**Version / as-of:** 24 May 2026.  
**Scope applied:** coal, domestic gas, imported LNG, oil/gas legacy turbines, and industrial thermal/cogeneration where capacity >30 MWe and where assets appear in EVN annual-report tables, PDP7/PDP7A/PDP8 planning annexes, or implementation-progress annexes supplied in the evidence pack. Biomass/waste-only projects are not tabulated unless they appear as “nhiệt điện” industrial thermal sources.  
**Important limitation:** A truly “complete” plant-by-plant inventory for Vietnam including every captive industrial thermal unit >30 MWe requires checking provincial industrial-zone permits, MOIT/ERAV licence lists, EVN metering lists, and company disclosures. The table below is a **primary-source anchored master inventory** from the supplied primary evidence plus current PDP8/revised-PDP8 web checks; where a known/candidate asset lacks unit-level primary confirmation, I mark **LOW** confidence rather than omit it.

## 1. Sector overview

Vietnam’s power system has grown from a hydro- and domestic-gas-heavy system into one where coal is the largest dispatchable source, while policy now caps new coal and pivots planned thermal growth toward domestic gas, imported LNG, and later hydrogen/ammonia conversion. EVN’s 2025 annual-report data show total system installed capacity of **82,387 MW in 2024**, including **26,757 MW coal-fired** and **8,653 MW gas + oil fired**; EVN also reports the power system was **80,946 MW in 2023** and **82,387 MW in 2024**. ([en.evn.com.vn](https://en.evn.com.vn/userfile/files/2026/4/AnnualRepot2025_V23-20260408155435105.pdf?utm_source=openai))

The current policy backbone is **PDP8**, approved by Decision 500/QĐ-TTg on **15 May 2023**, followed by the **PDP8 Implementation Plan** under Decision 262/QĐ-TTg on **1 April 2024**. The implementation plan translates PDP8 into project lists and capacity targets; contemporaneous summaries report 2030 thermal targets of roughly **30,127 MW coal**, **14,930 MW domestic gas**, and **22,400 MW LNG** under the implementation plan. ([vietnamenergy.vn](https://vietnamenergy.vn/promulgating-the-plan-for-implementing-power-development-planning-viii-32400.html?utm_source=openai)) In 2025, Vietnam issued a revised PDP8 framework under Decision 768/QĐ-TTg dated **15 April 2025** and implementation guidance under Decision 1509/QĐ-BCT dated **30 May 2025**; summaries report revised 2030 targets of **31,055 MW coal** and **22,524 MW LNG**, while preserving transition status for projects already included under Decision 500, Decision 262, or Decision 1682. ([kpmg.com](https://kpmg.com/vn/en/home/insights/2025/07/revised-pdp-8.html?utm_source=openai))

Key institutional actors are:

- **Prime Minister / Government Office:** approves PDPs and implementation plans.
- **MOIT / ERAV / Institute of Energy:** prepares and administers planning, market and licensing frameworks.
- **EVN and EVNGENCO 1/2/3:** own/operate many legacy coal, gas and oil plants and are developers of Quảng Trạch I, Ô Môn III/IV, Dung Quất gas projects, etc.
- **PVN / PV Power:** gas-fired fleet, Cà Mau, Nhơn Trạch, Sông Hậu, Long Phú, Ô Môn gas-chain projects.
- **Vinacomin/TKV:** domestic-coal mine-mouth plants such as Na Dương, Cao Ngạn, Cẩm Phả, Sơn Động, Mạo Khê.
- **BOT / foreign IPPs:** Phú Mỹ 2.2, Phú Mỹ 3, Mông Dương 2, Vĩnh Tân 1, Hải Dương, Nghi Sơn 2, Vân Phong 1, Duyên Hải 2 and planned LNG BOTs.
- **Industrial IPPs/captive generators:** Formosa Hà Tĩnh, Formosa Đồng Nai, Vedan, Hải Hà, Lee & Man and similar CHP/waste-heat/by-product sources.

Current challenges are: fuel-security and coal logistics, gas-field delays — especially **Lô B** and **Cá Voi Xanh** chains — LNG bankability and offtake risk, BOT financing after global coal-exit policies, grid bottlenecks, environmental opposition, and uncertain treatment of older oil/gas turbines. EVN’s 2024 coal-plant operation notices stress coal supply and reliable operation for dry-season supply; EVN also reports coal as the largest capacity category by end-2023 at 32.6% of installed capacity. ([en.evn.com.vn](https://en.evn.com.vn/d6/news/Thermal-power-operation-in-2024-and-3-no-tolerances-goal-66-163-3987.aspx?utm_source=openai))

## 2. Source hierarchy and confidence rules

| Confidence | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **HIGH** | Operating or formally planned asset confirmed by at least one primary source such as EVN annual report, PDP8/PDP7A annex, or implementation-progress annex; key capacity/status supported. |
| **MEDIUM** | Asset appears in formal planning annexes or progress reports but unit configuration/status has changed or conflicts across sources. |
| **LOW** | Candidate, cancelled, pre-FID, industrial/captive, or legacy plant with incomplete unit, status, province or fuel detail; included to preserve recall. |

## 3. Concise per-plant narratives

### Legacy coal fleet

- **Uông Bí / Uông Bí mở rộng** — Long-running Quảng Ninh anthracite coal plant. EVN2011 lists Uông Bí 105 MW and Uông Bí mở rộng II 330 MW; EVN2017 lists Uông Bí 105 MW, Extension 1 300 MW and Extension 2 330 MW under GENCO 1. PDP7A explicitly scheduled **Uông Bí 105 MW** for retirement/cessation in 2021. **Confidence: HIGH for historic capacity; MEDIUM for present operating status of old 105 MW unit.** Sources: EVN2011, EVN2017, PDP7A-2.

- **Phả Lại I–II** — Hải Dương coal complex, 440 MW older subcritical Phả Lại I and 600 MW Phả Lại II. EVN2011 and EVN2017/2018 consistently list 1,040 MW under EVN JSC/GENCO 2. **Confidence: HIGH.** Sources: EVN2011, EVN2017, EVN2018.

- **Ninh Bình** — 100 MW legacy coal plant. EVN2011/2017/2018 list 100 MW; PDP7 planned retirement around 2021. **Confidence: HIGH historic; MEDIUM current because retirement status requires licence/dispatch verification.** Sources: EVN2011, EVN2017, PDP7.

- **Na Dương I / II** — Lạng Sơn lignite/domestic-coal mine-mouth plant. EVN2011 lists Na Dương 111 MW under Vinacomin. PDP8 lists Na Dương II 110 MW as coal under construction. **Confidence: HIGH for Na Dương I and planned/under-construction Na Dương II.** Sources: EVN2011, PDP8-Coal-UC.

- **Cao Ngạn** — 115 MW Vinacomin domestic-coal plant in Thái Nguyên, listed by EVN2011. **Confidence: HIGH for existence/capacity; MEDIUM for current dispatch status.** Source: EVN2011.

- **Cẩm Phả I–II / Cẩm Phả III** — Quảng Ninh coal fleet. EVN2011 lists Cẩm Phả 600 MW under Vinacomin; PDP7 lists Cẩm Phả II 300 MW in 2011; PDP7A and reports later include Cẩm Phả III 2×220 MW but progress reports say no site/uncertain. **Confidence: HIGH operating 600 MW; LOW planned Cẩm Phả III.** Sources: EVN2011, PDP7, PDP7A-1, R32/R58.

- **Sơn Động** — 220 MW Vinacomin domestic-coal plant in Bắc Giang, listed by EVN2011. **Confidence: HIGH.** Source: EVN2011.

- **Mạo Khê** — 440 MW TKV domestic-coal plant, appears in PDP7 schedule. **Confidence: HIGH for operating asset from formal plan; MEDIUM for exact COD.** Source: PDP7.

- **An Khánh I / An Khánh Bắc Giang** — PDP7 lists An Khánh I 2×50 MW; PDP8 lists An Khánh–Bắc Giang 650 MW coal under construction. **Confidence: MEDIUM for An Khánh I; MEDIUM/LOW for 650 MW project because current status has been delayed.** Sources: PDP7, PDP8-Coal-UC, R58.

- **Hải Phòng I–II / Hải Phòng III** — EVN2011 lists Hải Phòng I 300 MW; EVN2017/2018 list Hải Phòng 1 and 2 at 600 MW each. Hải Phòng III appears in PDP7A/PDP7/R58 as future 2×600 MW but later progress is uncertain and local coal acceptance problematic. **Confidence: HIGH operating 1,200 MW; LOW future Hải Phòng III.** Sources: EVN2011, EVN2017, EVN2018, PDP7A-2/3, R58, E542-Coal.

- **Quảng Ninh I–II / III** — EVN2011 lists Quảng Ninh I 300 MW; EVN2017/2018 list Quảng Ninh 1 and 2 at 600 MW each. Quảng Ninh III appears in PDP7A/R32/R58 as 2×600 MW future/unclear. **Confidence: HIGH operating 1,200 MW; LOW Quảng Ninh III.** Sources: EVN2011, EVN2017, EVN2018, PDP7A-3, R32/R58.

- **Mông Dương I–II** — Mông Dương I is EVN/GENCO 3, 1,080 MW in EVN2017/2018; Mông Dương II is BOT, 1,200 MW in PDP7. **Confidence: HIGH.** Sources: EVN2017, EVN2018, PDP7.

- **Thái Bình I–II** — Thái Bình I 2×300 MW EVN is listed in EVN2018; Thái Bình II 2×600 MW PVN appears in PDP7A/R32/R58 with major delays, now operating but not confirmed in supplied EVN tables. **Confidence: HIGH for Thái Bình I; MEDIUM/HIGH for Thái Bình II capacity/status.** Sources: EVN2018, PDP7A-1, R32/R58.

- **Nghi Sơn I–II / LNG Nghi Sơn** — Nghi Sơn I 600 MW appears in EVN2017/2018. Nghi Sơn II 2×600 MW BOT appears in PDP7A/R58; PDP8 includes LNG Nghi Sơn 1,500 MW separately. **Confidence: HIGH for Nghi Sơn I and II planned/operating lineage; MEDIUM for LNG project.** Sources: EVN2017, EVN2018, PDP7A-2, R58, PDP8-LNG.

- **Vũng Áng I–II/III** — Vũng Áng I 2×600 MW PVN appears in PDP7. Vũng Áng II 1,330 MW is in PDP8 coal-under-construction. Vũng Áng III appears as delayed/after-2030 or difficult in reports. **Confidence: HIGH Vũng Áng I/II; LOW Vũng Áng III.** Sources: PDP7, PDP8-Coal-UC, R58.

- **Quảng Trạch I / II** — Quảng Trạch I 1,403 MW in PDP8 coal-under-construction; earlier sources list 2×600 MW and transfer from PVN to EVN. Quảng Trạch II converted to LNG 1,500 MW in PDP8. **Confidence: HIGH Quảng Trạch I; HIGH planned LNG conversion for II.** Sources: PDP8-Coal-UC, PDP8-LNG, R32/R58.

- **Duyên Hải I, II, III, III mở rộng** — Duyên Hải I and III are EVN coal plants in Trà Vinh, with Duyên Hải 1 and 3 listed around 1,245 MW each in EVN2017/2018; Duyên Hải III Extension 660 MW appears in R32/R58 as on schedule; Duyên Hải II BOT 2×600 MW appears in PDP7A/R58/E542. **Confidence: HIGH.** Sources: EVN2017, EVN2018, PDP7A-1/2, R58, E542-Coal.

- **Vĩnh Tân I, II, III, IV, IV mở rộng** — Vĩnh Tân II 1,244 MW GENCO 3 in EVN2017/2018; Vĩnh Tân IV 1,200 MW and extension 600 MW in EVN2018/R58; Vĩnh Tân I 2×600 MW BOT in PDP7A/R58; Vĩnh Tân III 1,980 MW delayed in PDP8. **Confidence: HIGH for I/II/IV/IV ext; MEDIUM/LOW for III.** Sources: EVN2017, EVN2018, PDP7A-1/2, PDP8-Coal-Delayed, R58.

- **Vân Phong I / II** — Vân Phong I listed in PDP8 coal under construction at 1,432 MW; E542 gives 2×716 MW and signed PPA. Vân Phong II appears only in older PDP7/PDP7A planning and was superseded. **Confidence: HIGH Vân Phong I; LOW Vân Phong II.** Sources: PDP8-Coal-UC, E542-Coal, PDP7/PDP7A.

- **Long Phú I / II / III** — Long Phú I 1,200 MW is listed by PDP8 as coal under construction but R32/R58 record prolonged delay due to sanctions/EPC difficulties. Long Phú II and III appear in PDP7A/R58/E542 as future/delayed or owner-transfer projects. **Confidence: MEDIUM for Long Phú I under construction; LOW for II/III.** Sources: PDP8-Coal-UC, R32/R58, E542-Coal.

- **Sông Hậu I / II / III** — Sông Hậu I 2×600 MW PVN appears in PDP7A/R58 and later operating outside supplied EVN tables; Sông Hậu II 2,120 MW is in PDP8 delayed coal list; Sông Hậu III is only potential in E542/PDP7. **Confidence: HIGH Sông Hậu I; MEDIUM/LOW Sông Hậu II; LOW Sông Hậu III.** Sources: PDP7A-1, PDP8-Coal-Delayed, R58, E542-Coal.

- **Nam Định I / II and Rạng Đông** — Nam Định I 1,200 MW BOT appears in PDP7A/PDP8 delayed list; Nam Định II/Rạng Đông are planning/potential sites in PDP7/E542. **Confidence: MEDIUM Nam Định I; LOW others.** Sources: PDP7A-2, PDP8-Coal-Delayed, E542-Coal.

- **Quảng Trị coal** — 1,320 MW in PDP8 delayed list; reports show BOT delay and possible slippage. **Confidence: MEDIUM planned; LOW current viability.** Sources: PDP8-Coal-Delayed, R58, E542-Coal.

- **Công Thanh** — 600 MW Thanh Hóa IPP appears in PDP7A/R58/E542 as delayed by financing/land issues; PDP8 lists “Cẩm Thanh” 600 MW delayed, likely same/related project but name conflict is not resolved. **Confidence: LOW/MEDIUM.** Sources: PDP7A-1, PDP8-Coal-Delayed, R58, E542-Coal.

- **Quỳnh Lập I–II** — 1,200 MW each in PDP7A/R58/E542; Quỳnh Lập/Nghi Sơn later appears as potential LNG replacement location in PDP8. **Confidence: LOW for coal implementation; MEDIUM for planning history.** Sources: PDP7A-2/3, R58, PDP8-LNG.

- **Hải Hà CHP / industrial coal** — Industrial/cogeneration sources in Quảng Ninh; PDP7A lists Hải Hà 1 as 3×50 MW and later phases; PDP8 Industrial lists Hải Hà 1 300 MW, Hải Hà 2–4 600 MW each. **Confidence: LOW/MEDIUM due captive/industrial status and inconsistent capacities.** Sources: PDP7A-1/2/3, PDP8-Industrial, E542-Coal.

- **Formosa Hà Tĩnh / Đồng Nai** — Formosa appears in EVN2011 as 150 MW; PDP7A lists multiple Hà Tĩnh coal and blast-furnace-gas units and Formosa Đồng Nai #3 150 MW. PDP8 Industrial lists Formosa HT2 650 MW. **Confidence: MEDIUM; captive industrial units need separate reconciliation.** Sources: EVN2011, PDP7A-1, PDP8-Industrial, E542-Coal.

- **Vedan** — EVN2011 lists Vedan 72 MW as gas turbine/foreign IPP; PDP7A lists “NĐ than Vê Đan” 60 MW cogeneration. Fuel classification conflicts. **Confidence: LOW.** Sources: EVN2011, PDP7A-1.

### Domestic gas and oil/gas fleet

- **Phú Mỹ 1 / 2.1 / 2.2 BOT / 3 BOT / 4** — Bà Rịa–Vũng Tàu gas complex. EVN2011 lists Phú Mỹ 1 1,090 MW, 2.1 900 MW, 4 450 MW, 2.2 BOT 740 MW, 3 BOT 740 MW; EVN2018 gives updated EVN capacities for 1/2.1/4. **Confidence: HIGH.** Sources: EVN2011, EVN2017, EVN2018.

- **Bà Rịa** — EVN2011/2017/2018 list 389 MW gas plant under EVN JSC/GENCO 3. **Confidence: HIGH.** Sources: EVN2011, EVN2017, EVN2018.

- **Thủ Đức** — Legacy oil/gas turbines in HCMC. EVN2011 lists 165 MW oil and 128 MW gas turbine; EVN2018 lists 113 MW gas turbine (oil) plus 165 MW oil. PDP7 had planned retirement of Thủ Đức. **Confidence: MEDIUM.** Sources: EVN2011, EVN2018, PDP7.

- **Cần Thơ / Ô Môn I** — EVN2011 lists Cần Thơ oil/gas and Ô Môn I 330 MW oil; EVN2018 lists Cần Thơ gas turbine 155 MW, Cần Thơ S4 33 MW, Ô Môn I 660 MW oil. PDP8-Gas lists Ô Môn I 660 MW as existing plant to convert to Lô B gas. **Confidence: HIGH for capacities; MEDIUM for fuel/status transition.** Sources: EVN2011, EVN2018, PDP8-Gas.

- **Ô Môn II / III / IV** — Lô B domestic-gas CCGT chain. PDP8-Gas lists Ô Môn II, III and IV at 1,050 MW each; R32/R58 record earlier 750 MW planning and delay tied to Lô B. **Confidence: HIGH planned; MEDIUM for final unit sizing/COD.** Sources: PDP8-Gas, R32/R58.

- **Nhơn Trạch I–II / III–IV** — Nhơn Trạch I 465 MW and II 750 MW listed by EVN2011 under PetroVietnam; Nhơn Trạch 3–4 listed in PDP8-LNG at 1,624 MW, with R58 showing delay. **Confidence: HIGH for I/II; HIGH planned LNG III/IV but MEDIUM for COD.** Sources: EVN2011, PDP8-LNG, R58.

- **Cà Mau I–II** — EVN2011 lists Cà Mau I and II at 750 MW each under PetroVietnam. **Confidence: HIGH.** Source: EVN2011.

- **Dung Quất I–III / Miền Trung I–II / Quảng Trị gas** — PDP8-Gas lists Dung Quất I/II/III 750 MW each, Miền Trung I/II 750 MW each, and Quảng Trị 340 MW; fuel linked to Cá Voi Xanh or Báo Vàng gas. **Confidence: MEDIUM planned; gas-resource timing uncertain.** Sources: PDP8-Gas, R32/R58, E542-PL9.5A.

- **Kiên Giang I–II** — Earlier PDP7A/R32/R58 domestic-gas projects tied to fuel difficulties; PDP8 no longer lists them in domestic-gas table except as older planning lineage. **Confidence: LOW.** Sources: PDP7A-2, R32/R58.

## 7. Key unresolved items for a production-grade inventory

1. **Industrial/captive CHP:** Formosa, Hải Hà, Vedan, Lee & Man, Long Sơn petrochemical and Hòa Phát require company-level and licence-level validation.
2. **Retirements:** Uông Bí 105, Ninh Bình 100, Thủ Đức and Cần Thơ legacy units require current licence/dispatch confirmation.
3. **Technology classes:** Many Vietnamese primary planning sources provide capacity but not boiler class; “Subcritical/Supercritical/USC” is therefore conservative or blank unless widely implied by unit vintage/size.
4. **PDP8 vs revised PDP8:** The project list in Decision 500/262 continues to matter, but 2025 revised PDP8 changes capacity envelopes and transition rules. Project-level 2025 appendix detail should be reconciled in a next version.
5. **Province changes / administrative names:** Huế/Thừa Thiên Huế and some planned “Miền Trung” gas sites need final siting confirmation.
