# 1. SECTOR OVERVIEW

## 1.1 Electricity Mix (2024 baseline)

In 2024, Vietnam's total electricity generation reached 308 TWh, with coal-fired power contributing the largest share at 148 TWh — 48% of the total. Hydropower followed with 98 TWh (32%), while solar and wind together produced 39 TWh (13%). Gas-fired power accounted for 23.4 TWh, or 7% of total generation.

On an installed-capacity basis, coal-fired power plants remain dominant, accounting for approximately 43% of total installed capacity. PDP8 projects that Vietnam's coal capacity will peak at 30.1 GW, compared to the 27.2 GW in operation as of June 2024.

## 1.2 Policy Framework

Vietnam's thermal generation is governed by a cascading hierarchy of power development plans:

- **PDP7** (Decision 1208/QĐ-TTg, July 2011): The original seventh Power Development Plan, now superseded.
- **Revised PDP7 (PDP7A)** (Decision 428/QĐ-TTg, March 2016): The revision that restructured the thermal pipeline, adding BOT coal projects and CCGT gas projects.
- **PDP8** (Decision 500/QĐ-TTg, May 2023): On May 15, 2023, the Vietnamese government released PDP8 for the period of 2021–2030, with a vision to 2050. Within PDP8, Vietnam cancels around 13 GW of planned coal capacity from the pipeline, while keeping a pipeline of new coal projects to around 13 GW.
- **Revised PDP8** (Decision 768/QĐ-TTg, April 2025): On 15 April 2025, the Prime Minister issued Decision 768 approving the revision to PDP8, superseding Decision 500. Subsequently, on 30 May 2025, Decision 1509/QĐ-BCT approved the implementation plan.

Key thermal-relevant policies within PDP8:
- There will be no additional development of new coal-fired thermal power. PDP8 allows only on-going coal-fired thermal projects currently under construction and under investment to continue.
- After 2030, no new coal-fired power plants will be developed; active plants 20+ years old will begin transitioning to biomass or ammonia, and plants over 40 years old that cannot switch will be retired.
- The initial PDP8 had a targeted capacity of 24,930 MW of domestic gas-based power by 2030, which has been revised to a range of 10,861–14,930 MW in the revised PDP8.

## 1.4 Current Challenges

1. **Coal dominance vs. transition**: Vietnam imported 42 million tonnes of thermal coal in 2024, a 27% year-on-year increase. Coal accounted for a record 64.6% of Vietnam's electricity in April 2024.

2. **LNG-to-power nascent**: Nhơn Trạch 3&4 is the first LNG-fired power project in Vietnam, only achieving COD in late 2025. PPA pricing for LNG plants remains unresolved.

3. **Gas supply delays**: The Block B (Ô Môn) and Cá Voi Xanh (Blue Whale) upstream gas projects face persistent delays, cascading into CCGT project timelines.

4. **Delayed coal projects**: Phả Lại (440 MW) and Ninh Bình (100 MW) are set to cease operation by 2030 if they cannot upgrade technology or improve emissions.

5. **Aging fleet**: Vietnam's oldest power station (Uông Bí) has passed 40 years; Phả Lại units started operations in the 1980s and will exceed 40 years within this decade.

# 2. PER-PLANT NARRATIVES

## 2.1 Operating Coal-Fired Plants

### Uông Bí (Quảng Ninh)
Vietnam's oldest thermal complex. The original plant (4×25 MW + 1×5 MW ≈ 105 MW) dates from the 1960s–1970s. Expansion 1 (2×150 MW = 300 MW, subcritical) was added circa 2005. Expansion 2 (1×330 MW, subcritical) was commissioned in 2011 per PDP7 annex. The original 105 MW block was scheduled for retirement in 2021 under PDP7A ("NĐ Uông Bí ngừng phát điện: 105 MW") and was absent from EVN AR 2018 GENCO listings. Extensions 1 and 2 remain operational under GENCO 1. The complex is targeted for full fuel conversion or replacement by 2050.

### Phả Lại (Hải Dương)
Phả Lại I (4×110 MW = 440 MW, subcritical, Soviet-era) was commissioned 1983–1986. Phả Lại II (2×300 MW = 600 MW, subcritical, Japanese-assisted) was commissioned 2001–2002. Both are operated by GENCO 2. Phả Lại (440 MW) is set to cease operation by 2030 if it cannot update technology or improve emissions. Phả Lại II (600 MW) is targeted for replacement by 2045.

### Ninh Bình (Ninh Bình)
100 MW subcritical plant (4×25 MW), originally commissioned in the 1970s. Operated by GENCO 3. Set to cease operation by 2030 if emission improvements are not made — one of the two earliest scheduled retirements.

### Hải Phòng I & II (Hải Phòng)
Hải Phòng I: 2×300 MW = 600 MW, subcritical, EVN/GENCO 2. Phase 1 (#1) commissioned ~2012, Phase 2 (#2) ~2013. Hải Phòng II: 2×300 MW = 600 MW, subcritical, EVN/GENCO 2. Commissioned 2013–2014 per PDP7 annex. Both phases now operated by GENCO 2. Combined 1,200 MW targeted for replacement by 2050.

### Quảng Ninh I & II (Quảng Ninh)
Quảng Ninh I: 2×300 MW = 600 MW, subcritical, GENCO 1. Commissioned ~2010. Quảng Ninh II: 2×300 MW = 600 MW, subcritical, GENCO 1. Commissioned 2014–2015 per PDP7 annex. Combined 1,200 MW targeted for replacement by 2050.

### Na Dương I (Lạng Sơn)
2×55 MW = 110 MW (originally listed as 111 MW), circulating fluidized bed (CFB), using local lignite. Operated by TKV. One of the few plants using domestic coal from an adjacent mine. In October 2025, Na Dương reportedly tested biomass co-firing.

### Cao Ngạn (Thái Nguyên)
115 MW (2×57.5 MW), CFB, using domestic coal. Operated by TKV. Targeted for replacement by 2050.

### Cẩm Phả I & II (Quảng Ninh)
Phase I: 2×150 MW = 300 MW, CFB, TKV. Commissioned ~2009–2010. Phase II: 1×300 MW, subcritical, TKV. Commissioned 2011 per PDP7 annex ("ND Cẩm Phả II: 300 MW, TKV, 2011"). Total ~600 MW operated by TKV.

### Sơn Động (Bắc Giang)
2×110 MW = 220 MW, CFB, TKV. Commissioned ~2010. Uses domestic coal. Targeted for replacement by 2050.

### Mạo Khê (Quảng Ninh)
2×220 MW = 440 MW, subcritical, TKV. Commissioned 2013 per PDP7 annex. Uses domestic coal.

### Nông Sơn (Quảng Nam)
~30 MW, small coal plant, TKV. Commissioned ~2013. At the threshold of the 30 MW scope.

### Vũng Áng I (Hà Tĩnh)
2×600 MW = 1,200 MW, subcritical, PVN/GENCO. Commissioned 2012 (#1) and 2013 (#2) per PDP7 annex. Uses imported coal. One of the first large imported-coal plants in the central region.

### Nghi Sơn I (Thanh Hóa)
2×300 MW = 600 MW, subcritical, GENCO 1. Commissioned 2013 (#1) and 2014 (#2) per PDP7/PDP7A annexes. Located in Nghi Sơn Economic Zone.

### Thái Bình I (Thái Bình)
2×300 MW = 600 MW, subcritical, EVN. Commissioned 2017 per Report 58 ("Đã vận hành đúng tiến độ"). Appeared in EVN AR 2018 under EVN direct.

### Duyên Hải I (Trà Vinh)
Originally 2×622 MW but commonly listed as 1,244–1,245 MW, subcritical, GENCO 1. Commissioned 2014–2016 per PDP7A timelines. Part of the Duyên Hải power center in the Mekong Delta.

### Duyên Hải III (Trà Vinh)
2×622 MW = ~1,245 MW, subcritical, GENCO 1. Commissioned 2016, "Đã vận hành sớm 6 tháng" (operated 6 months early).

### Duyên Hải III Extension (Trà Vinh)
1×660 MW, subcritical, EVN. Commissioned 2019, "Đáp ứng tiến độ" (met schedule).

### Vĩnh Tân II (Bình Thuận)
2×622 MW = 1,244 MW, supercritical, GENCO 3. Commissioned 2014–2015. Part of the Vĩnh Tân power center. Has faced significant community opposition over environmental impacts.

### Vĩnh Tân IV (Bình Thuận)
2×600 MW = 1,200 MW, subcritical, EVN. Commissioned 2017–2018, "Đã vận hành sớm 3 tháng" (operated 3 months early).

### Vĩnh Tân IV Extension (Bình Thuận)
1×600 MW, subcritical, EVN. Commissioned 2019, "Đúng tiến độ" (on schedule).

### Vĩnh Tân I (Bình Thuận, BOT)
2×600 MW = 1,200 MW, supercritical, BOT by China Southern Power Grid (CSG) and China Power Investment Holdings (CPIH). Commissioned 2018–2019, "Sớm tiến độ, trước 7 tháng" (7 months early).

### Mông Dương I (Quảng Ninh)
2×540 MW = 1,080 MW, subcritical, GENCO 3/EVN. Commissioned 2015–2016 per PDP7A timeline. Uses imported coal.

### Mông Dương II (Quảng Ninh, BOT)
2×600 MW = 1,200 MW, supercritical, AES-VCM BOT. Commissioned ~2015. One of the first foreign BOT coal projects.

### An Khánh I (Thái Nguyên)
2×50 MW = 100 MW, subcritical, IPP. Commissioned 2012 (#1) and 2013 (#2) per PDP7 annex.

### Thăng Long (Quảng Ninh)
2×300 MW = 600 MW, subcritical, IPP (Geleximco). Commissioned 2017–2018, "Đáp ứng, đã và đang đóng điện."

### Formosa Hà Tĩnh Phase 1 (Hà Tĩnh)
Captive power for the Formosa Ha Tinh Steel complex. Multiple units using coal and blast furnace gas. Per PDP7A: #2 (150 MW coal, 2016), #3-4 (2×100 MW BFG, 2016), #5 (150 MW coal, 2016) = ~500 MW in Phase 1. The 2011 EVN AR listed only "Formosa: 150 MW."

### Nghi Sơn II (Thanh Hóa, BOT)
2×600 MW = 1,200 MW, supercritical, Marubeni-KEPCO BOT. PDP7A planned COD 2021–2022; assessed as "Chậm tiến độ ~1 năm." Commissioned ~2022.

### Hải Dương BOT (Hải Dương)
2×600 MW = 1,200 MW, subcritical, Jaks Resources (Malaysia) BOT. Planned COD 2020–2021; assessed "Chậm 1 năm." Commissioned ~2021.

### Duyên Hải II (Trà Vinh, BOT)
2×600 MW = 1,200 MW (later reported as 1,320 MW), supercritical, Janakuasa (Malaysia) BOT. Duyên Hải II is a 1,320-MW coal-fired facility that came online in 2021.

### Sông Hậu I (Hậu Giang)
2×600 MW = 1,200 MW, subcritical, PVN. Planned COD 2019; assessed "Chậm tiến độ 2 năm," estimated COD 2021. Commissioned ~2022.

### Thái Bình II (Thái Bình)
2×600 MW = 1,200 MW, subcritical, PVN. Planned COD 2017–2018; assessed as "Chậm tiến độ 3 năm và vẫn còn rủi ro chậm tiếp." The 1,200 MW coal-fired power plant developed by PVN faced delays and financial difficulties but is nearing completion. Believed commissioned ~2023–2024.

## 2.2 Coal Plants Under Construction / Recently Completed

### Vũng Áng II (Hà Tĩnh, BOT)
2×665 MW = 1,330 MW, supercritical, Mitsubishi-KEPCO BOT. Vung Ang power station, at least 1860 MW in Ha Tinh, with multiple units. Unit 1 achieved COD in July 2025 per Vietnamese media.

### Vân Phong I (Khánh Hòa, BOT)
2×716 MW = 1,432 MW, supercritical, Sumitomo BOT. Van Phong power station, at least 1432 MW. The plant is expected to begin operations in 2025. As of July 2025, Vân Phong-1 was reportedly not operating at full capacity.

### Quảng Trạch I (Quảng Bình)
2×600 MW ≈ 1,403 MW, subcritical, EVN. Planned COD 2021–2022; assessed as "Chậm 1 năm." Still under construction per PDP8 Table 2. The plant is under construction with a capacity of 1,200 MW (capacity reports vary: PDP8 lists 1,403 MW).

### Long Phú I (Sóc Trăng)
2×600 MW = 1,200 MW, subcritical, PVN. Under construction but stalled. Assessed as "Chậm tiến độ có khả năng đến 48 tháng và còn có thể chậm tiếp do ảnh hưởng cấm vận của Mỹ." The EPC contractor (Power Machines, Russia) was sanctioned. Construction ~78% complete but suspended.

### Na Dương II (Lạng Sơn)
110 MW, CFB, TKV. Planned COD 2019; assessed as "Chậm tiến độ ~3 năm," COD ~2022. TKV stated target commercial operation remained 2026. EPC contract signed December 2023.

### An Khánh-Bắc Giang (Bắc Giang)
650 MW, subcritical, IPP. Listed in PDP8 Table 2 as under construction. Planned COD 2022–2023; assessed "Chậm 1 năm," COD ~2023.

## 2.4 Operating Gas-Fired Plants

- **Phú Mỹ II.1**: ~896–945 MW, CCGT, GENCO 3. COD ~2002–2005.
- **Phú Mỹ IV**: ~450–477 MW, CCGT, GENCO 3. COD ~2004.

- **Phú Mỹ III BOT**: 740 MW, CCGT, BOT (BP/Kyushu). COD ~2004.

### Bà Rịa (Bà Rịa-Vũng Tàu)

- **Nhơn Trạch I**: 465 MW, CCGT, PV Power. COD ~2008–2009.
- **Nhơn Trạch II**: 750 MW, CCGT, PV Power. Commissioned 2011 per PDP7 annex.

### Cà Mau I & II (Cà Mau)
- **Cà Mau I**: 750 MW, CCGT, PV Power. COD ~2007–2008.
- **Cà Mau II**: 750 MW, CCGT, PV Power. COD ~2008.
Uses domestic gas from the PM3-CAA gas pipeline. Ca Mau Combined Cycle Power Plant, a 1,500-MW natural gas-fired power plant, came online in 2008.

### Ô Môn I (Cần Thơ)
660 MW, currently operates on oil (FO/DO), GENCO 2. Originally commissioned with 2 OCGT units (~330 MW) + steam (~330 MW). To be converted to domestic gas from Block B. Listed in PDP8 Table 5 as "chuyển sang sử dụng khí Lô B."

### Thủ Đức (HCM City)
Gas turbines: ~113–128 MW (OCGT), GENCO 3. Steam/oil: 165 MW, EVN. A legacy plant, scheduled for eventual retirement.

### Cần Thơ (Cần Thơ)
Gas turbine: 150–155 MW (OCGT), GENCO 2. Oil/steam unit: 33 MW. Planned for retirement under PDP7 annex II ("ND Cần Thơ ngừng chạy").

## 2.5 New LNG-Fired Plants

### Nhơn Trạch 3 & 4 (Đồng Nai)
PV Power fired up for the first time its 812 MW Nhon Trach 3 LNG-fired power plant; the project comprises two 812 MW units (Nhon Trach 3 & 4). Total investment of $1.4 billion, total capacity 1,624 MW. This is the first LNG-fired power project in Vietnam. Nhơn Trạch 3 COD was November 20, 2025; Nhơn Trạch 4 COD was December 20, 2025. EPC by Samsung C&T / LILAMA; GE Vernova equipment.

# 6. METHODOLOGICAL NOTES AND LIMITATIONS

1. **Capacity discrepancies**: The same plant often appears with slightly different capacities across EVN annual reports, PDPs, and third-party sources. For example, Phú Mỹ I shows 1,090 MW (2011), 1,108 MW (2017), and 1,118 MW (2018). This likely reflects uprating, different measurement standards (nameplate vs. net), or rounding. The most recent EVN figure is preferred unless contradicted.

2. **Technology classification**: Technology (subcritical/supercritical/USC) is not stated in the evidence-pack sources. Assignments are based on: (a) unit size heuristics (Vietnamese-built ≤300 MW units are typically subcritical; 600+ MW imported-technology units may be supercritical); (b) known EPC contractors; (c) financing conditions (JBIC policies distinguish supercritical from USC). Many assignments are MEDIUM confidence.

3. **Province attribution**: The evidence pack generally does not include province data except in Study E-542 PL9.2. Province assignments are based on well-known plant locations and cross-referenced against PL9.2 where available.

4. **COD dates**: For plants commissioned before 2016, CODs are estimated from PDP7 annex schedules (which were *planned* CODs) adjusted by the delay assessments in Reports 32/58. Some are rounded to the year.

5. **Status temporality**: The "as-of" date reflects the source date. PDP8 tables are as-of May 2023. Reports 32/58 are ~2019–2021. Web sources are 2024–2025. Status may have changed since the source date; the Notes field flags known subsequent changes.

6. **Cancelled projects**: Projects that appeared in PDP7/PDP7A but were dropped from PDP8 are classified as CXL. However, some may be "suspended" rather than formally cancelled, as Vietnamese planning rarely uses explicit cancellation language.

7. **Revised PDP8 (Decision 768, April 2025)**: This supersedes Decision 500 (original PDP8). Decision 1509 (May 2025) provides the implementation plan. The full project-by-project tables from the Revised PDP8 implementation plan were not available in the evidence pack. LNG and domestic gas project listings in this inventory are based on the original PDP8 (Decision 500) tables supplemented by press reporting on the Revised PDP8 implementation plan.

8. **Scope boundary**: Industrial captive plants (Formosa, Hòa Phát, Lee & Man, etc.) are included where capacity exceeds 30 MW and the plant appears in formal planning documents. Some smaller industrial CHP plants may exist that do not appear in any planning document and are therefore absent.
