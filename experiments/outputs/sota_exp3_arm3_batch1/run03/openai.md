### Per-asset / per-project explanatory notes

**Scope and classification note.** The table treats “thermal” as coal, domestic-gas, LNG, and industrial/cogeneration fossil or by-product-gas assets above 30 MWe. Oil-only historical units are included only where they were material system plants or are explicitly linked to retirement/replacement planning; because the requested fuel taxonomy does not include oil, those rows have deliberately low fuel-confidence notes.

**PDP8 temporality.** The evidence pack’s final statutory PDP8 source is Decision 500/QĐ-TTg dated **15 May 2023**. A later Revised PDP8 under Decision 768/QĐ-TTg dated **15 April 2025** exists and changes aggregate capacities; however, I did not locate a fully primary, per-project annex during this run. Therefore, rows grounded in PDP8 project lists use the 2023 annex unless a later primary/operator/public report is explicitly cited. A KPMG legal update states that Decision 768/QĐ-TTg revised PDP8 on 15 April 2025 and was followed by Decision 1509/QĐ-BCT on 30 May 2025, but because that is a secondary summary, it was used only for temporal awareness, not as a row-level project source. ([kpmg.com](https://kpmg.com/vn/en/home/insights/2025/07/revised-pdp-8.html?utm_source=openai))

**Vũng Áng II — HIGH existence/status confidence.** PDP8 2023 listed Vũng Áng II as a 1,330 MW coal plant under construction. 2026 reports state that the plant reached full operation/inauguration after Unit 1 entered commercial operation on **22 July 2025** and Unit 2/full capacity followed by **April 2026**. Capacity is recorded at 1,330 MW, following PDP8; technology is marked USC based on common project characterization, but unit-technology should be verified against VAPCO/Mitsubishi EPC documents for a formal primary line item. ([enerdata.net](https://www.enerdata.net/publications/daily-energy-news/vietnam-inaugurates-132-gw-vung-ang-ii-coal-fired-power-plant.html?utm_source=openai))

**Quảng Trạch I and II.** Quảng Trạch I is a coal project that PDP8 lists as 1,403 MW and under construction; Quảng Trạch II is no longer treated as a coal row in the final-current plan because PDP8’s LNG table explicitly says Quảng Trạch II was approved for conversion to LNG by Government Office Notice 54/TB-VPCP dated **25 February 2022**. The earlier Report 58 and PDP7A coal schedule are therefore treated as lineage, not current fuel status.

**Long Phú I.** Row-level existence is HIGH because PDP8 lists Long Phú I among coal plants under construction and E542 reports construction at 78% but temporarily stopped. Status is marked Shelved rather than Construction because a stopped project is not actively in EPC execution in the evidence pack; however, the 2023 PDP8 annex still classifies it under “đang xây dựng,” creating a real status tension.

**Sông Hậu II, Nam Định I, Quảng Trị, Vĩnh Tân III.** These projects are in PDP8 Table 3, specifically “delayed coal projects with financing/shareholder difficulties.” They are therefore not treated as cancelled, even though older schedules have slipped badly. Status is Shelved for Nam Định I, Quảng Trị, and Vĩnh Tân III where no current construction evidence is present, and Permitted for Sông Hậu II because the evidence indicates BOT contracting and a live PDP8 row, but not completed construction.

**Quỳnh Lập I and II.** Quỳnh Lập I was a Vinacomin 2×600 MW coal project in PDP7A and later progress reports; PDP8 2023 does not list it among coal plants under construction or delayed-financing coal plants, so it is treated as Shelved. Quỳnh Lập II has a capacity conflict: PDP7A used 2×600 MW, while E542 says POSCO was preparing an FS for 2×660 MW and the site could expand. The row uses the statutory PDP7A 1,200 MW and flags the 1,320 MW alternative in notes.

**Công Thanh / Cẩm Thanh ambiguity.** PDP7A and progress reports refer to **Công Thanh**; PDP8 Table 3 lists **Cẩm Thanh** 600 MW with a June 2024 deadline for implementation before termination review. This may be a transcription or OCR/name error for Công Thanh, but I did not merge them because the instruction says not to fabricate. Both rows appear, with Công Thanh as the historically known project and Cẩm Thanh as a low-confidence PDP8-name row.

**Ô Môn complex.** PDP8 2023 re-sizes Ô Môn II, III, and IV at 1,050 MW each and treats Ô Môn I as an existing plant converted to / using Lô B domestic gas. Older PDP7A and progress reports used 750 MW for Ô Môn II–IV. The table follows PDP8 for current/future capacity, while notes preserve the prior 750 MW lineage.

**Nhơn Trạch 3–4.** PDP8 lists 1,624 MW LNG. Public reports and PV Power communications after 2024 have described testing/commissioning progress, but exact unit COD/status should be pinned to PV Power or NLDC primary documents before changing status from Construction to Operating. The row therefore remains Construction as a conservative as-of 2025/2026 treatment.

**Sơn Mỹ I and II.** PDP8 treats both as 2,250 MW LNG plants for 2021–2030. Older progress reports show serious schedule risk. They are retained as Announced because the evidence pack does not show permits/construction.

**Bạc Liêu LNG.** PDP8 lists 3,200 MW as a firm LNG project. E542 says the project was already added to Revised PDP7 and at pre-feasibility stage. Because no construction/permit evidence is present in the supplied pack, status is Announced.

**Industrial by-product/cogeneration rows.** Formosa HT2, Hải Hà 1–4, Đức Giang, and Hòa Phát II come from PDP8 Table 4. Their fuels do not map cleanly into the requested three fuel categories. I classified coal-fired industrial boilers as Coal and by-product-gas plants as Domestic gas, but noted ambiguity where the table says blast-furnace gas, waste heat, or industrial by-products.

**Historical gas fleet — Phú Mỹ, Bà Rịa, Cà Mau, Nhơn Trạch I–II.** The EVN annual report extract from 2011 is the main broad primary roster for non-EVN/PVN/BOT gas assets. Later EVN rosters cover only EVN and GENCO plants, so BOT/PVN plants are often absent from 2017/2018 EVN tables despite continuing operation. That is why several rows are MEDIUM rather than HIGH.

**Retirement rows — Uông Bí old, Thủ Đức, Cần Thơ, Hiệp Phước.** Retirement status comes mainly from PDP7/PDP7A retirement/removal entries and older EVN capacity rosters. Where no post-retirement official closure confirmation is in the evidence pack, confidence is capped at MEDIUM.

**Generic PDP7 placeholders.** Rows such as “Than miền Bắc 1000 MW” and “Than miền Nam 1000 MW” are included because the user requested shelved/cancelled future assets and “known plants even without a primary source rather than omitting them.” These are not real project companies or sites; they are planning placeholders and are marked LOW.
