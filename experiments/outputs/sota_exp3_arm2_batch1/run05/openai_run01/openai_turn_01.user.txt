Evidence and confidence rules:
- Source 1 and Source 2 should be admissible, independently consulted sources that actually support the row attributes. Primary sources are official government documents, regulator-aggregated official data, and operator/owner filings or press releases. Admissible secondary sources include international-institution reports, bylined trade press, industry trackers/data brokers that expose underlying sources, EPC/financier disclosures, and credible company reports.
- Not admissible for final justification: Wikipedia, Wikidata, DBpedia, mirrors, unsourced aggregators, and repository datasets that do not expose per-value sources. If such a source gave a lead but no admissible source is found, keep the asset if likely real, set Source fields to 'not found' as appropriate, mark LOW, and mention the lead type only in Notes.
- Confidence: HIGH = at least two independent concordant sources, at least one primary. MEDIUM = one primary source, or a sourced aggregator citing a verifiable primary. LOW = secondary only, inferred, unresolved conflict, stale/uncorroborated, or not found. Merge sources sharing one origin; two citations from the same original source do not create HIGH confidence.
- Hard ceilings: commercial operator self-reported but not officially confirmed is at most MEDIUM; status supported only by sources older than 24 months is at most MEDIUM, and older than 48 months is LOW; any 'not found' row is LOW.
- Use exact status terms only: Announced, Pre-permit, Permitted, Construction, Operating, Shelved, Cancelled, Retired.
- Each row should normally be a plant or distinct unit-group, not a whole power center, when units differ by capacity, COD, owner/developer, fuel, status, financing, or technology. Aggregate only where detail is unavailable and mark ambiguity.
- Record electrical MWe nameplate. If sources do not specify gross vs net, note 'gross/net unspecified'. Do not mix heat, boiler, steam, investment-package, or thermal MW with electrical MWe.
