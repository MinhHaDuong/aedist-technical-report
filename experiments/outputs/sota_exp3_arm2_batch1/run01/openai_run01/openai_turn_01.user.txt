Produce a complete, primary-sourced reference inventory of Vietnam's past, present, and future thermal generation assets greater than 30 MWe, including operating, retired, cancelled, shelved, announced, permitted, pre-permit, and construction assets. This is a multi-turn task under budget; do not produce the final report until you have completed a discovery pass and a source-verification pass, unless the harness forces a final answer.

Required final output: a single Markdown document that begins directly with the inventory table, with no title, no preamble, and no overview section. The plant inventory must be exactly one pipe table, never split into sub-tables. Columns in this exact order: Name (Vietnamese), Name (English), Province, Fuel (Coal / Domestic gas / Imported LNG), Technology (Subcritical / Supercritical / USC for coal; CCGT / OCGT for gas), Units × MW, Total MWe, Status, Status as-of-date, COD, Owner/Developer, Confidence, Source 1, Source 2, Notes. After the table, add per-asset/project explanatory notes only for major, disputed, ambiguous, or historically important assets. Then add an annotated bibliography of every cited source, with full citation, URL when available, original-language title plus English translation for Vietnamese/non-English titles, and a short annotation of what was drawn from it. Do not add statistical summaries, cross-tabs, or additional pipe tables; if needed, put aggregate observations in prose.

Research plan to execute:
1. Discovery pass: build a candidate list from Vietnam PDP documents, EVN/PVN/PV Power/Vinacomin/Petrovietnam/Genco/operator pages, MOIT/ERAV documents, provincial approvals, ADB/World Bank/JICA documents, GEM or similar trackers as leads only, and reputable trade press. Include all coal, domestic gas, and imported LNG power assets >30 MWe, including small older thermal plants, captive/industrial plants if grid-scale generation assets, BOT/IPP projects, retired plants, and cancelled/shelved PDP projects.
2. Verification pass: for each row, obtain at least one admissible source that supports existence, province/location, fuel, capacity/unit configuration, status/as-of date, COD or planned COD, and owner/developer. Prefer two independent sources with at least one primary. Treat re-syndicated/common-origin sources as one source. If a lead cannot be traced to an admissible source, include it anyway only if plausibly in-scope, set Source 1/2 to 'not found' as appropriate, mark LOW, and explain in Notes.
3. Confidence assignment: HIGH = at least two independent concordant sources with at least one primary; MEDIUM = one primary or a sourced aggregator citing a verifiable primary; LOW = secondary only, inferred, unresolved conflict, stale unconfirmed status, or not found. Apply hard ceilings: operator-only unconfirmed claims cap at MEDIUM; status based only on a source older than 24 months caps at MEDIUM, older than 48 months caps at LOW; 'not found' rows are LOW.
4. Asset granularity: default row is a plant/unit-group, not a broad power center. Split co-located plants if capacity, COD, owner/developer, fuel, status, or financing arrangements differ. Aggregate only when detailed evidence is unavailable, mark ambiguous, and explain in Notes. Do not split identical units of the same plant unnecessarily.
5. Status terms must be exactly one of: Announced, Pre-permit, Permitted, Construction, Operating, Shelved, Cancelled, Retired. Use nameplate electrical MWe. If gross/net is unspecified, record the stated MW and note 'gross/net unspecified'. Ensure Units × MW reconciles with Total MWe or explain the discrepancy.
6. Temporality: every status needs a Status as-of-date, preferably the date of commissioning, approval, cancellation, latest official plan, current operator page, or source publication. Use concrete dates, not 'current'. Distinguish source publication date from the fact date where possible.

Search discipline under budget: batch searches by source family and technology. First gather official planning/regulator sources (PDP7 revised, PDP8, implementation plans, MOIT/ERAV, Vietnam Energy Magazine if needed only as secondary), then operator portfolios, then individual ambiguous projects. Keep an internal candidate matrix; do not spend heavily trying to perfect straightforward operating plants once two adequate sources are found. If budget becomes tight, prioritize recall, source fields, confidence calibration, and short notes over narrative detail.

On early planning turns, return a compact progress report: candidate source families searched, provisional asset groups found, major uncertainties, and next searches. On the final report turn, output only the required Markdown document beginning with the inventory table.
