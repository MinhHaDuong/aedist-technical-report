Produce the Phase B deliverable: a complete, primary-sourced Markdown reference inventory of Vietnam thermal generation assets greater than 30 MWe, including past, present, future, shelved, cancelled, and retired assets.

Do not draft the final report immediately unless you have already gathered enough evidence. You may use up to three planning/search turns before the report. In early turns, search and build a working checklist of assets and source families; keep visible output brief and focused on gaps, not prose. The final report must begin directly with the inventory table, with no title, preamble, or overview.

Mandatory final structure:

1. Exactly one Markdown pipe table for the plant inventory. Do not split it into sub-tables. Columns must appear in this exact order and with these exact names:
Name (Vietnamese) | Name (English) | Province | Fuel (Coal / Domestic gas / Imported LNG) | Technology (Subcritical / Supercritical / USC for coal; CCGT / OCGT for gas) | Units × MW | Total MWe | Status | Status as-of-date | COD | Owner/Developer | Confidence | Source 1 | Source 2 | Notes

2. Per-asset or per-project explanatory notes only where warranted: major, disputed, ambiguous, historically important, cancelled/shelved, or low-confidence records. Keep straightforward operating-asset notes compact or omit them if the table Notes field is enough. Notes must qualify existence/status confidence and, where useful, disputed attributes such as capacity, owner, COD, fuel, technology, or location.

3. Annotated bibliography of every source cited. Include full citation, URL when available, original-language title plus English translation for Vietnamese/non-English sources, and a short annotation saying exactly what was drawn from the source.

Scope and row rules:
- Include all Vietnam thermal generation assets >30 MWe using coal, domestic gas, or imported LNG at any lifecycle stage: announced, pre-permit, permitted, construction, operating, shelved, cancelled, retired.
- One row normally equals one plant or coherent unit group. Split co-located assets when capacity, COD, owner/developer, fuel, status, or financing arrangement differs. Aggregate to a power-center row only when detailed evidence is unavailable; mark it ambiguous.
- Do not split a single plant into multiple rows when units share commissioning pattern, owner, status, fuel, and technology; represent as Units × MW.
- Record electrical nameplate MWe. If gross/net is unspecified, state that in Notes. Ensure Units × MW arithmetic matches Total MWe or explain the discrepancy.

Status terms must be exactly one of: Announced, Pre-permit, Permitted, Construction, Operating, Shelved, Cancelled, Retired. Use Global Energy Monitor-style meanings: Announced = in plans/filings only; Pre-permit = approvals being sought; Permitted = approvals received but construction not started; Construction = site prep or EPC underway; Operating = formally commissioned; Shelved = halted without formal cancellation; Cancelled = formally cancelled/replaced; Retired = permanently closed/decommissioned.

Source-quality rules:
- Discovery may use parametric knowledge, tertiary pages, Wikipedia, mirrors, general search, and unsourced trackers only to find leads.
- Final justification may cite only admissible sources: official government documents; regulator-aggregated official data; operator filings/press releases; international-institution reports; bylined trade press; industry trackers/data brokers that expose the primary they cite.
- Not admissible for final source columns: Wikipedia/Wikidata/DBpedia, encyclopedia pages, mirrors, re-syndicated aggregators, or deposited datasets that do not expose per-value primary sourcing.
- If a plant is known only from inadmissible leads and no admissible source is found, include it anyway with Source 1 = 'not found', Source 2 = 'not found', Confidence = LOW, and explain the lead/source problem in Notes without treating the lead as justification.

Confidence rules:
- HIGH = at least two independent concordant admissible sources, with at least one primary.
- MEDIUM = one primary source, or a sourced aggregator citing a verifiable primary.
- LOW = secondary only, inferred, unresolved conflict, stale status beyond hard ceilings, or source not found.
- Check independence before assigning confidence; sources sharing an origin count as one source.
- Operator self-report not confirmed by official source caps affected claims at MEDIUM.
- Status attested only by a source older than 24 months and unconfirmed since caps status at MEDIUM; older than 48 months caps it at LOW.
- Source = not found rows are LOW by construction.
