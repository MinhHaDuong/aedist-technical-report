## **1. Sector Overview**
### **Electricity Mix and Policy Framework**
- **Installed Capacity (2026):** 80,555 MW total; **thermal power (coal, gas, LNG) = 56% of generation, 33.2% of capacity (26,757 MW coal, ~10% gas, 2% LNG)**.
- **Policy Drivers:**
  - **PDP8 (2021–2030, vision to 2050):** Targets **30,127 MW coal, 22,400 MW LNG, 14,930 MW domestic gas** by 2030.
  - **JETP Commitment:** Cap coal at **30.2 GW by 2030**, phase out unabated coal by 2050.
  - **LNG Expansion:** **22.5 GW LNG capacity by 2030** (from 0.8 GW in 2023).
  - **Nuclear Revival:** **4,000–6,400 MW** planned for 2030–2035.
- **Key Actors:** MOIT (policy), EVN (transmission/14.7% generation), PetroVietnam (gas/LNG), Vinacomin (coal supply).
- **Challenges:** Coal dependence (6 GW under construction), LNG delays (only 1.6 GW operational), grid constraints (14.5% reserve margin in north).

## **4. Per-Plant Narratives**
### **A. Vung Ang 2 Thermal Power Plant**
- **Key Facts:** 1,320 MW USC coal plant, inaugurated April 2026. BOT contract with Mitsubishi (60%) and KEPCO (40%).
- **Significance:** First USC coal plant in Vietnam; supplies ~3% of national demand.
- **Challenges:** Typhoon damage in 2025 delayed full operation.

### **B. Nhon Trach 3 & 4 LNG Power Plant**
- **Key Facts:** Vietnam’s first LNG-fired plants (1,624 MW), operational 2026. Financed without government guarantees.
- **Significance:** Milestone for private investment in LNG; flexible fuel-switching to hydrogen.
- **Challenges:** Securing long-term LNG supply and PPAs with EVN.

### **C. Song Hau 2 Thermal Power Plant**
- **Status:** Planned (2,000 MW), but delayed due to financing and resettlement issues.
- **Discrepancy:** BOT contract signed in 2020, but no construction progress as of May 2026. Confidence: MEDIUM.
