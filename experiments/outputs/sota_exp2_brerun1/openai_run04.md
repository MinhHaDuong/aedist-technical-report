# Vietnam thermal-generation reference inventory (>30 MWe) — coal, domestic gas, imported LNG

**Coverage date:** 23 May 2026.  
**Scope:** Vietnam thermal generation assets and formal planning-cycle projects >30 MWe using **coal**, **domestic gas**, or **imported LNG**, including operating, retired/reserve where identified, under-construction, planned, suspended/cancelled, captive/CHP/off-grid where admissible evidence was found.  
**Capacity basis:** nameplate electrical MW where public sources state MW; gross/net distinctions are noted where material.

---

## 1. Sector overview

Vietnam’s thermal fleet is dominated by coal and domestic gas, while imported LNG is the main planning category for new gas-fired capacity. EVN’s national overview and annual-report material report that coal-fired power was the largest installed-capacity category at end-2023 and that domestic gas supply to power generation remained constrained in 2023. **S01, S02**

Decision **262/QĐ-TTg dated 1 April 2024**, approving the PDP8 implementation plan, is the key official planning-cycle source for coal, domestic-gas and LNG projects to 2030. The implementation plan listed priority portfolios including **coal thermal power**, **domestic gas thermal power**, and **LNG thermal power**. **S03**

The revised PDP8, approved by **Decision 768/QĐ-TTg dated 15 April 2025**, and implementation guidance under **MOIT Decision 1509/QĐ-BCT dated 30 May 2025**, retained a substantial conventional buildout. KPMG’s summary of the official revised plan reports 2030 targets of **31,055 MW coal**, **10,861–14,930 MW domestic gas**, and **22,524 MW LNG**. **S04**

Recent large coal additions include **Nghi Sơn 2 BOT**, **Thái Bình 2**, **Sông Hậu 1**, and **Vân Phong 1 BOT**. Recent gas/LNG additions under construction or commissioning include **Nhơn Trạch 3–4 LNG** and **Ô Môn IV**. **S18, S20, S21, S22, S24, S31**

## 2. Concise narratives for important / disputed / ambiguous assets

### Phả Lại 1 and Phả Lại 2
EVN’s coal-development history gives **Phả Lại 1 = 4 × 110 MW, COD 1983–1986** and **Phả Lại 2 = 2 × 300 MW, COD 2002–2003**. EVNGENCO2 2024 reporting still refers to Phả Lại thermal units and notes fuel-conversion study needs for Phả Lại 1. The table therefore keeps both rows as operating/transition assets, capped at **MEDIUM** confidence because current unit-by-unit availability was not fully verified. **S05, S11**

### Uông Bí
Uông Bí is split between historical old units and modern extensions. This table includes **Uông Bí Extension 1, 300 MW**, and **Uông Bí Extension 2, 330 MW** as operating coal assets. Old Soviet-era Uông Bí legacy capacity is not aggregated into the 630 MW modern row. **S05, S10**

### Duyên Hải complex
Duyên Hải is split into **Duyên Hải 1**, **Duyên Hải 3**, and **Duyên Hải 3 Extension**. EVN’s 2024 Duyên Hải source states that Duyên Hải Thermal Power Company operates Duyên Hải 1 and Duyên Hải 3 with **2,490 MW** total design capacity and also operates the **688 MW** Duyên Hải 3 Extension. **S09**

### Vĩnh Tân complex
Vĩnh Tân is split into **Vĩnh Tân 1 BOT**, **Vĩnh Tân 2**, and **Vĩnh Tân 4 including extension** because ownership/project identity differs. EVNGENCO3 sources confirm **Vĩnh Tân 2 = 1,244 MW** and Vĩnh Tân 4 project material confirms **2 × 600 MW** for the main plant; the 600 MW extension is included in the Vĩnh Tân 4 row to reconcile the operating complex. **S06, S07**

### Phú Mỹ gas center
Phú Mỹ is split into EVNGENCO3-operated domestic-gas CCGT plants and BOT plants. EVNGENCO3 confirms that Phú Mỹ Thermal Power Company operates four plants totaling **2,540 MW**, while wider Phú Mỹ power-center references include Phú Mỹ 2.2 BOT and Phú Mỹ 3 BOT, bringing the center above 4,000 MW. **S07, S13**

### Nhơn Trạch 3–4
Nhơn Trạch 3–4 are classified as **Imported LNG, CCGT, under construction/commissioning**. PV GAS reports a 25-year LNG supply contract for the two plants, effective 2025, and gives combined capacity of **1,624 MW**. **S31**

### Ô Môn / Block B gas chain
Ô Môn is treated as a domestic-gas chain. Ô Môn 1 is an existing plant with fuel-conversion relevance; Ô Môn 2 and 3 are formal planned domestic-gas CCGT projects; **Ô Môn IV, 1,155 MW**, has an EPC award and scheduled operation by December 2028. **S03, S24**

### Vân Phong 1 capacity discrepancy
Vân Phong 1 is reported as **1,432 MW** in Vietnam News/VNA inauguration coverage and Decision 262 summaries, while some investor/transaction material reports **1,320 MW**. This inventory uses **1,432 MWe** and notes that 1,320 MW may be a net/contract or transaction figure. **S20, S23**

### Vũng Áng 2
Vũng Áng 2 is treated as **under construction**, with **1,330 MW** used as the row capacity. Public material varies between approximately 1,200–1,330 MW; confidence is therefore **MEDIUM**. **S03, S25**

### Captive / CHP assets
Only captive/CHP assets with at least some admissible public evidence are retained. **Formosa Hà Tĩnh** is included as a captive operating coal-power lead because Hà Tĩnh provincial coverage reports stable operation of its internal power-plant system and annual electricity output. Capacity remains **LOW** confidence because source material confirms output and operation more clearly than electrical nameplate. **S32**
