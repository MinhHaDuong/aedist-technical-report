# **Vietnam Thermal Generation Asset Inventory (>30 MWe)**
**As of May 23, 2026**

---

## **1. Sector Overview**

### **Electricity Mix and Policy Framework**
- **Total Installed Capacity (2025):** 82,387 MW.
  - **Thermal (coal, gas, oil):** 45–50% (37,410 MW).
  - **Coal:** 32.5% (26,757 MW).
  - **Gas and oil:** 10.5% (8,653 MW).
  - **Hydropower:** 28.7% (23,664 MW).
  - **Renewables (wind, solar):** 26% (21,447 MW).
- **Generation Output (Q1 2026):** Coal 56.5%, gas 6.4%, hydropower 19.1%, renewables 16%.
- **Policy Drivers:**
  - **PDP8 (2021–2030, vision to 2050):** No new coal plants after 2025; peak coal capacity at 30,127 MW by 2030, then phase out by 2050. Gas and LNG to expand to 28–33 GW by 2030, with LNG imports rising to 8–15 MMTPA.
  - **JETP:** $15.5 billion to cap and phase out unabated coal, accelerate renewables.
  - **Grid Upgrades:** 500 kV Quang Trach–Pho Noi line completed in 2024.

### **Key Institutional Actors**
- **Ministry of Industry and Trade (MOIT):** Oversees PDP8 implementation, approves projects, sets tariffs.
- **Vietnam Electricity (EVN):** State-owned utility, dominant in generation, transmission, and distribution.
- **PetroVietnam (PVN):** Controls domestic gas production and LNG imports.
- **Vinacomin:** State-owned coal producer and thermal plant operator.
- **Independent Power Producers (IPPs):** Marubeni, Mitsubishi, T&T Group, AES, Sumitomo, GS Energy, VinaCapital.

### **Current Challenges**
- **Grid Constraints:** Transmission bottlenecks, especially in the north, led to 2.56 billion kWh of emergency imports from China in 2024.
- **Fuel Supply:** Domestic gas production is declining, increasing reliance on LNG imports (Thi Vai terminal expansion).
- **ESG Pressures:** Coal consumption hit a record in 2024, clashing with Net Zero 2050 commitment.
- **Project Delays:** Many large-scale thermal and hydropower projects are behind schedule.

## **2. Per-Plant Narratives**

### **Coal-Fired Plants**
- **Vũng Áng-1 (1,200 MW, Hà Tĩnh):** Operational since 2015 (2 × 600 MW), owned by PetroVietnam. Expected to consume 3 million tons of coal in 2026.
- **Vũng Áng-2 (1,320 MW, Hà Tĩnh):** Ultra-supercritical (USC), developed by Mitsubishi (60%) and KEPCO (40%) under BOT. Unit 1 operational July 2025, Unit 2 delayed to Q1 2026 by storm.
- **Mông Dương-2 (1,200 MW, Quảng Ninh):** Under construction, expected COD 2025–2027.
- **Vân Phong-1 (1,320 MW, Khánh Hòa):** Under construction, Sumitomo (75%), Aboitiz Power (25%). Expected COD 2025.
- **Quảng Trạch 1 (1,200 MW, Quảng Bình):** Under construction, expected COD 2025.
- **Nghi Sơn-2 (1,200 MW, Thanh Hóa):** Operational since 2022, owned by Marubeni and KEPCO (BOT).
- **Na Dương-1 (100 MW, Lạng Sơn):** Operational since 2015, biomass co-firing test in 2025.
- **Duyên Hải 2 (1,200 MW, Trà Vinh):** Operational since 2015, owned by EVN.
- **Vĩnh Tân-1 (1,245 MW, Bình Thuận):** Operational since 2018, owned by EVN.
- **Vĩnh Tân-3 (1,320 MW, Bình Thuận):** Under construction, Adani interested in 2024, Vingroup proposed LNG repowering.
- **Vĩnh Tân-4 (1,200 MW, Bình Thuận):** Operational since 2018, owned by EVN.

### **Gas-Fired Plants (Domestic Gas and LNG)**
- **Nhơn Trạch 3 & 4 (2 × 750 MW, Đồng Nai):** LNG-fired CCGT, developed by PVN. Expected COD Q2–Q3 2025.
- **Long Sơn (4,800 MW, Bà Rịa-Vũng Tàu):** LNG-fired CCGT, developed by Marubeni. Expected COD 2025.
- **Long An 1 & 2 (2 × 1,500 MW, Long An):** LNG-fired CCGT, developed by GS Energy and VinaCapital. Expected COD Dec 2025 (Unit 1), Dec 2026 (Unit 2).
- **Hải Phòng LNG (3,000 MW, Hải Phòng):** LNG-fired CCGT, developed by Vingroup and Vinenergo. Construction began March 2025, expected COD 2026.
- **Quang Ninh LNG (1,500 MW, Quảng Ninh):** Planned, expected COD 2028–2029.
- **Sơn Mỹ 1 & 2 (2.25 GW + 2.2 GW, Bình Định):** LNG-fired CCGT, developed by AES and PV Gas. Expected COD 2024–2026.
- **O Mon 4 (750 MW, Cần Thơ):** Domestic gas CCGT, EPC contract signed 2025, expected COD 2025.
- **Quảng Trạch 2 (1,500 MW, Quảng Bình):** LNG-fired CCGT, planned, part of Quang Trach Power Center.
- **Hiệp Phước (2,250 MW, TP. Hồ Chí Minh):** LNG-fired CCGT, planned, Phase 1 expected 2027+.
- **Chan May LNG (4,000 MW, Thừa Thiên-Huế):** Under construction, expected COD 2027.

### **Cancelled/Repurposed Projects**
- **Quảng Ninh-3 (1,200 MW):** Cancelled under PDP8.
- **Nam Định:** Originally coal, now eyed for LNG repowering.
