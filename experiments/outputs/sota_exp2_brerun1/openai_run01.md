**Scope:** Coal, domestic gas, imported LNG. Includes operating, retired/legacy where identified, under-construction, approved/planned, suspended, cancelled/terminated, captive/CHP, and formal-planning-cycle pre-FID projects.  
**Status cut-off:** 23 May 2026.  
**Capacity basis:** nameplate electrical MWe where available; gross/net ambiguities are stated in Notes.  
**Source convention:** every `Source 1` / `Source 2` value maps to the bibliography in Section 5.

## 1. Sector overview

Vietnam’s thermal fleet is dominated by coal in installed capacity and generation, while domestic gas generation is concentrated in the Southeast and Southwest gas-to-power clusters: Phú Mỹ, Bà Rịa, Nhơn Trạch, Cà Mau and Ô Môn. EVN’s 2023 system overview and EVN annual reporting provide the sector-level context for coal and gas fuel supply constraints. [S01], [S02]

The PDP8 implementation plan, approved by **Decision 262/QĐ-TTg dated 1 April 2024**, provides the main formal-planning baseline for thermal projects through 2030. Its thermal planning totals include **30,127 MW coal**, **14,930 MW domestic gas**, **22,400 MW LNG**, and **2,700 MW cogeneration / surplus-heat / blast-furnace-gas / process by-product power**. [S03], [S04]

The 2025 revised PDP8, approved by **Decision 768/QĐ-TTg dated 15 April 2025**, and MOIT’s revised implementation guidance under **Decision 1509/QĐ-BCT dated 30 May 2025**, shifted the planning context further toward LNG, domestic-gas chains, flexible sources, and a limited number of transitional coal projects. [S05], [S06]

Operating coal capacity is concentrated in Quảng Ninh, Hải Dương, Hải Phòng, Thanh Hóa, Hà Tĩnh, Bình Thuận, Trà Vinh, Thái Bình and Hậu Giang, with smaller Vinacomin/TKV circulating-fluidized-bed coal plants serving mine-mouth or lower-grade-coal roles. [S07], [S08], [S09]

The LNG project pipeline is much less mature than the operating coal and domestic-gas fleet. Several LNG rows in this inventory are formal planning-cycle or approved/pre-FID projects rather than construction projects; those rows are retained because the requested scope includes approved, planned and pre-FID assets. [S03], [S04], [S27], [S28], [S29], [S30], [S31], [S32], [S33]

## 2. Concise sourced narratives for important / disputed / ambiguous assets

### Phú Mỹ gas complex

The Phú Mỹ complex is frequently reported as one gas hub, but it comprises distinct EVN/EVNGENCO3 and former BOT assets. EVNGENCO3 confirms **Phú Mỹ 2.2** was transferred/O&M-assumed on **4 February 2025** with **715 MW CCGT** capacity. Sembcorp confirms **Phú Mỹ 3** was transferred to EVN on **8 March 2024** after 20 years of BOT operation. [S12], [S13], [S14]

### Nhơn Trạch 3–4 LNG

Nhơn Trạch 3–4 is Vietnam’s first LNG-to-power project. PV Power signed the PPA with EVNEPTC on **4 October 2024**, signed major facility agreements in October 2024, and PV GAS signed a 25-year LNG supply contract for commercial operation. This inventory uses **2 × 812 MW = 1,624 MW**, while noting that some planning summaries round the pair to **1,500 MW**. [S15], [S16], [S17]

### Mông Dương 2 BOT

AES identifies Mông Dương 2 as a Quảng Ninh BOT coal plant using domestic coal and subject to transfer after 25 years. Capacity is reported inconsistently across sources, commonly around **1,120–1,242 MW**. This inventory uses **1,240 MW** from AES history and flags gross/net uncertainty. [S18], [S19]

### Vinacomin/TKV CFB coal plants

Vinacomin/TKV’s CFB fleet is consistently identified as Na Dương, Cao Ngạn, Sơn Động, Cẩm Phả, Mạo Khê/Đông Triều and Nông Sơn. **Nông Sơn is excluded from the table** because cited sources give **30 MW**, not greater than 30 MWe. Cẩm Phả is reported as **670 MW** in one Vinacomin source and **680 MW** in another technical summary; this inventory uses **670 MW**. [S07], [S08]

### Formosa Hà Tĩnh captive power

Formosa Hà Tĩnh is included as a captive industrial thermal asset. Provincial reporting confirms the internal power system produced over **3.5 billion kWh** in 2025 and coordinated with EVN. Engineering/project sources report a thermal-power complex around **650 MW**; other public infrastructure datasets show slightly higher figures. This inventory uses **650 MW** and marks the row **MEDIUM** because plant-level nameplate evidence is less transparent than for grid-connected EVN/BOT assets. [S34], [S35]

### Hòa Phát captive / residual-heat and coke/coal power

Hòa Phát’s captive thermal generation appears in two rows: Dung Quất and Hải Dương. Hòa Phát’s 2024 annual report identifies **Hòa Phát Dung Quất Thermal Power Plant No. 2** as **240 MW**, while older Hòa Phát material identifies the Hải Dương coke/thermal plant at **37 MW**. Both are retained because they exceed 30 MWe, but Hải Dương is marked **LOW** due to stale status evidence. [S36], [S37], [S38]

### Sông Hậu 2

Sông Hậu 2 was a **2,120 MW** BOT coal project. MOIT terminated the BOT contract in July 2024 after the investor failed to meet the financial-arrangement deadline. The row is classified as **Cancelled / BOT terminated** with status as of **1 July 2024**. [S24], [S25]

### LNG pipeline projects

Several LNG projects are approved or formally planned but not yet under construction. Quảng Ninh LNG has investor/project-company and transmission evidence; Long An I–II has provincial investment approval; Sơn Mỹ I–II have EDF/AES-led development evidence; Bạc Liêu LNG has a 2026 grid-connection agreement and targeted phased 2030 COD. These rows are therefore high or medium for planning existence, but not necessarily high for COD certainty. [S27], [S28], [S29], [S30], [S31], [S32], [S33]
