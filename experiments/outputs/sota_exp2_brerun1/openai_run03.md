# Vietnam Thermal Generation Assets >30 MWe — Corrected Markdown Reference Inventory  
**Scope:** Coal, Domestic gas, Imported LNG thermal generation assets in Vietnam greater than 30 MWe, including operating, retired/legacy, under-construction, approved, planned, suspended, cancelled, captive, CHP, off-grid, micro-grid where found, and formal planning-cycle pre-FID projects.  
**Inventory date:** 23 May 2026.  
**Capacity basis:** Nameplate electrical MWe where available; where an official plan provides only project capacity, that capacity is used and noted.  
**Source convention:** `Bxx` source labels in the table map to the annotated bibliography in Section 6. Every `Source 1` and `Source 2` cell points to a bibliography entry with a specific URL.

---

## 1. Sector overview

Vietnam’s thermal generation fleet consists of a large coal fleet built through EVN, PVN/PV Power, Vinacomin/TKV and BOT/IPP projects; legacy and modern domestic-gas CCGT/OCGT plants supplied by Nam Con Son, Cuu Long, PM3–CAA, Block B and planned Cá Voi Xanh/Báo Vàng gas; and a major formal planning pipeline of imported-LNG CCGT projects.

The current planning reference for future thermal projects is **MOIT Decision 1509/QĐ-BCT dated 30 May 2025**, approving the implementation plan for the adjusted PDP8. Its Appendix II.1 lists priority LNG projects, reserve LNG projects, coal plants under construction, coal projects facing implementation difficulties, industrial co-generation/by-product power projects, and domestic-gas plants.

For operating plants, current provenance is strongest for listed operating-company annual reports and official EVN/EVNGENCO/PV Power pages. For older legacy plants, captive industrial plants, and some BOT assets, publicly retrievable primary data are incomplete; such rows are retained for recall but marked MEDIUM or LOW as appropriate.

## 2. Concise narratives for important, disputed, or ambiguous assets

### Nhơn Trạch 3–4
Decision 1509 places **Nhơn Trạch 3 and Nhơn Trạch 4** in the LNG thermal-power table at **1,624 MW** with planned operation in **2025**. PV Power’s 2024 reporting also identifies Nhơn Trạch 3–4 as projects being prepared for commercial operation. They are therefore classified as **Imported LNG / CCGT**, not Domestic gas.

### Ô Môn I
Decision 1509 lists **Ô Môn I** at **660 MW** in the domestic-gas table and notes it is an existing plant to use **Block B gas**. The row is therefore classified as **Domestic gas** with status **Operating / conversion to Block B gas planned**.

### Vũng Áng II
Decision 1509 lists **Vũng Áng II** as a coal plant under construction at **1,330 MW**. VAPCO’s EIA confirms the project configuration as **2×665 MW gross**, and 2026 industry/press reports indicate entry into full commercial operation. Because the strongest official planning source still listed the plant as under construction in 2025 while later evidence indicates completion, the corrected row uses **Operating / recently completed** with a 2026 status note and MEDIUM confidence.

### Sông Hậu II
Decision 1509 lists **Sông Hậu II** under coal projects facing difficulties and notes that MOIT terminated the BOT contract by document **4579/BCT-ĐL dated 1 July 2024**. The row is therefore classified as **Cancelled / BOT terminated** with the status-change date **2024-07-01**.

### Quảng Trị coal vs Quảng Trị gas
The coal-fired **Quảng Trị** project and the domestic-gas **TBKHH Quảng Trị** project are separate planning identities. The coal project is listed as difficult/suspended, while the gas project is a 340 MW CCGT using Báo Vàng gas.

### Industrial CHP / by-product projects
Decision 1509 includes co-generation and industrial by-product power projects such as **Formosa HT2**, **Hòa Phát II**, **VNT19**, **Đức Giang**, and **Hải Hà** phases. Where the official plan does not specify a conventional fuel, the row is included but fuel classification is noted as constrained by the user’s allowed categories.
