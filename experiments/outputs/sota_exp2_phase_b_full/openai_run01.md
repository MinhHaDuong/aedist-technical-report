# Vietnam Thermal Generation Assets >30 MWe  
## Coal, Domestic Gas, and Imported LNG — Primary-Sourced Markdown Reference Inventory

**Scope:** Vietnam thermal generation assets greater than 30 MWe, including operating, retired/legacy, under-construction, approved, planned, suspended, cancelled, captive/CHP, and formal-planning-cycle pre-FID projects using **Coal**, **Domestic gas**, or **Imported LNG**.  
**As-of date:** **22 May 2026**.  
**Capacity basis:** Nameplate electrical MWe where available. Gross/net is often unspecified in Vietnamese public sources.  
**Confidence basis:**  
- **HIGH** = at least two independent concordant admissible sources, at least one primary.  
- **MEDIUM** = one primary source, or reliable secondary/trade/legal source tracing to primary evidence.  
- **LOW** = secondary only, unresolved conflict, stale/ambiguous status, or source not found.  

---

## 1. Sector overview

Vietnam’s thermal power system is anchored by a large coal fleet, a southern domestic-gas fleet built around Nam Côn Sơn, PM3–Cà Mau, Cửu Long, and Block B gas, and a fast-growing imported-LNG pipeline. EVN’s 2022–2023 annual report reported coal-fired installed capacity of **26,756 MW in 2023**, while EVN’s 2023 power-source overview highlighted continuing dependence on coal supply and constrained domestic gas availability. 

The main planning instruments used here are **PDP8 / Decision 500/QĐ-TTg of 15 May 2023**, **Decision 262/QĐ-TTg of 1 April 2024** implementing PDP8, and the 2025 revised PDP8 / revised implementation instruments. Decision 262’s implementation plan lists formal 2023–2030 project pipelines for domestic gas, imported LNG, coal, and other generation classes; its Appendix III includes specific domestic-gas and LNG project tables. 

Under PDP8 and revised PDP8, Vietnam has narrowed the new-coal pipeline. Legal summaries of the revised PDP8 identify a limited set of coal projects expected to continue toward completion by 2030, notably **Na Dương II, An Khánh–Bắc Giang, Vũng Áng 2, Quảng Trạch I, and Long Phú 1**. Other formerly planned coal projects are treated as suspended, cancelled, converted, or replacement candidates unless directly retained in current planning. 

Imported LNG has moved from planning into operation/construction. Decision 262 listed **Nhơn Trạch 3–4** as an under-construction imported-LNG power project in Đồng Nai, and later operator/EPC reporting described inauguration/commissioning of the Nhơn Trạch 3–4 complex in late 2025.

## 2. Concise narratives for important, disputed, or ambiguous assets

### Nhơn Trạch 3–4 — first LNG-to-power complex
Decision 262 lists **Nhơn Trạch 3 and Nhơn Trạch 4** as a **1,624 MW** imported-LNG project in Đồng Nai with 2024–2025 operation timing and under-construction status. Later EPC reporting states the complex was inaugurated in December 2025. The table uses **1,624 MWe** from the PDP8 implementation source; other sources round the capacity to about **1.6 GW**. 

### Phú Mỹ complex — domestic gas with LNG supplementation
EVNGENCO3 identifies the Phú Mỹ thermal company as operating **Phú Mỹ 1, Phú Mỹ 2.1, Phú Mỹ 2.1 Extension, and Phú Mỹ 4**, with aggregate capacity of **2,540 MW**. S&P Global separately reports Phú Mỹ 3 at about **715 MW** and identifies Phú Mỹ 2.1 and Phú Mỹ 4 as **477 MW** plants. EVNGENCO3 also reported regasified LNG use at Phú Mỹ in 2024, but the plants are retained as **Domestic gas** because their planning identity and normal fuel basis are domestic gas, with LNG supplementation noted. 

### Ô Môn gas-power chain — dependent on Block B
Decision 262 lists **Ô Môn I** as operating and identifies **Ô Môn II, III, and IV** as 1,050 MW domestic-gas CCGT projects in Cần Thơ using Block B gas. Because upstream Block B schedule drives downstream COD, the status of Ô Môn II–IV remains schedule-sensitive. 

### Quảng Trạch I and LNG Quảng Trạch II
Quảng Trạch is split in this inventory. **Quảng Trạch I** is treated as an under-construction coal plant retained in the revised-PDP8 coal completion set. **LNG Quảng Trạch II** appears separately in Decision 262’s LNG project list as a **1,500 MW** imported-LNG project. 

### Vĩnh Tân 3
Vĩnh Tân 1, 2, 4, and 4 Extension are operating coal assets or BOT assets. **Vĩnh Tân 3** is retained as a cancelled/suspended coal identity because current PDP8/revised-PDP8 summaries do not place it in the active coal completion set and legal commentary identifies delayed/abandoned coal projects outside the core retained buildout. Confidence is **MEDIUM**, not HIGH, because a direct cancellation decision was not captured in this pass. 

### Long Phú complex
**Long Phú 1** remains in the revised-PDP8 coal completion set and is listed as under construction/delayed. **Long Phú 2** and **Long Phú 3** are retained as suspended/cancelled identities because no current active-completion evidence was found; confidence is LOW where direct cancellation instruments were not located. 

### Captive and industrial CHP
Captive/CHP evidence is weaker than grid-connected assets. **Lee & Man Hậu Giang** is supported by a Vietnamese environment-sector article confirming a **50 MW coal-fired thermal unit** in operation, while tracker evidence reports up to **125 MW**. **Formosa Hà Tĩnh** is retained as a LOW-confidence captive coal row because only secondary/tracker evidence was found in this pass.

## 5. Verification and caveats

- **Duplicate identities:** Quảng Trạch I coal and LNG Quảng Trạch II are intentionally split. Quỳnh Lập coal and LNG Quỳnh Lập are intentionally split. Legacy Hiệp Phước and LNG Hiệp Phước are intentionally split.
- **Arithmetic:** Summary tables were recalculated from the structured rows. Aggregate rows are used where unit-level data were unavailable.
- **Technology:** Coal technology classifications are best-effort and sometimes capped at MEDIUM/LOW where primary technology evidence was not found.
- **Captive plants:** Industrial captive/CHP rows are less complete than grid-connected assets.
- **Backup LNG rows:** These are formal planning / backup-location identities, not necessarily investable projects.
- **Status freshness:** Operating rows are mostly supported by 2024–2025 sources; older status evidence is confidence-capped.
