# Vietnam Thermal Generation Assets >30 MWe — Corrected Reference Inventory

**Scope:** Coal, domestic gas, imported LNG.  
**Status date of inventory:** 23 May 2026.  
**Capacity basis:** nameplate electrical MWe where available; gross/net often unspecified in public sources.

---

## 1. Sector overview

EVN’s 2025 annual report reports Vietnam’s 2024 system installed capacity at **82,387 MW**, including **26,757 MW coal-fired** and **8,653 MW gas + oil-fired** capacity. Decision 262/QĐ-TTg approving the PDP8 Implementation Plan lists Vietnam’s priority thermal additions through 2030: domestic-gas projects, imported-LNG projects, and a limited coal pipeline consisting mainly of already-approved or advanced projects.

Decision 262 lists new or continuing coal projects: **Vân Phong 1, Vũng Áng II, Nà Dương II, Quảng Trạch I, An Khánh–Bắc Giang, Long Phú 1**, and a separate delayed coal group: **Quảng Trị, Công Thanh, Nam Định I, Vĩnh Tân III, Sông Hậu II**. For LNG, Decision 262 lists **Nhơn Trạch 3–4, Hiệp Phước LNG Phase 1, Bạc Liêu, Sơn Mỹ I/II, Quảng Ninh, Hải Lăng, Thái Bình, Nghi Sơn, Cà Ná, Quảng Trạch II, Quỳnh Lập, Long An I**, plus backup locations. Domestic gas additions are concentrated in the **Ô Môn / Block B**, **Cá Voi Xanh**, and **Báo Vàng** chains.

## 2. Concise narratives for important / disputed / ambiguous assets

### Nhơn Trạch 3–4 LNG
Decision 262 lists Nhơn Trạch 3–4 as **1,624 MW**, Đồng Nai, **under construction**, operation **2024–2025**. PV Power describes the project as Vietnam’s first LNG power plant project and also uses rounded commercial descriptions of about **1,500 MW**. This inventory uses **2 × 812 MW = 1,624 MW**.

### Phú Mỹ complex
EVNGENCO3/EVN sources identify Phú Mỹ Thermal Power Company as operating **four CCGT plants** — Phú Mỹ 1, Phú Mỹ 2.1, Phú Mỹ 2.1 Extension and Phú Mỹ 4 — totaling **2,540 MW**. The inventory keeps those as one grouped row because the public primary sources retrieved give a reliable grouped figure, while Phú Mỹ 2.2 BOT and Phú Mỹ 3 BOT are listed separately at **715 MW** each.

### Ô Môn chain
Decision 262 lists Ô Môn I as an existing **660 MW** plant to switch to Block B gas, and lists Ô Môn II, IV and III at **1,050 MW each**. A MOIT/VIOIT source describes the chain as Ô Môn I 2×330 MW, Ô Môn II 1,050 MW ±10%, and Ô Môn III–IV 2×1,050 MW ±10%.

### Quảng Trạch I
Decision 262 lists Quảng Trạch I as **1,403 MW**, Quảng Bình, **under construction**, operation **2026**. A 2025 provincial/EVN event report states the project has **2 units**, **1,403 MW**, **ultra-supercritical technology**, and about **94.5%** overall progress as of 18 August 2025.

### Delayed coal projects
Decision 262 lists Quảng Trị, Công Thanh, Nam Định I, Vĩnh Tân III and Sông Hậu II as delayed coal projects with shareholder/capital issues and an extension deadline to June 2024. Because final termination or conversion decisions were not consistently verified, they are retained as **suspended / delayed / termination-risk** rather than definitively cancelled.
