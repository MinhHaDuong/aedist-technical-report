### Key Policy Context
- Total thermal capacity targets by 2030: domestic gas 14,930 MW; LNG 22,400 MW; coal 30,127 MW; co-generation/waste-heat 2,700 MW
- Of 13 LNG projects (22,400 MW), only 3 projects/2,824 MW are under construction (Nhơn Trạch 3,4, Hiệp Phước) on schedule
- Vietnam will not build new coal plants after 2030; no new gas plants after 2035
- Amended PDP8 (Decision 768/QĐ-TTg) approved April 15, 2025
- Nhơn Trạch 3 and 4 (PV Power's first LNG plants, 1.6 GW combined) have entered commercial operation — Vietnam's first LNG-fired generation

### Specific Plants from PDP8 Implementation Plan
Domestic gas projects include: Ô Môn I (660 MW, converting to Block B gas); Ô Môn II (1,050 MW); Ô Môn III (1,050 MW); Ô Môn IV (1,050 MW); Dung Quất I (750 MW); Dung Quất II (750 MW); Dung Quất III (750 MW); plus Miền Trung I and II

### BOT Coal Plants (confirmed)
