## Publication rulings

- **The preprint is `slides/manuscript/main.tex`** (2026-06-05, then
  converted from `main.md` to hand-curated LaTeX in c911bf0c, ticket 0524).
  `report/report.tex` is an internal working document "with no future": keep
  it coherent with the shared generated-artifact DAG, spend editorial effort
  on the preprint.
- **Exp 2 sections owed to the report** (tickets 0255–0262) are deferred,
  never closed silently: the pre-registered hypotheses are commitments, and
  dropping them would be selective reporting.
- **"Article" means peer-reviewed** (2026-06-16). A working paper, preprint
  or report is never called an article, in English or French, anywhere
  outward-facing.
- **Code and data need their own persistent identifier.** A release sequence
  is not closed until code+data carry a DOI; cite the Zenodo *concept* DOI
  10.5281/zenodo.20715179, not a version DOI (open reminder: ticket 0678,
  blocked by the arXiv push 0665).
- **H1–H4 and the OSF preregistration** were set aside for the 2026-05-27
  conference and return with the journal paper; `docs/hypotheses.md` stays
  their source. Do not reshape tickets to fit H1–H4 sweeps.
- **H3 is empirical**: write "does not simultaneously achieve", never
  "cannot" or "is incompatible with".
- **Numbering**: Experiments 1–3; ticket 0200 is the *case study*, not
  "Exp 4".

## Process rulings

- **Merge, review, merge**: land each PR once approved and green rather than
  batching long-lived branches (2026-06-11).
- **Handoffs live in STATE.md, MASTERPLAN.md and `Blocked-by:` edges**, never
  in a tracker ticket (0545 rejected, 2026-06-11).
- **A reminder for a future event is an open ticket `Blocked-by` the
  triggering ticket**; a note in a closed or `deferred` ticket never
  resurfaces in `erg ready`.
- **A human sign-off criterion is not waived** by STATE.md's standing merge
  authorization: prepare everything and ask.
- **Supervised runs need no standing guards**: a guard is justified on
  unattended batch paths (workers, raids), not on callsites a human watches
  (0195 closed wontfix).
- **Review depth scales with diff risk**: a mechanical substitution sweep
  takes a light review, not the full panel (author remark on PR #1017).
- **READMEs of experiment output folders are written by hand**, not
  generated by a script.
- **Minimal solutions**: no over-engineering.

## Working with the author

- "Braindump" or "Imagine mode" opens a design dialogue: reflect, name the
  load-bearing ideas, ask sharpening questions; write nothing to docs,
  tickets or memory until the author ratifies.
- Challenge a proposed structure or figure placement instead of agreeing at
  once; a short multi-agent debate is welcome on non-obvious design calls.
- Mirror the author's modality: "typically" stays "typically". Domain
  boundaries are working patterns applied with the compiler's judgment, not
  mechanical tests.
- A question asked mid-edit is answered in the conversation; it is not text
  for the manuscript. Avoid stilted verbs ("accord with") in the author's
  voice.
- Before writing a plot with a derived Y metric, ask whether it should be an
  absolute count or a ratio.
